STAFF REPORT

To:              Town Council
Title:           2026 MS-535 Financial Report of the Budget
Meeting:         Town Council - 09 Sep 2026
Department:      Finance
Staff Contact:   Christine Tewksbury, Finance Director

BACKGROUND INFORMATION:
The Town is required to complete the State form MS-535 Financial Report of the Budget prior to
setting the tax rate. This report is prepared following the NH Department of Revenue Administration
Rev 1700 Rules for Financial Accounting for Cities and Towns.

The Finance Director, using the unaudited trial balance for the period ending June 30, 2026,
completed the form and believes it to be in accordance with the NH DRA Rev 1700 Rules.

Attached is a reconciliation of MS-535 to the Budget Status Report FY 2025-26 and the Revenues to
Offset Taxes FY 2025-26, which are included in the Town's annual report.


FINANCIAL IMPACT:
On the bottom of page 8 on MS-535 is the Unassigned Fund Balance for June 30, 2026, which is
$7,304,983. This is an increase of $1,694,972 from the prior year.

RECOMMENDATION:
Motion to authorize the Town Council to sign the 2026 MS-535 Financial Report of the Budget for the
period ending June 30, 2026 as presented.

SUGGESTED MOTION:
Motion to authorize the Town Council to sign the 2026 MS-535 Financial Report of the Budget for the
period ending June 30, 2026, as presented.

TOWN ADMINISTRATOR'S RECOMMENDATION:
I agree with the suggested motion.
