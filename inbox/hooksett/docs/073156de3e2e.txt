Title: Town Council Special Meeting Minutes 2/01/2025

Town of Hooksett
    Town Council Special Meeting Minutes (after Town Deliberative Session)
                          Saturday, February 1, 2025

The Hooksett Town Council met on Saturday, February 3, 2024, at 2:05pm in the Cawley Middle
School, 89 Whitehall Road, Hooksett, NH

CALL TO ORDER
Chair Tsantoulis called the Special Meeting to order at 2:05pm

PROOF OF POSTING
Executive Assistant, Geralyn Ciardelli provided proof of posting.

ROLL CALL
Present: Chair Tim Tsantoulis, Vice-Chair James Sullivan, Councilor Randall Lapierre, Councilor
Alex Walczyk, Councilor David Boutin, Councilor Roger Duhaime, Councilor Vincent Lembo Jr
and Councilor Jodi Pinard.
Absent: Councilor Keith Judge.

OLD BUSINESS
Warrant amendment from the 2/1/2025 Deliberative Session, on Article 17: Capital Reserve
Funding – Geographic Information System (GIS) Update. Resident Ray Miclette motioned to
amend Article 17 to reduce the Article to $37,500. Seconded by resident Alyssa Ehl. The motion
carried with 40 YES and 40 NO; Moderator voted YES to break the tie.

Councilor Lapierre motioned to recommend the amended Warrant Article #17 with a revised
value of $37,500. Seconded by Councilor Nyhan. The motion passed 8 AYE to 0 NAY.

Roll Call Vote
R. Duhaime – Aye
R. Lapierre – Aye
A. Walczyk – Aye
T. Tsantoulis – Aye
J Boutin - Aye
J. Sullivan – Aye
J. Pinard – Aye
V Lembo Jr – Aye
Voted FOR (8-0)

ADJOURNMENT
Councilor Lapierre motioned to adjourn at 2:06 pm. Councilor Nyhan seconded the motion.
Voted unanimously in favor (8-0 – with a voice vote)

Respectfully submitted,
Geralyn Ciardelli
