        2. No person may address the council more than twice on any issue in any

           reserves the right to end questioning if the questions depart from clarification to
           deliberation.

           subsequent meeting.

BACKGROUND INFORMATION:
  HealthTrust serves as the benefits advisor for the Town's Anthem medical and Delta Dental
  insurance programs. HealthTrust representative Debie Clayton is scheduled to attend the Town
  Council meeting on November 4, 2026, to provide an overview of the CY2027 renewal and answer
  questions regarding the proposed rates.

  CY2027 Health Insurance Renewal Rates
  The medical insurance renewal reflects an overall 2.0% decrease. Based on current enrollment
  levels and excluding vacant positions, this reduction is projected to result in annual savings of
  approximately $37,565.00.
    Access Blue (AB201PDED): 5.0% decrease
    Anthem Blue Site of Service (HRAABSOS20/40): 1.1% decrease
    Lumenos (High-Deductible Health Plan): 5.6% decrease
    Open Access (Retiree Plan, Under Age 65): 5.9% decrease

  two-person coverage, and $10,000 for family coverage. As of October 22, 2026, 17 employees
  participate in the Town's health insurance opt-out program.

   *Medical Insurance Rate History (2015-2027) is attached for Council's reference.

   How Your Rates Are Determined

HealthTrust, a nonprofit public risk pool exclusively serving New Hampshire, has been providing benefits
and services to public sector employers for more than 40 years. As the industry changes and evolves,
HealthTrust continues to outperform benchmarks based on a variety of performance indicators, delivering
robust benefits and providing more value than its competitors and the private insurance market.

Nonprofit: Created by The Public Sector for The Public Sector
What it means for Member Groups: HealthTrust knows New Hampshire’s Public Sector.
As a nonprofit risk pool, we are governed by a Board of Directors elected from the employees and
leadership of the Member Groups we serve. The decisions by the Board are rooted in the needs of public
sector employers and employees. HealthTrust does not receive or pay commissions and any excess funds
generated from rates are returned to Member Groups.

Non-Assessable Coverage
What it means for Member Groups: HealthTrust does not issue assessments.
HealthTrust’s non-assessable coverage means your contributions (premiums) are the limit of your
exposure and we will not require you to pay additional assessments. This protects you, and your taxpayers,
from unexpected mid-year bills and year-end assessments. If claims exceed contributions, HealthTrust
is responsible for any losses that may occur and will replenish through prospective rate adjustments in
future renewals.

Rating Process and Pooling Mechanisms
What it means for Member Groups: Your contribution covers expected costs.
HealthTrust uses data-driven, transparent, actuarially determined processes to set the rates needed to
cover claims, reserves, and administration. After public hearings, the HealthTrust board sets the final rates,
which it then communicates to Member Groups.

HealthTrust reduces volatility and enhances rating stability by utilizing key pooling mechanisms, which
include using the number of enrolled individuals to determine a group’s credibility weighting and pooling
the impact of high-cost claims. By blending a group’s unique claims experience with the broader shared pool,
this approach protects groups from sudden budget spikes caused by unexpected and high-dollar claims.

Plan-Specific Pricing
What it means for Member Groups: Pricing is specific to your Group’s plan selection.
HealthTrust is in the third and final year of a board approved actuarial plan pricing adjustment (known as
plan relativities). These relativity changes mean that the medical and prescription products you offer each
have their own rate change in order to achieve the actuarially-determined pricing of the plans relative
to each other. Within a rating entity, both the dollar value of contributions required to be raised and the
overall rate change for the rating entity remains the same as it would if relativities were not adjusted,
presuming no movement in enrollment. If you are part of a combined rating entity, or the 50 and under

Capital Risk Charge
What it means for Member Groups: HealthTrust builds sufficient reserves to pay
unexpected future claims.
The rates you pay cover projected claims and expenses. However, at times, claims can exceed projections.
In this case, the capital adequacy reserve covers unexpected future claims and expenses. This proved
effective when claims spiked in FY2022 and FY2023 far above rating expectations, with the capital
adequacy reserve covering all claims and expenses with no assessments being issued. HealthTrust has
been successfully rebuilding these reserves by adding a capital risk charge to rates in order to protect
groups against any future spikes. Our rebuild plan that started in January 2024 has proven effective in a
challenging market and will protect Member Groups against future volatility.

Contribution Components
What it means for Member Groups: You know how your contributions are allocated.
As a nonprofit, there are no shareholder or investor dividends, and we do not earn or pay commissions.
Your rates pay for your healthcare: The majority of your contributions (98%) go toward payment of claims,
claims reserves, and excess insurance components. The remaining 2% covers fees, operations, and well-
being programs.

      Built for New Hampshire’s Public Sector

HealthTrust is a nonprofit risk pool that has exclusively supported New Hampshire towns, cities, schools,
counties, and other municipal organizations for more than 40 years. Built by the public sector, for the
public sector, we deliver stability, transparency, and comprehensive benefits.
Unmatched Stability
With decades of experience, actuarially sound standards, and a consistent focus on long-term solvency,
our model works.
  • No budget surprises. Our non-assessable, fixed-cost model protects budgets year after year by
      never issuing assessments.
  • Claims covered, payment you can trust. Our reserves ensure reliable coverage for unexpected
      medical claims.
  • Financial solvency. Strong reserves secure long-term stability and peace of mind for our members.
The Power of the Pool
We are a community of communities, stronger together.
 • Leveraging scale. As New Hampshire’s largest public risk pool, we negotiate competitive rates,
     lower administrative fees, and deliver innovative coverage, programs and services.
 • Ensuring access. We remain committed to quoting all eligible New Hampshire public entities,
     ensuring all local communities have dependable options.
Uncompromised Coverage
  •    Robust plans. Flexible, high-value plans that fit your budget and deliver strong coverage without
       sacrificing choice.
  •    Expansive network coverage. Protection where you need it, when you need it — spanning New
       England and nationwide, including 99% in-state coverage.

Each year incidents to the Town of Hooksett employees cause untold suffering, loss of
productivity, low morale, and inefficient use of thousands of dollars. Investigations have
revealed that many of these incidents could have been prevented if the injured employee,
fellow workers, or supervisor had used greater caution and expended a little extra effort
in safely completing the task at hand. Many incidents have resulted simply because an
employee or a supervisor failed to meet their responsibility for ensuring that safe work
practices were continuously followed.

Every employee of the Town of Hooksett has the right to a workplace free from safety
and health hazards (Lab 1403.01). A "Joint Loss Management" program is designed to
prevent incidents and illnesses, and is established jointly between the employees and the
management of the Town of Hooksett. This program provides the framework and
structure for safety concerns to be managed like any other function of government through
planning, organization, leadership, control and communication. It is an established fact
that a well-trained, well-disciplined and well-supervised employee operating in a safe and
healthful environment is less likely to have an incident.

This manual has been prepared in order to provide all Town of Hooksett personnel with a
comprehensive set of written safety policies and procedures. Additional safety materials
specific to individual departmental operations may be provided from time to time.

These policies and procedures have been developed, and are expected to be followed in
an effort to minimize incidents in all departments and agencies. The material in this manual
will be of no benefit unless it is periodically reviewed and used as intended. Every employee,
supervisor and manager shall be expected to be thoroughly familiar with the contents of
this manual and shall be held responsible for compliance with the directives contained
herein.
