148          implementation costs, positions warranting further discussion, and proposed

151   Carol Granfield, Director of Human Resource: Stated they created a plan for the Town

277          14.2. Update to Ambulance Service Billing & Collection Policy
278
279   Joseph Stalker, Captain (Fire and Rescue): As a municipality-run ambulance service, the
280   Hooksett Fire Rescue Department has a policy outlining the standardization of billing and
281   collection procedures. In this policy is a process for individuals who have utilized the
282   ambulance service to apply for financial hardship. As it is currently written, requests for all
283   amounts must go before the Town Council for approval in non-public session. The Fire
284   Rescue Department is seeking to modify this policy to allow the Fire Rescue Department -
285   Administration to have the authority to grant these approvals for amounts up to $500.00.
286   Any amount surpassing $500.00 will continue to be forwarded to the town council for
287   approval. We feel this change would be the best use of time for the town administration,
288   town council and Fire Rescue Department.
289
290   R. Lapierre motioned to accept the policy revisions to the Ambulance Service Billing &
291   Collection Policy as amended plus revisions to the paramedic intercept billing
292   paragraph as discussed; seconded by A. Walczyk
293   Motion Passed (5-1)
294
295   A. Walczyk: Asked if there is a number to show how many of these requests would be
296   handled by the Fire Department with the $500 cap per year
297
298   Joseph Stalker, Captain (Fire and Rescue): Noted they may see 3-5 of these requests a year
299   at this amount.
300
301   R. Lapierre: Asked why the amount of $500 was chosen for the cap
302
303   J. Stalker: Noted that in his 10 years of doing this, the smaller amounts for these requests
304   tend to range anywhere between 150 to 450. Therefore, they felt that $500 would be a
305   good cap and beyond that would come to the Council for approval
306
307   L. Laliberte: Asked for clarification on the difference between the Paramedic Intercept
308   Billing vs the hardship requests
309
310   J. Stalker: Noted when the Hooksett Fire Rescue Department (HFRD) performs a paramedic
311   intercept with an outside agency, the HFRD sends an invoice to the requesting
312   department/service for the amount of $516.38. Stated this is an outdated number and they
313   are requesting this be increased to $549.00.
314
315   J. Stalker: Explained that a paramedic intercept is when an outside agency is the
316   transporting ambulance and they are bringing someone to the hospital and they have a
317   basic level provider like an EMT or an AMT and they have a very sick or injured individual
318   that requires an advanced level of care, they may require a paramedic. The Hooksett Fire
      8 | Town Council Meeting Minutes September 9, 2026


319   Rescue department has paramedics and offer paramedic services. The transporting
320   ambulance will dispatch requests for a paramedic and the paramedic will hop on their
321   ambulance, provide that paramedical care and continue on to the hospital with the patient.
322   Stated when that occurs, the ambulance that's transporting the patient sends a traditional
323   bill to the patient and the HFRD as the paramedic level service provider then bills the
324   ambulance service for what's called a paramedic intercept fee
325
326   R. Lapierre: Asked how often this number changes and recommended changing the
327   wording within the Paramedic Intercept Billing section to “The amount specified in the
328   agreement”
329
330   R. Lapierre motioned to amend the motion on the table to state, “...to accept the policy
331   as submitted plus revisions to the paramedic intercept billing paragraph as
332   discussed”; seconded by A. Walczyk
333   Motioned Passed (5-1)
334
335   R. Duhaime: Stated he would like to review all fire department rules and policies before any
336   new policies are added. Feels they have too much control at the moment
337
338   A. Walczyk: Noted this is not a blanket statement that all amounts under this threshold will
339   be forgiven. Noted there will still be a hardship review completed by the fire department as
340   it always has
341
342   J. Sullivan: Asked that any of these financial hardship applications that are made and

345   R. Duhaime: Noted that when the Town Council made these decisions, they could see what

350   J. Stalker: Referenced HIPPA and noted before promising any information being sent,
