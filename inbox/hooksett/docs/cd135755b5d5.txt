105          6.2 Library
106
107   FY 2028 Town Administrator's recommended budget of $1,233,077 is $70,302 or 6.05%
108   more than FY 2027
109
110   Heather Rainier, Library Director: Provided statistics on staffing as a percentage of the
111   library operating budget and a comparison with libraries with comparable per capita
112   municipal budgets. Noted that Hooksett’s staffing is 77.2% of our operating budget which
113   ranked 6th out of the 10 libraries on the comparison. Noted that restoring as much of the
114   budget that that had to be cut due to contractual increases over the last few years in our
115   default budgets, while also trying to balance that with not having a budget increasing too
116   dramatically was the focus on creating the upcoming proposed budget. Noted looking to
117   slightly increase hours at the library, the blood type support from our friends at the library,
118   but as we are moving forward, we are looking to reinstate the materials line. Noted that
119   the library staff continue to provide outstanding programs which continue to be in high
120   demand. In fiscal year 2026, the staff provided 360 youth programs with 10,761 children
121   and families attending and hosted 173 programs for adults with 4,758 attendees. Noted
122   regarding utilities, they are actively monitoring and adjusting the schedules for heating and
123   cooling throughout the year in addition to energy efficiency improvements for lights and
124   water have allowed us to maintain this line with a small increase. Highlighted the efforts to
125   monitor this line item and do everything to keep this as low as they can and they have
126   shown savings due to their efforts
