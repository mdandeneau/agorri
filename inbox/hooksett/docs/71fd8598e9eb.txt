unaccepted portions of University Circle (2700’), Crawford Lane (500’) and Alumni Drive (300’) as

responsibility of the Town of Hooksett.

The roadways of University Circle, Alumni Drive and part of Crawford Lane has now been completed
in accordance with Hooksett's roadway standards and is being acceptance in accordance with the
Town of Hooksett's Roadway acceptance procedure.

RECOMMENDATION:
Accept these portions of University Circle and Crawford Lane, and all of Alumni Drive as a public
roadways.
