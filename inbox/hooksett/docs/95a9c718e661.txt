195          Tax Increment District (TIF) Sewer Pipe -Merrimack River Crossing
196
197   A. Garron: Noted the Tax Increment Finance Committee are present to discuss a project that was
198   voted on to move forward. Noted the way the tax Increment Finance District works is that the
199   Finance Committee is the advisory committee of the council with regard to administering the plan
200   of the tax Increment Finance district. Noted he is the administrator to carry forth the wishes of the
201   council. Stated he is here to present a project which will be to construct an element of the full plan
202   which will bring the sewer from the Martin’s Ferry pump station underneath the Merrimack River
203   to the West Bank of Hooksett and the Exit 10 area. Stated the Tax Increment Finance (TIF) District
204   Committee recommends moving forward with the Sewer Pipe River Crossing Project. The
205   Hooksett Sewer Commission has initiated work on the sewer force main project, which is
206   scheduled for completion in 2027. In addition, the Commission has committed funding toward the
207   Martins Ferry Pump Station upgrade. The next phase of the overall sewer infrastructure program,
208   and a critical component in providing sew. Noted the project will begin at the Martins Ferry Pump
209   Station, extend approximately 110 feet/per pipe beneath the Merrimack River, and terminate at
210   the future Kimball Drive Pump Station site. Upon installation, the pipes will be capped and remain
211   inactive until funding is secured for construction of the Kimball Drive Pump Station. Noted the TIF
212   District Committee and the Hooksett Sewer Commission believe this project will serve as a
213   catalyst for future development within the Exit 10 area by providing critical wastewater
214   infrastructure. The availability of this infrastructure is expected to encourage businesses and
215   property owners to invest in their portion of the sewer system, advancing economic development
216   and supporting long-term growth in the district. Noted the Tax Increment Finance (TIF)
217   Committee recommends that Town Council approve the expenditure of TIF funds, in the amount
218   of $1million, to install double barrel sewer lines from Martins Ferry Pump station to the west side
219   of the Merrimack River to the proposed location of the Kimball Drive Pump Station.

685   Administrator's Job Description. The Assistant Town Administration position will be effective

696
697   A. Garron: Presented the proposed reorganization plans, in accordance with Section 10.12 of the
698   Town Charter, for consideration by the Town Council. Noted The Administration Department
699   currently consists of the following positions: Executive Assistant, Project Coordinator, and Human
700   Resources Coordinator. Noted The Town Administrator currently serves in a dual capacity as both
701   the chief administrative officer of the Town and the Director of Economic and Community
702   Development. While this structure has allowed the Town to address community and economic
703   development needs, the continued growth and complexity of these responsibilities have
704   demonstrated a need for dedicated leadership within the Community Development Department.
705   Additionally, the Town is undertaking a transition in leadership due to the impending retirement
706   of the current Town Administrator. Recruiting and retaining a future Town Administrator with
707   responsibility for both general administration and community and economic development
708   functions may prove challenging and may not provide either area with the level of attention
709   necessary to achieve the Town's long-term goals. Furthermore, the Town Council identified as a
710   2022 goal the need to evaluate the creation of a Community Development Director/Department
711   Head position. This proposal is intended to address that goal while establishing a sustainable
712   organizational structure for the future. Proposed Reorganization, Town Administrator proposes:
713   1. Reclassifying and elevating the current Project Coordinator position to Assistant Town
714   Administrator.
715   2. Assigning the Assistant Town Administrator to serve as Department Head of the Community
716   Development Department and as the Town's Economic and Community Development Director.
      16 | Town Council Meeting Minutes October 14, 2026

717   3. Transferring responsibility for the day-to-day management and leadership of community and
718   economic development functions from the Town Administrator to the new Assistant Town
719   Administrator position.
720   The Assistant Town Administrator will continue to support the Town Administrator in
721   organizational management while providing dedicated oversight and leadership of community
722   and economic development initiatives. A proposed job description outlining the duties,
723   responsibilities, and qualifications of the position accompanied this proposal. The salary
724   adjustments reflect the increased managerial, supervisory, and departmental leadership
725   responsibilities associated with the Assistant Town Administrator and Community Development
726   Director position.
727
