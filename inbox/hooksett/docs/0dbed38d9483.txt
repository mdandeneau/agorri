86   Cindy Robertson, 33 Autumn Run, former Chair of the Conservation Commission for over 20 years:
87   In favor of the Quimby Mountain Stewardship Plan. Identified the 5 most important pieces of
88   property that needed to be identified and this was one of them. (Also included were Clay Pond, The
89   River Walk Trail and Heads Pond and the Pinnacle). We worked really hard and we purchased this
90   property for that purpose and if we didn’t this land would be a development and homes and cars
91   and sidewalks and pavement on that almost 100 acres. And there isn’t; it is protected and beautiful
92   and open to everyone and the residents. John Turbine served on the Council for a long time. John
93   handled abutters concerns very well. Pinnacle works fine, there are no issues, no abutter issues. I
94   support this and was a large part of getting this property purchased for the town and it is important
95   that it be managed properly. Everyone needs an opportunity to enjoy this property.
