308          David Cote, 26 Benton Road, Snow Storage Drainage Grievance
309
310   A. Garron: Noted that on August 26, 2026, David Cote, under public input shared a prepared
311   grievance with Town Council regarding the snow storage on the abutting Merchant Auto's site.
312   He has shared this grievance with Town staff starting in January 2019. Merchant Motor received
313   approval for their current site in 1997 and amended plan, which added a garage and showroom, in
314   1999. In 2000, Merchant Motors amended their site again. In 2008, a drainage swale plan was
315   submitted, no approval is shown on the plan. On those plans, a snow storage area was identified
316   on their plan. The site has since been constructed in accordance with the approved plan and the
317   snow storage area is provided in the area approved by the Town of Hooksett Planning Board. Mr.
318   Cote mentioned at Town Council’s August 26, 2026, meeting, Public Input, in a prepared
319   statement, that he had contacted Bruce Thomas, former Town Engineer, about his water issue
320   with Merchants Auto in 2019. Bruce visited Mr. Cote’s land and the area of Merchants Auto site
321   where snow is stored. Mr. Thomas, based on his observation, suggested that they extend the swale
322   or berm at a length show on his sketch to address Mr. Cote’s concern. It is my understanding that
      8 | Town Council Meeting Minutes September 23, 2026

323   Merchant Auto extended the berm in accordance with Mr. Thomas’s suggestion. From 2019 to
324   2025, Mr. Cote had no documented contact with the Town about this matter. In accordance with
325   Mr. Cote, the suggested berm extension did not address his concerns about drainage onto his
326   property due to the snow storage. Dana Pendergast and myself have met with Mr. Cote on several
327   occasions about his concerns. We have investigated his concerns. Dana has stopped by the
328   Merchants Auto site on multiple occasions during the winter, to observe the location of the snow
329   mounds, and on occasion requested that they move the mounds back from the tree line, if portion
330   of the snow mounds exceeded the berm. Also, Dana, had Merchants move the snow storage
331   mounds back from the woods within Merchant’s site. Merchant Auto’s approved plans show
332   “Snow Storage” areas on their plan. There is one note on the May 1999 approved plan which
333   states: “In the event that the existing snow storage area provided on the site is completely utilized
334   excess snow shall be transported off site for disposal in accordance with NHDES regulations”. The
335   plan nor Planning Board approval sets limits on the maximum height of the snow storage, amount
336   or origin. The note on the “May 1999 plan” does imply that the snow brought to the area of the
337   site is confined to the designated area.
