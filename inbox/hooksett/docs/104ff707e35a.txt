STAFF REPORT

To:              Town Council
Title:           FY 2026-27 Budget and Warrant Articles
Meeting:         Town Council Special Meeting - 21 Jan 2026
Department:      Finance
Staff Contact:   Christine Tewksbury, Finance Director

BACKGROUND INFORMATION:
1) Operating Budget: (This information is subject to change at the Budget Committee's Public
Hearing on January 15, 2026)
The Town Council recommended a budget of $22,918,112.00 and the Sewer Commission
recommended a budget of $2,781,691.00 for a combined total of $25,699,803.00.

The Budget Committee reviewed each of the budgets and made no changes. The Budget
Committee's recommended operating budget for FY 2026-27 is $25,699,803.00.

2) Default Budget:
Approve and sign the FY 2026-27 Default Budget in the amount of $24,643,338.00. See Attached
State form 2026 MS-DTB and 2026-27 Default.

3) Warrants:
Determine which councilor will 1st and 2nd each of the warrant articles at the Deliberative Session on
Saturday January 31, 2026. See attached Warrant Article List.

FINANCIAL IMPACT:
The operating budget of $25,699,803.00 less anticipated revenues in the amount of $10,557,024.00
has a net effect on the tax rate of $4.72. The current town share of the tax rate is $4.28.

Fiscal impacts are on each of the warrant articles.

If all the articles were to pass in March, the estimated increase in the town share of the tax rate would
be $1.09.

SUGGESTED MOTION:
1) Motion to recommend the FY 2026-27 operating budget in the amount of $ 25,699,803.00 (tally
vote needed)
2) Motion to sign the 2026 MS-DTB (default budget) in the amount of $24,643,338.00.
3) Determine which councilor will 1st and which will 2nd each of the warrant articles at the
Deliberative Session on Saturday January 31, 2026.
