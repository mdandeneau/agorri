      PUBLIC INPUT
        1. Two 15-minute Public Input sessions will be allowed during each Council
           Meeting. Time will be divided equally among those wishing to speak, however,
           no person will be allowed to speak for more than 5 minutes.
        2. No person may address the council more than twice on any issue in any
           meeting. Comments must be addressed to the Chair and must not be personal
           or derogatory about any other person.
        3. Any questions must be directly related to the topic being discussed and must
           be addressed to the Chair only, who after consultation with Council and Town
           Administrator, will determine if the question can be answered at that time.
           Questions cannot be directed to an individual Councilor and must not be
           personal in nature. Issues raised during Public Input, which cannot be resolved
           or answered at that time, or which require additional discussion or research,
           will be noted by the Town Administrator who will be responsible for researching
           and responding to the comment directly during normal work hours or by
           bringing to the Council for discussion at a subsequent meeting. The Chair
           reserves the right to end questioning if the questions depart from clarification to
           deliberation.
        4. Council members may request a comment be added to New Business at a
           subsequent meeting.
        5. No one may speak during Public Input except the person acknowledged by the
           Chair. Direct questions or comments from the audience are not permitted
           during Public Input.

The Project Coordinator has concerns about providing 24/7 access to volunteers or elected officials,
including Town Councilors. The primary purpose of the security and access control project was to
secure Town Hall, enhance employee safety, and limit access to authorized personnel based on
operational need.

Town Councilors do not have regular operational duties in the building outside of scheduled meetings
and official functions. Granting unrestricted access would increase the number of non-employees
able to enter staff-only areas, which could increase the risk of unauthorized access to sensitive
records and employee workspaces.

Limiting access to scheduled meeting times and approved functions helps preserve the intent of the
security system, ensures accountability, and maintains a clear distinction between governance and
daily operations.

RECOMMENDATION:
The Project Coordinator's recommendation is to leave Councilor access as limited rather than
unrestricted.

TOWN ADMINISTRATOR'S RECOMMENDATION:
Town Council had voted on this matter on September 10, 2025, and concluded that key fobs will be
provided to the Councilors that request them but are limited to times of meeting at Town Hall.

Title:         Veterans Service - Connected Total Disability Tax Credit
Meeting:       Town Council - 25 Feb 2026
Department:
Staff Contact:

BACKGROUND INFORMATION:
During the 2025 Legislature session the NH General Court passed HB House Bill 99 amending RSA 72:35 which
included:
     1. Raising the amount which may be adopted by the communities from $4,000. To $5,000. for the Totally &
        Permanently Disabled Veterans’ Tax Credit (Currently the Town of Hooksett’s amount is $2800.)
     2. Changing the language of statute to emphasize the credit is for those who
      Are permanent and 100% Disabled Service connected.
     3. Prohibiting the combining of Optional Veterans’ Credit ($420.) with the Totally & Permanently Disabled Veterans’
        tax credit.
 By adjusting the Totally & Permanently Disabled Veterans’ Tax Credit to include the value of the Regular Veteran’s Tax
 Credit we would be keeping the tax credit level for most of the current recipients.
 The Board of Assessors have voted to approve this.


FINANCIAL IMPACT:
$2100

POLICY IMPLICATIONS:
We currently have 59 Veterans receiving the Veterans’ Totally and Permanently Disabled Service-Connected Tax Credit
of $2800. Only 5 out of the 59 Veterans do not receive the regular Veterans Tax Credit. The other 54 Veterans receive
both Tax Credits ($420+$2800) totaling $3220.


RECOMMENDATION:
 A motion to increase the amount of Veterans’ Totally & Permanently Disabled Service-Connected Tax Credit from $2800
to $3220 as recommended by the Board od Assessors.


SUGGESTED MOTION:
Motion to increase the amount of Veterans’ Totally & Permanently Disabled Service-Connected Tax
Credit from $2800 to $3220.

TOWN ADMINISTRATOR'S RECOMMENDATION:
I agree with the suggested motion.

ATTACHMENTS:
HB 99
