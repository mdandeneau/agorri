        1. Two 15-minute Public Input sessions will be allowed during each Council
           Meeting. Time will be divided equally among those wishing to speak, however,
           no person will be allowed to speak for more than 5 minutes.
        2. No person may address the council more than twice on any issue in any
           meeting. Comments must be addressed to the Chair and must not be personal
           or derogatory about any other person.
        3. Any questions must be directly related to the topic being discussed and must
           be addressed to the Chair only, who after consultation with Council and Town
           Administrator, will determine if the question can be answered at that time.
           Questions cannot be directed to an individual Councilor and must not be
           personal in nature. Issues raised during Public Input, which cannot be resolved

To:            Town Council
Title:         ARPA Funding Request - Andy Janosz on Behalf of Parks & Recreation Advisory
               Board - Request for 2-Bay Swing Set at Lambert’s Park
Meeting:       Town Council - 22 May 2024
Department:    Public Works
Staff Contact: Ben Berthiaume, Public Works Director

BACKGROUND INFORMATION:
The Parks & Recreation Advisory Board is recommending the construction and installation of a swing
set at Lambert’s Park. Total estimated cost is around $10,000. This estimate includes a swing set
with 2 belt seats, 1 ADA swing seat, 1 bucket seat, arch post, bark mulch and base, ADA compliant
flooring system, border timbers and hardware and concrete bags for footings.

FINANCIAL IMPACT:
$10,000 to be paid out of ARPA funds.

POLICY IMPLICATIONS:
None.

RECOMMENDATION:
Approve and consent the construction and installation of a 2-bay swing set at Lambert’s Park for a
total amount not to exceed $10,000 using ARPA funds.

SUGGESTED MOTION:
Motion to approve and consent the construction and installation of a 2-bay swing set at Lambert’s
Park for a total amount not to exceed $10,000 using ARPA funds.

TOWN ADMINISTRATOR'S RECOMMENDATION:
The ARPA Committee recommended that this project be presented to Town Council for consideration
for ARPA funding.

Pantry project is requesting using the money already allocated to this project. A few of the items that

the funds.

and flooding conditions at each location.

$125,000. Currently there is $168.049.12 balance in the account. This balance will be increased to
$268,049.12 on July 1, 2024. A full summary of the Drainage Upgrades account is attached.

STAFF REPORT

To:              Town Council
Title:           ARPA Funding Request - Town Building Telephone Upgrades
Meeting:         Town Council - 22 May 2024
Department:      Administration
Staff Contact:   Leann McLaughlin, Project Coordinator

BACKGROUND INFORMATION:
As a crucial component of the security enhancements initiative, we are addressing the need for
upgrading the telephone infrastructure to incorporate a vital paging system and allow for a cohesive
environment with the outside departments. Regrettably, during our pursuit of funding through the
Homeland Security Grant, we discovered that communication equipment did not fall within the eligible
categories for inclusion.

We are seeking approval from the Town Council to allocate funds, not exceeding $42,500.00 (cost
includes the Town Offices, Safety Center, Public Works Department and Library), for the installation
of new telephones along with the necessary accompanying equipment for all departments. This
includes the provision of additional speakers and acquisition of a software license to ensure the
seamless integration of a paging system in the Town Offices. The attached quote does not include
the Library but after further investigation, a quote of $2,500 was obtained for incorporate the Library.

By investing in these upgrades, we aim to bolster communication capabilities across all town entities,
enabling us to efficiently disseminate crucial information during emergencies or security incidents.
Your support in approving this allocation is crucial as we endeavor to strengthen the safety and
resilience of tour community.

The amount requested is based off a quote received from our current provider. Due to the cost
of the project, this will have to go out to bid.

RECOMMENDATION:
Approve use of ARPA funds for the telephone system upgrade.

SUGGESTED MOTION:
Motion to allocate $42,500.00 for the purpose of upgrading the telephone system as well as
integrating all departments within the same IP phone system.

TOWN ADMINISTRATOR'S RECOMMENDATION:
The ARPA Committee recommended this project be presented to Town Council for consideration for
ARPA funding.

139   11.5 Ken Conaty on behalf of the Sewer Commission Request for Sewer Easements on Depot Road
140
141   K. Conaty- The current force main from Martin’s Ferry pump station runs in the railroad right of way on
142   the eastern side of the tracks. It was constructed in 1968 of single 8500’ 6” cast iron pipe. Over the
143   years corrosion has occurred both inside and outside the pipe. The internal corrosion is causing
144   significant flow losses due to friction. Another issue is the location of the pipe near the Merrimack
145   River. Any break in the pipe would result in raw wastewater being spilled into the river. The issues with
146   the pipe and its location were brought to light when we started doing hydraulic calculations for the
147   upgrade at Martin’s Ferry pump station to accept wastewater flow from the southern area of the TIF
148   district around Exit 10.
149
150   It was determined that the best solution to these problems would be to replace the force main. We
151   looked at replacing it in the same location along the railroad but that may not serve as a long-term
152   solution if commuter rail were established, and a second rail would be need as the distance to the new
153   line would be minimal and access not easy. The new force main would be directionally drilled from the
154   pump station approximately 4500’ feet to a spot where gravity sewer could resume. The pipe will be 8”
155   HDPE which has a very low friction loss resulting in much higher pumping volumes to allow the
156   expansion of the TIF district. Relocating the line as indicated through the western edge of the former
157   Manchester Sand property is the best scenario for environmental and access reasons. This line would
158   cross the following lots: • 29 Lehoux Dr-Map 24 Lot 33- Maclellan • 25 Lehoux Dr-Map 24 Lot 32-Old
159   Castle • 21 Lehoux Dr-Map 24 Lot 36-Public Service (formerly Manchester Sand) • Off Depot Rd- Map
160   30 Lot 57-Town of Hooksett • 57 Martin’s Ferry Rd- Map 33 Lot 2- SNHU • 75 Martin’s Ferry Rd-Map 29
