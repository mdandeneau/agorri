TOWN OF RYE
                                     BEACH COMMITTEE
                                      DRAFT MINUTES
                                 Monday, October 5, 2026 – 5:30 p.m.
                                    Rye Town Hall & via Zoom


Present: Co-Chairs Coleen Penacho and Chuck Latvis, Doug Beattie, Joe Leverone,
Selectboard Rep Suzanne Barton, and Alternate Nicole Boucher


    I.        Call to Order

The meeting was called to order at 5:32 p.m.

    II.       Public Comment

    •      Peter Fregeau, 1196 Ocean Blvd, spoke to the Committee about the possibility of
           having signage at public access ways to the beach that indicate public/town beach areas
           versus private property.
           It was noted that this is an issue that will need to go before the Select Board. However,
           the Beach Committee can give recommendations to the Board on things that they support.
           After discussion, the Beach Committee agreed to take Mr. Fregeau’s comments into
           consideration and see if there’s an appropriate way to address the concerns.

    III.      Approval of Minutes – September 8th

Motion by Doug Beattie to approve the minutes of September 8, 2026, as presented.
Seconded by Joe Leverone. All in favor.

    IV.       2027 Budget

The Committee reviewed the proposed 2027 budget for the Beach Committee. The budget will
be presented to the Select Board and Budget Committee at budget work sessions later in October.

Motion by Coleen Penacho to accept the draft Beach Committee 2027 budget in the amount
of $1,440. Seconded by Joe Leverone. All in favor.

    V.        Shoulder Season Water Testing Assignments
              • October 7 & 21
              • November 4 & 18 – Chuck Latvis
              • December 2 & 16 – Chuck Latvis

    VI.    October 31st Beach Cleanup with Surfrider

Co-Chair Latvis is coordinating the Halloween beach cleanup with Surfrider. The cleanup will
take place at Jenness Beach on Saturday, October 31st from 9:00 to 11:00 a.m.

    VII.   Old Business
           • Bacterial warnings signs on beach

Select Board Rep Barton confirmed that the signs have been ordered and may have already been
received by the Town. The signs are put up by the Fire Department personnel (lifeguards) based
on information from the State.

           •   Fires on the beach in the sand

The Beach Committee discussed this at their last meeting. If there are any issues, people should
call the Police or Fire Department. Rules for fires are listed on the Fire Department’s fire permit
application.

           •   Parsons Creek Advisory update moved to November meeting

Building Inspector/Code Enforcement Officer Amber Harrison will be attending the November
2nd Beach Committee meeting to give an update on the Parsons Creek Advisory Committee.

    VIII. New Business
          • Mary-Ann Sullivan, past Beach Committee Chair, passed on an email from a
            resident about a wooden walkway along the beach on South Road that was
            destroyed during a winter storm a few years ago. The resident was wondering if
            the walkway could be restored.
            It was noted that the walkway will not be restored due to FEMA and safety
            regulations.

           •   The Committee discussed whether there should be a recommendation to the
               Select Board regarding the concerns addressed earlier in the meeting by Peter
               Fregeau.

Motion by Coleen Penacho to make a recommendation to the Select Board that they
consider educating the public regarding private property versus public beaches along the
Rye seacoast. Seconded by Doug Beattie. All in favor.

Next Meeting: November 2, 2026, 5:30 p.m., Rye Town Hall

Motion by Doug Beattie to adjourn at 6:25 p.m. Seconded by Joe Leverone. All in favor.
