Title: Minutes: Demolition Review Committee Agenda
Fetched: 2026-11-01

MINUTES

Demolition Review Committee
Meeting 10/09/2026

D. Choate called the meeting to order at 8:00 a.m. at the Rye Town Hall.
Present were: David Choate, Elizabeth Sanborn, Frank Lagana and Tom Sedoric (Alternate).
Also present was Michael Bryan, the applicant.

The Minutes from the 09/23/2026 and 10/02/2026 meetings were approved. E. Sanborn
moved, F. Lagana seconded, Unanimous approval.

Purpose of today’s meeting: follow up to site visit and application review for the demolition of
the barn at 51 Wentworth Road.

S. Bedard and M Bryan did discuss the plans for replacement. M. Bryan needs to raise it to fa-
cilitate putting in a workshop on the lower level which is prevented currently by the ledge. No
plans were available per Sandy from the Building Department. It was evident that some demoli-
tion has already been done. However, E. Sanborn moved and D. Choate seconded to issue the
permit with two conditions:

a. The DRC can photograph for historical purposes; and
b. The DRC requests a transom window be replaced over the barn entry in front.

Unanimous approval and T. Sedoric abstained as he is an abutter.

D. Choate will go to the Select Board re: the sequence of asbestos removal on the Demolition
Permit Application as we would like to have a preliminary meeting with the applicant BEFORE
walls, ceilings, etc. are ripped apart looking for asbestos..

We asked M. Bryan to validate that all permits (NHDES & Asbestos Reports) have been re-
ceived by the Building Department.

E. Sanborn moved to adjourn, T. Sedoric 2nd. Unanimous approval.

Meeting adjourned at 8:21 a.m.

Respectfully submitted,
Elizabeth Sanborn
