Title: Minutes: Beach Committee Agenda
Fetched: 2026-11-01

TOWN OF RYE
                               BEACH COMMITTEE
                             DRAFT MEETING MINUTES
                             Monday, September 7, 2026 – 5:30 p.m.
                                 Rye Town Hall & via Zoom


Present: Co-Chairs Coleen Penacho and Chuck Latvis, Joe Leverone, Doug Beattie, and
Selectboard Rep Suzanne Barton


    1) Call to Order

Co-Chair Penacho called the meeting to order at 5:32 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

    2) Public Comment
       None

    3) Review and approval of August 3, 2026 Minutes

Motion by Coleen Penacho to approve the minutes of August 3, 2026. Seconded by Chuck
Latvis. All in favor.

    4) 4th Grade Beach Sign Removal

It was noted by Co-Chair Penacho that the DPW reuses the wooden placards that the 4th grade
signs are posted to. The Beach Committee takes down the signs and brings the stands back to
the DPW Transfer Station to be stored for the next season. The Committee divided up the areas
to collect signs.

    5) September shoulder season water testing assignments

Co-Chair Latvis reported that the schedule has not yet been received; however, it will start at the
end of September. He has plenty of bottles available, which will cover this season. Once the
schedule is received, he will send it out to the Committee. The testing is done mostly at Pirates
Cove with an occasional testing at Jenness Beach.

    6) Flood 101

The Building Department will be giving a presentation about the flood regulations in Rye. The
event is being held on October 7th from 3:00 to 4:00 p.m. at the Rye Public Library. More
information can be found through the Town’s website or at the Building Department.

Building Inspector Amber Harrison will be meeting with the Beach Committee at the October
meeting to give an update on the Parsons Creek Advisory Committee.

    7) Old Business
       a. Bacterial warning signs on beach

Member Beattie asked who is responsible for maintaining the bacteria warning signs.

Co-Chair Latvis explained that the State notifies Rye’s Public Health, Fire and Police
Departments. The staff from the Fire Department put up the signs and DPW is responsible for
taking them down. Co-Chair Latvis commented that the signs look old and should probably be
replaced. He has mentioned this to the Town Administrator.

    8) New Business
       a. Fires on the beach in the sand

An email was received from a resident who saw someone having a fire on Foss Beach in the
sand. The resident was asking about the rules relating to fires on the beach.

It was noted by Member Beattie that the rules about having a fire are in the permit application.
Co-Chair Penacho will follow up with the resident who emailed. If someone sees that the rules
are not being followed, they should call the Town.

       b. Rocks removed from the beach

Selectwoman Barton commented that she was always under the impression that rocks cannot be
taken off the beach. Recently, a couple was taking rocks from the beach. She is not sure if this
is a town or state ordinance. The Beach Committee should probably do some research to see if
there is an ordinance or rules around this issue.

       c. Master Plan Implementation Committee

Selectwoman Barton noted that the update to the Town’s Master Plan has action steps that the
Master Plan Implementation Committee are addressing. She read through some of the action
items that relate to the beaches.

        d. Beach Cleanup

Surfrider is holding a beach cleanup on October 31st. The Beach Committee will be involved in
this cleanup and will discuss further details at the October meeting.

       Adjournment

Motion by Doug Beattie to adjourn at 5:58 p.m. Seconded by Coleen Penacho. All in favor.


                                                            Respectfully submitted, Dyana F Ledger
