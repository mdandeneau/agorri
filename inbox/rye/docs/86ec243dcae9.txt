Fire Department 4220-16 Chief Lambert
Chart 2 Fire & Safety Calls
See Fire Department Narrative. Not posted online.

Discussed the cultural change that makes it harder to attract and keep people who want to be firemen.
Staff continues to get paramedic certifications as more and more of the calls are for ambulance related
needs. Budget increases are due to staff to rely less on per-diem personal.
There was some discussion about having separate accounts for per-diem employees, a fund for when
officers are called in (anyone who responds whether they go on a call get a minimum payment) and staff
to cover when a regular shift fireman has planned absences.
The total calls show the spike in second calls (a call when a vehicle is already out on a call) from 2021.

Chief Lambert discussed how the town’s Insurance Services Office (ISO) rating impacts the cost of
insurance homeowners pay in Rye – the better the score, the lower the cost of policies.


Motioned by Doug Abrams to recommend the Fire Department Budget. Seconded by Elsie DiBella. All
in favor 9 to 0.
