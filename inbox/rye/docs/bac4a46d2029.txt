Title: Minutes: Demolition Review Committee Agenda
Fetched: 2026-11-01

MINUTES

Demolition Review Committee
Site Visit and Meeting 10/02/2026

D. Choate, E. Sanborn, F. Lagana, and T. Sedoric met at the barn site at 51 Wentworth Road at
2:15 pm. Steve Bedard of Bedard Preservation was only available via a FaceTime call. All
members walked the site and D. Choate was able to give S. Bedard a clear picture of the barn,
areas in need of repair, etc. S. Bedard felt the barn would be less expensive to renovate than
replace and that it was very feasible to do so. The DRC members returned to the Rye Town
Hall to discuss our findings and to vote.

D. Choate called the meeting to order at 2:55 p.m. at the Rye Town Hall.
Present were: David Choate, Elizabeth Sanborn, Frank Lagana, and Tom Sedoric (Alternate).

Purpose of today’s meeting: to continue the review of the application for demolition of the barn
at 51 Wentworth Road.

D. Choate suggested we extend our discussion because Mike Bryan, the owner, could not be in
attendance for the site visit and D. Choate thinks he remembered that Mr. Bryan had expressed
an interest in preserving the barn if it was financially feasible and less expensive than rebuilding.
He would like to ask him to speak with S. Bedard before we make a final decision.

Accordingly, E. Sanborn moved that we postpone a formal vote until S. Bedard and M. Bryan
can speak with a deadline of 10/16/2026. If M. Bryan doesn’t want to speak with S. Bedard,
then the DRC will meet to vote on the permit as soon as another meeting can be scheduled. F.
Lagana 2nd. T. Sedoric abstained as he is an abutter. The motion was approved unanimously
and D. Choate will speak with M. Bryan about the DRC request that he speak with S. Bedard..

Other business: D Choate reviewed some recent sales.

T. Sedoric moved to adjourn and F. Lagana 2nd. Unanimous approval.

Meeting adjourned at 3:19 p.m.

Respectfully submitted,
Elizabeth Sanborn
