Rye Energy Committee
                   Meeting Minutes - DRAFT
                        November 2, 2026, 3:30 PM
                              Rye Public Library

1. Approval of October Minutes - Minutes were reviewed
and approved unanimously

2. Rye Community Power Update - Renewable Energy
Credit revenue will be sent in the next couple of weeks. It
is expected to be about $600 and will be deposited into
the Rye Energy Revolving Fund to be used for energy
related expenses. Money expended from this account
muist be approved by the Select Board.

3. Renewable Energy Credit Reporting - The Energy
Committee has set up its independent generation
reporting with Energy Audits Unlimited. November 1 is
the first day to report generation to EAU. This reporting
confirms generation for the minting of Renewable Energy
Credits. Solar Edge monitors production for Rye solar
arrays and a report on the month’s and lifetime
generation is produced monthly.

4. RJS Consumption Meter Reporting - Tom showed the
committee the software that shows energy consumption
at the Rye Junior Hogh School. With the recently
purchased software upgrade, the software can report
consumption by the minute. This data will be shared with
the school and building manager to see if there are any
opportunities for more energy efficiency.

5. PSB Energy Efficiency Study and Grant - The PSB
used significantly more energy this year than last.
Eversource and the energy committee are working with a
third party vendor to do a free (paid for by Eversource)
energy audit to see where the increased demand is
coming from. We are awaiting Eversource to approve the
contract with the vendor.

6. EV Grant Planning - the energy committee is doing
outreach to businesses that are likely to qualify for the
Level 2 EV charging grants. EV charging can be installed
at locations that are open to the public, conducive to cars
charging for at least 90 minutes, and are maintained year
round. The grant will pay for up to 80% of the cost of
installation. The energy committee will write the grants.
Grants will be awarded each month until the fund is
expended. It is open to all towns and cities in NH.

7. NH EnergySaver Program - Dan Hackett of Revision
Energy updated the committee on the NH Energy Saver
Program. It is a new program that WILL HELP INCOME
ELIGIBLE HOMES WITH ENERGY EXPENSES. The
energy committee will be doing more outreach to the
community as we learn more about the details, The
program is not specific to any energy company. The first
step is an energy audit. The audit will make
recommendations about how a home can be more
efficient. There is money that can help homeowners meet
the expense of upgrades. THis si great opportunity that
people should be aware of!

8. New Business - none

9. Next Meeting - December 1 at 3:30 pm at Rye Public
Library
