DRAFT MINUTES of the HC Meeting 9/03/26


                                       TOWN OF RYE
                                   HERITAGE COMMISSION
                                Thursday, September 3, 2026 – 5:30 p.m.
                                      Rye Town Hall & via Zoom


Present: Chair Alex Herlihy, Vice-Chair Gail Hughes (arrived 5:36), Treasurer Peter White,
David Choate, Frank Lagana, and Alternate Elizabeth Sanborn


I.         CALL TO ORDER

Chair Herlihy called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.
II.        INTRODUCTIONS
           The Commission introduced themselves.

III.       APPROVE MINUTES

Motion by Peter White to approve the June 4, 2026, meeting minutes as presented. Seconded by
Frank Lagana. All in favor.

       •   August 6th
           It should be noted that Member David Choate was not at the August 6th meeting. He should not
           be listed as present.

Motion by Peter White to approve the August 6, 2026, meeting minutes as amended. Seconded by
Frank Lagana. All in favor.

IV.        BUSINESS

       •   HOUSE PLAQUES

It was noted that the Heritage Commission is in charge of the house plaques. Residents can find
information on the Heritage Commission webpage on the Town’s website. Checks for the plaques can be
made out to the Rye Heritage Commission and may be sent to the Town Hall or dropped off in person.
The plaques will be ordered by the Heritage Commission once the check is received.

       •   N.H. PRESERVATION ALLIANCE

Chair Herlihy asked the Commission if they should become a member of the N.H. Preservation Alliance
(NHPA). NHPA sends a monthly newsletter to their members. It was agreed that it would beneficial for
the Commission to become a member.

    •   TREASURERS REPORT – Peter White

Treasurer White reported that the current cash balance is $28,768. The biggest expenditure for this year
so far has been for the shutter restoration/replacement project. The Commission is currently under
budget.

Motion by Alex Herlihy to accept the Treasurer’s report. Seconded by Frank Lagana. All in favor.

Treasurer White noted that the 2027 budget will have three components:
    1) Annual operating budget to support fundraising opportunities and management of the
        commission;
    2) Reserve funds for a capital project; and
    3) Funds for the renovation of the first floor of Town Hall.

The Budget Committee and Select Board will be reviewing proposed department budgets for 2027 on
October 27th. The Commission will review what will be presented at their October monthly meeting.

    •   WELCOME TO RYE SIGNS

The ‘Welcome to Rye’ signs have been installed at their locations. An article about the signs has been
submitted for the October issue of Stroll Magazine. The Commission noted that flowers have been
planted around one of the signs, which looks beautiful. The Commission would like to thank whoever
planted the flowers. It was noted that there has been a lot of positive feedback from the residents about
the signs.

    •   TOWN HALL SHUTTERS

The Town Hall shutters were installed on August 25th.

    •   FLIER FOR NEW VETERAN’S MONUMENT

Treasurer White submitted a draft flier for the Commission’s review for the fundraiser for the veteran’s
monument. He noted that four bronze plates are going to be relocated from the existing war monument
with four additional bronze plates developed for all the wars, which will create an eight slab monument.
He suggested the Commission start the fundraising campaign in September. He pointed out that it may
take two fundraising cycles to get all the funds needed for the project. Treasurer White noted that the
project has been presented to the Select Board who were in full support.

The Commission suggested some changes to the flier. They discussed the details of the project and
possible costs. The monument is proposed to be located in the Town Green at the cemetery. The
Commission agreed to present the complete project at the table that will be set up at the upcoming
election. It was also agreed that more is needed on the flier before launching the fundraiser.

    •   GAZEBO STEP AND SEATING

The gazebo project is almost complete. The next steps involve the installation of a step and seating.
Member Choate read a proposal from Rye Beach Landscaping to complete the work. The proposal
includes an antique granite step at gazebo entry, and a gravel pathway for a total of $1,251.99. Member
Choate pointed out that Rye Beach Landscaping said that now is the time to do the plantings for the
landscaping, which should be something that doesn’t require a lot of maintenance.

Motion by Elizabeth Sanborn to accept the proposal from Rye Beach Landscaping for $1,251.99 to
bring the gazebo project closer to completion. Seconded by Alex Herlihy. All in favor.

V.        OTHER BUSINESS

      •   Chair Herlihy noted that there is enough staffing for the Heritage Commission table that will be
          set up at the election on September 8th. The hats and tote bags will be available for purchase at
          the table.

VI.       SPECIAL RECOGNITION

      •   Chair Herlihy recognized David Choate as one of the key members of the Friends of The Music
          Hall who played an instrumental role in saving the historical venue. The book by J. Dennis
          Robinson about The Music Hall outlines the history and the events that took place to save this
          iconic structure. David is featured in the writing of ‘Music Hall: How a City Built a Theater and
          a Theater Shaped a City’. Chair Herlihy thanked Member Choate for his work.

          ADJOURNMENT

Motion by Frank Lagana to adjourn at 6:51 p.m. Seconded by Peter White. All in favor.


Respectfully submitted,
Dyana F. Ledger
