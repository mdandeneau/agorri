Title: Minutes: Demolition Review Committee Agenda
Fetched: 2026-11-01

MINUTES

Demolition Review Committee
Meeting 09/23/2026

D. Choate called the meeting to order at 3:00 p.m. at the Rye Town Hall.
Present were: David Choate, Elizabeth Sanborn, Frank Lagana and Tom Sedoric (Alternate)
Guest: Mike Bryan, applicant.

The Minutes from the 08/31/2026 meeting were approved. E. Sanborn moved, F. Lagana se-
conded. T. Sedoric abstained as he was not at the meeting. Unanimous approval.

Purpose of today’s meeting: to review the application for the demolition of the barn at 51 Went-
worth Road.

E. Sanborn noted that the barn is architecturally important and historically significant. Since the
owner wants to replace the barn, we offered the help of review by Steve Bedard of Bedard
Preservation. Perhaps he (applicant) would also be able to take advantage of the NH 79D pro-
gram if he rebuilds the current barn. F. Lagana made a motion to table for a week to see if we
can arrange a site visit by next Friday with S. Bedard, then vote at a meeting at the Town Hall
afterwards. E. Sanborn 2nd. Tom Sedoric abstained as he is an abutter. The rest voted for
unanimous approval on the motion.

Other business: D. Choate once again asked the members of the commission to review the
current Demolition Permit Application to see how to reword it to make us the first stop before
any other steps are taken (e.g., asbestos removal). Currently, we are usually the last stop after
all of the other approvals on the application have been obtained. He noted that it is the Select
Board that can approve any changes to the application.

E. Sanborn moved to adjourn, F. Lagana 2nd. Unanimous approval.

Meeting adjourned at 3:56 p.m.

Respectfully submitted,
Elizabeth Sanborn
