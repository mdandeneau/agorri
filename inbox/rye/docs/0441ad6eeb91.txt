Discussion of State Legislation changes.

The School Warrant must now separate the SAU 50 costs from the Rye School Budget. So there will be
two budget warrants. One for the SAU cost and a second one for the Rye Schools and Portsmouth High
School.

HB 1300 will be a much more complex change. It will be on the November 2026 and November 2028
ballots. How the spending caps are calculated was discussed and it is not straight forward. This needs
to pass by a 3/5 majority and the School Board can hold education sessions.

The SAU will also no longer be able to retain a fund balance. Rye can expect about $100K credit for SAU
50 contributions.

HB 1575 requires the Budget Committee to prepare the default budget for the town and school.

Select Board Representative Kalet reported that the town is close having a full time Finance Director.
