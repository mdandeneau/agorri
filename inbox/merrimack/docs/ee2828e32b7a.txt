 2      1. Public Hearing – Chapter 195, Kratom Prohibition
 3          Submitted by Councilor Murphy and Prevention, Enforcement & Treatment Officer Justin Agraz
 4          The Town Council will hold a public hearing to review and consider the acceptance of Chapter 195,
 5          Kratom Prohibition, to be added as a chapter in the Merrimack Town Code, pursuant to Charter
 6          Article V.
 7
 8   Councilor Murphy stated that they are proposing a prohibition on the sale of kratom in the Town of
 9   Merrimack. She acknowledged that communications have been received from some Merrimack residents
10   and outside groups. Officer Agraz informed the Town Council that he has reached out to other communities
11   and the prosecutor regarding the fines. He explained that the prosecutor agreed that that the fines should be
12   increased from $50 to a step approach of $250 for the first offense per item, $500 for the second offense per
13   item and $1,000 for the third offense per item per calendar year. Officer Agraz stated that there are very few
14   communities that have imposed a ban on kratom. He referenced the following municipalities and fine
15   structures:
16           City of Nashua’s fine structure: $50 per day for the first offense, $100 per day for the second offense
17           and $250 per day for the third offense.
18           City of Franklin fine structure: $1,000 per day
19           Town of Lancaster fine structure: $1,000 per day for any psychoactive substance
20
21   Officer Agraz explained that there will be bills introduced later in the year at the State level. He stated that
22   the DA has been actively working this. He informed the Town Council that since August 13, 2026 the DA
23   has attempted to schedule 7-OH in a couple forms (Mitragynine pseudoindoxyl, MGM-15 and MGM-16).
24   He stated that on October 14, 2026 the DA issued a letter essentially withdrawing the attempt. Officer Agraz
25   explained that the DA is now working on a new schedule that will be based on total weight of 7-
26   hydroxymitragynine and Mitragynine pseudoindoxyl that is contained in the actual products. Officer Agraz
27   advised the Town Council that the reason for this change is because natural kratom does contain small trace
28   amounts of these two alkaloids when sold in the natural form.
29
30   Officer Agraz stated that the does not feel that the language the state will be considering is not appropriate
31   for Merrimack at this time because in order to adopt the state’s language there would need to be testing
32   mechanisms in place. He feels that the ordinance they are proposing allows for clear enforcement.
33
34   Officer Agraz addressed the concern relating to kratom extracts. He explained that the extracts are typically a
35   natural form of kratom however they do contain enhanced alkaloids. Officer Agraz advised the Town
36   Council that natural kratom is typically 1-2% mitragynine and many of the extract powders contain between
37   20-55% mitragynine. He stressed that they are extremely more potent than natural kratom leaf. He stated
38   that it is also important to know that mitragynine has adverse health effects and it is addictive.
39
40   Councilor Murphy reiterated this would be much easier if the Federal Government took action. She
41   explained the challenges at the State level. Councilor Murphy explained the goal of this is to protect
42   Merrimack residents by being proactive. She agreed that the testing issue would be a challenge for
43   Merrimack. Councilor Murphy explained that the proposed language will meet the needs of the community
44   and will be enforceable. Officer Agraz stated that he spoke to gentlemen this week who told him that if this
45   passes in Merrimack the rest of the state will look to Merrimack and duplicate our efforts.
46

11          Submitted by Town Manager Paul T. Micali
12          The Town Council to issue a proclamation for the dedication of a flag pole located at the Matthew
13          Thornton Graveyard in honor of the 250th Anniversary American Independence. Councilor Albert
14          read the following proclamation:
15
16                                  The Town of Merrimack, New Hampshire
17                                            by The Town Council
18                                               A Proclamation
19                           In the year of our Lord Two Thousand and Twenty-Six
20                 The 250th Anniversary of the founding of the United States of America
21   WHEREAS, the year 2026 marks the 250th Anniversary of the founding of the United States of America,
22   commemorating the courage, vision, and determination of those who secured our nation’s independence; and
23   WHEREAS, the American Revolution was sustained through the sacrifices and service of ordinary citizens
24   who answered the call to defend the principles of liberty, self-government, and individual rights; and
25   WHEREAS, the town of Merrimack proudly recognizes the men from our community who served as
26   Patriots during the American Revolution, enduring hardship, danger, and personal sacrifice in support of the
27   cause of independence; and
28   WHEREAS, the legacy of these Patriots continues to inspire present and future generations to cherish the
29   freedoms secured through their devotion and perseverance; and
30   WHEREAS, across generations, the men and women of Merrimack answered the call of duty to protect,
31   defend and preserve our freedoms and the founding principles upon which our nation was founded.
32   NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT PROCLAIMED, that the Town Council of Merrimack, New Hampshire
33   hereby recognizes and commemorates the 250th Anniversary of the founding of our nation and honors the
34   service and sacrifices of all veterans from Merrimack, New Hampshire who fought to establish and preserve
35   our freedoms.
36   BE IT FURTHER PROCLAIMED, that all citizens are encouraged to reflect upon the courage, dedication,
37   and sacrifices of these Veterans, and to participate in activities and observances that preserve and promote
38   the history and ideals of the American Revolution.
39   MOTION made by Councilor Thornton seconded by to adopt and issue the Liberty Pole Ceremony
40   Proclamation. MOTION CARRIES 7-0-0
