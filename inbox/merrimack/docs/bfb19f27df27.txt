Recognitions, Resignations and Retirements

    1. Recognition of Resignation of Town Employee
       Submitted by Human Resources Director Sharon Marunicz
       The Town Council will present a recognition award to former Equipment Operator III Julio Molinari
       for more than 13 years of full-time service to the Merrimack Public Works Solid Waste Division:
       December 11, 2012 – April 2, 2026

    2. Recognition of Retirement of Town Employee
       Submitted by Human Resources Director Sharon Marunicz
       The Town Council will present a recognition award to Equipment Operator II Robert Golemo in
       recognition of his retirement from the Merrimack Public Works Highway Division after more than 23
       years of full-time service: December 22, 2002 – September 4, 2026

    3. Recognition of Former Town Moderator Lynn Christensen
       Submitted by Town Council Chair Nancy Harrington and Vice Chair Jennifer Jobin
       The Town Council to present a recognition award to former Town Moderator Lynn Christensen for
       more than 34 years of service to the Town of Merrimack.

Appointments

    1. Annual Review with the Town Center Committee
       Submitted by Town Center Committee Chair Tyrel Borowitz
       Per Town of Merrimack Charter Section 6-6, at least annually, there should be an annual review with
       the Town Center Committee. This agenda item is to highlight the committee’s significant actions,
       current projects, anticipated actions, and to raise any concerns the Council should know or could act
       on.
Public Hearing

    1. Public Hearing - Timothy Mark Gibson Memorial Scholarship Fund
       Submitted by Tom Gibson
       The Town Council will hold a public hearing to authorize the acceptance and expenditure of a
       donation of up to $35,700 to the Timothy Mark Gibson Memorial Scholarship Fund held by the
       Trustees of Trust Funds, to be used towards scholarships for the Merrimack High School, pursuant to
       RSA 31:19, RSA 31:95-b and Charter Article 8-15.

New Business

    1. Proposed Eagle Scout Project for Twin Bridge Park
       Submitted by Boy Scout Brayden Miller
       The Town Council to consider Boy Scout Brayden Miller’s proposed Eagle Scout Project to build an
       information kiosk and map at Twin Bridge Park to replace an old wooden sign.

    2. Nashua Regional Planning Commission (NRPC) Walk Audit Program Discussion
       Submitted by Town Center Committee Chair Tyrel Borowitz
       The Town Council to be presented with the details and to consider the Town Center Committee’s
       recommendation to participate in the NRPC’s First Walk Audit Program and to consider writing a
       letter of support.

    3. Master Plan Steering Committee Establishment
       Submitted by Community Development Director Robert Price
       The Town Council to consider establishing a Master Plan Steering Committee, to provide guidance
       and oversee the development of a new Master Plan.

    4. Planning & Zoning Technician Position
       Submitted by Town Manager Paul T. Micali
       The Town Council to be presented with the details of the Planning & Zoning Technician position to
       be added to the Classification and Compensation Plan.

    5. Sklar Waterfront Park Trail Cameras Donation
       Submitted by Nick Ilyadis
       The Town Council to consider the acceptance of the donation of four (4) cellular connected trail
       cameras with a value of $1,168.00, for the Sklar Waterfront Park trails, pursuant to RSA 31:95-e and
       Charter Article 8-15.

    6. Merrimack Police Department Chief Position Confirmation
       Submitted by Town Manager Paul T. Micali
       The Town Council shall consider the confirmation of the appointment of a new Police Chief,
       pursuant to Charter Article 7-8.

    7. Capital Reserve Fund Expenditure Request for Wastewater Treatment Facility
       Submitted by Assistant Director of Public Works/Wastewater Leo Gaudette
       The Town Council to consider the request to expend up to $75,000 from the Sewer Infrastructure
       Capital Reserve Fund to be used to replace the standing seam metal roof on the Septage Receiving
       Building and perform other roof repairs on the two other roof areas, as identified in the FY 2026-27
       Minor CIP.

    8. MS-434 Update
       Submitted by Finance Director Paul Calabria
       The Town Council to receive an update on the MS-434.
