                          Town Council Meeting Agenda
            Thursday, October 22, 2026, at 7:00 PM, in the Matthew Thornton Room
     Call to Order
     Pledge of Allegiance
     Announcements
     Comments from the Press and Public
     Recognitions, Resignations and Retirements
     Appointments
       1.    Annual Review with the Conservation Commission
             Submitted by Conservation Commission Chair Karis Welsh
              Per Town of Merrimack Charter Section 6-6, at least annually, there should
            be an annual review with the Conservation Commission. This agenda item is
            to highlight the commission’s significant actions, current projects, anticipated
            actions, and to raise any concerns the Council should know or could act on.
       2.    Annual Review with the Invasive Species Committee
             Submitted by Invasive Species Committee Chair Matt Urban
             Per Town of Merrimack Charter Section 6-6, at least annually, there should
            be an annual review with the Invasive Species Committee. This agenda item
            is to highlight the committee’s significant actions, current projects, anticipated
            actions, and to raise any concerns the Council should know or could act
            on.
     Public Hearing
        1. Public Hearing – Chapter 195, Kratom Prohibition
            Submitted by Town Councilor Nancy Murphy and Prevention,
            Enforcement & Treatment (PET) Officer Justin Agraz
            The Town Council will hold a public hearing to review and consider the
            acceptance of Chapter 195, Kratom Prohibition, to be added as a chapter in
            the Merrimack Town Code, pursuant to Charter Article V.
