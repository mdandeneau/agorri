       conservation and recreation. She mentioned Leather Leaf Bog as an area that should not
       be widely open to visitors.

      Approval of plant population survey to be conducted by the Native Plant Trust at
       Gilmore Hill. The parcel is located at 37 Green Pond Road. Tax Map 3C Lot 73.

       Commissioner Perry said that an email he recently received from Native Plant Trust came
       specifically to him because over the past years he has accompanied them on their annual
       walk at Gilmore Hill. The letter’s purpose is to receive the permission of the MCC to
       conduct the walk in the coming weeks. The purpose of the walk is to monitor the
       condition of numerous plants that Plant Trust has been observing. The Community
       Development Department also received the letter. Commissioner Perry said that this
       event has until very recently been conducted with very little publicity to limit traffic to
       what is a sensitive area and that we should continue to do that. The exchange of emails
       between the Plant Trust, the MCC, and Community Development suggest that the final
       approval for the walk will come from the Town Manager after the MCC agrees to it.
       Commissioner Perry and The MCC expressed concern that this sequence is unnecessary
       and has the potential to further erode our attempts to protect the sensitive areas. Chair
       Welsh said that she will express those preferences with Community Development.

      Discussion regarding annual seedling purchase

       Chair Welsh said she added this item to the agenda now to be prepared ahead of time to
       smooth out the process of purchasing seedlings for the giveaway. Commissioner Perry
       said that last year we spent just under $800.00 and purchased 400-500 plants. He said that
       costs have gone up over time and supply is limited. A couple of years ago the state had a
       poor crop and ran out early so we started buying plants from the Arbor Day Society. He

      Discussion and approval regarding the expansion of the landing zone on Mitchell
       Street which will involve clearing 1/10 of an acre for the Watkins Forest & Mitchell
       Woods timber harvest

       Commissioner Duane asked about the location of the landing area. Is it along the street
       self or it in the cleared area a few hundred yards down. Commissioner Perry said that the
       area to be expanded is the original landing area that is now overgrown. We are cutting
       that area back. He said he walked it with Greg from DPW in the spring. He said that this
       area was under the plans of the Watkins Forest cutting. Chair Welsh said that we are
       discussing this now because the Trustees of the Trust Funds need permission from us.
       She said that it never came before the Commission. It came under discussion, but there
       was never a vote on it.

       Motion by Commissioner Duane to expand the landing zone on Mitchel Street to
       1/10th acre.

       Seconded by Commissioner Perry
       Motion Carried
       7-0-0

       the fogging on the plexiglass. In his research he learned that Lexan would be a better
       product instead of because it would be necessary to drill holes through the material and
       Plexiglass would break under those circumstances.

DISCUSSION/POSSIBLE ACTION REGARDING OTHER ITEMS OF CONCERN –

      Fields Farm Subcommittee first meeting – Submitted by Steve Perkins

       Commissioner Perkins said that logging is completed at Farm Fields, that the Equipment
       has been taken away, and that the landing area has been cleared. He said that there was
       rip-rap near the entry way on Amherst Road that would have to be replaced with
       something more suitable to daily traffic.

       Chair Welsh asked Commissioner Perkins if he could address the issue of a Bittersweet
       presence near the parking area. She said that cutting them back each year would help
       remedy the situation.

      Chair Welsh then said a business would like to hang a business flyer at one of the
       Conservation Commission properties. Chair Welsh said since the flyer was for a business
       she didn’t feel it was appropriate to hang. Commission consensus was that this should not
       be allowed.

       to improve access to the Souhegan River from Watson Park, which is currently steep and
