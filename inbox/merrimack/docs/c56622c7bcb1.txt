  1. Proposed Eagle Scout Project for Twin Bridge Park
     Submitted by Boy Scout Brayden Miller
     The Town Council to consider Boy Scout Brayden Miller’s proposed Eagle Scout Project to build an
     information kiosk and map at Twin Bridge Park to replace an old wooden sign.

Boy Scout Brayden Miller was present to discuss his Eagle Scout Project with the Council, which is to build
an information kiosk and map at Twin Bridge Park to replace the wooden sign that is currently there. He first
shared that he is a Life Scout with Troop 15, and has been in scouting for over 8 years. He has served as
Senior Patrol Leader, Assistant Senior Patrol Leader, Patrol Leader, and Instructor. He currently attends
MHS (class of 2027), and has interests in advanced academic coursework, distance running, spaceflight,
model rocketry, science, and engineering. He then reviewed his project with the Council, and discussed how
the current map at Twin Bridge is aging and lacks information, and there are no signs or directions at the
northern entrance directly below the parking lot, which are issues he is hoping to resolve. His project serves
to build a new map of Twing Bridge Park and its trail network to cover the other main entrance directly
below the parking lot, and will include a kiosk, new to-scale map, noticeboard, and educational/historical
section. He shared that he is currently going through all his project approvals, and once that step is
completed, he will finalize the planning build and fundraising dates and then gather the materials and tools
required to build, with the installation planned for late October to early November. Mr. Miller then shared
that he will lead the planning, fundraising, volunteers, getting materials, building, decorating, and guiding
installation, and any youths that are helping out will have at least two adults monitoring them at all times,
with power tools only being used by trained individuals. He is hoping to have a spaghetti dinner at the VFW
sometime in November as his fundraiser, and expects his total costs to be roughly $650. He then reviewed
the benefits of his completed project, which include improved clarity of the various trails, educational
information about the park, and an improved park experience for visitors. Councilor Albert suggested
contacting the Merrimack Historical Society and Chuck Mower to help Mr. Miller get historical context of
Twin Bridge. Mr. Micali suggested utilizing DPW and the Highway dept to help with the digging and answer
questions about the project.

MOTION made by Councilor Rothhaus and seconded by Councilor Albert to accept Boy Scout
Brayden Miller’s proposed Eagle Scout Project to build an information kiosk and map at Twin Bridge
Park to replace an old wooden sign, and authorize the Town Manager to sign any necessary
documents.
MOTION CARRIES 6-0-0

       Submitted by Town Center Committee Chair Tyrel Borowitz

    4. Planning & Zoning Technician Position
       Submitted by Town Manager Paul T. Micali
       The Town Council to be presented with the details of the Planning & Zoning Technician position to
       be added to the Classification and Compensation Plan.

Mr. Micali shared that every time an individual leaves their position, they take the time to look at their
department needs and see if positions can be changed or added. Mr. Price shared that this new position is
essentially a reorganization of the Assistant Planner position that they have, as zoning and site plan
violations and enforcement have been at the bottom of their department’s priority list for at least the past
decade, if not longer, for timing reasons. The Planning and Zoning Administrator’s position cannot dedicate
the time needed to conduct enforcement effectively, as they are inundated with other higher priority
workloads, deadlines, meetings, etc. The Assistant Planner position became vacant in August 2026, so they
are taking the opportunity now to review the job description and make changes necessary for the
department’s needs. With this new proposed position, they are hoping to increase the necessary work
experience to 3 years to make it a more skilled position, and they will be tasked with handling all the duties
of the Assistant Planner position as well as the additional enforcement expectations without an overall
negative impact to their workload. Additional duties would include administrative approvals, conducting
zoning review for sign permits, administrative support to the Conservation Commission to assist the Part
Time Administrative Assistant, and serve as department GIS liaison. Mr. Micali shared that this new
position only increases by 1 grade, and is a $6,700 increase in salary to create this needed position.

    8. MS-434 Update
       Submitted by Finance Director Paul Calabria
       The Town Council to receive an update on the MS-434.

Mr. Micali shared that this is a form that the town must submit every year, and this is for the revenues as
they vote on the appropriations in April, and in September they have to send up the revenues for the RA to
review, which helps set the tax rate. During the budget process, they send up estimates for what they think
the tax rate will be based on revenues that they know at the time, and as the year goes on, they can see how
they are tracking. He also shared that right now, they’re projecting the tax rate to be $3.28 if the Council is in
agreement with $80,000 for interest, an increase of $100,000 for building permits, an increase of $100,000
for car registrations, and a decrease of in lieu taxes of about $3,700. Overall, they are increasing revenues of
$276,231, which he is projecting is a $2.28 tax rate for the municipality, which is a $0.32 increase from last
year. He stressed that this has nothing to do with the revaluation, but rather the vote on the increases to the
budget, 3 union contracts that passed, and the bond payment.

MOTION made by Councilor Rothhaus and seconded by Councilor Albert to approve the increases to
interest of $80,000, building permits of $100,000, car registrations of $100,000, in lieu taxes decrease of
$3,769, and at this time not to use any fund balance to help offset the tax rate.
MOTION CARRIES 6-0-0
