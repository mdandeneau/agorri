    1. Public Hearing – Acceptance of a FEMA Assistance to Firefighter Grant
       Submitted by Fire Chief Joseph Guarnera
       The Town Council will hold a public hearing to authorize the acceptance and expenditure of grants
       funds of up to $169,200, with the Town’s matching share of $16,200, for the Assistance to
       Firefighters Grant made available through the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA),
       which will assist up to six firefighters to obtain paramedic licensure, and to authorize the Town
       Manager or his designee to execute any and all documents which may be necessary, pursuant to RSA
       31:95-b and Charter Article 8-15.
