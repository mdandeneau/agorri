Agenda - 10/22/2026 1. 7:00 P.M. Town Council Regular Meeting Agenda Town Council Meeting Agenda Thursday, October 22, 2026, at 7:00 PM, in the Matthew Thornton Room Call to Order Pledge of Allegiance Announcements Comments from the Press and Public Recognitions, Resignations and Retirements Appointments Annual Review with the Conservation Commission Submitted by Conservation Commission Chair Karis Welsh Per Town of Merrimack Charter Section 6-6, at least annually, there should be an annual review with the Conservation Commission. This agenda item is to highlight the commission's significant actions, current projects, anticipated actions, and to raise any concerns the Council should know or could act on. Annual Review with the Invasive Species Committee Submitted by Invasive Species Committee Chair Matt Urban Per Town of Merrimack Charter Section 6-6, at least annually, there should be an annual review with the Invasive Species Committee. This agenda item is to highlight the committee's significant actions, current projects, anticipated actions, and to raise any concerns the Council should know or could act on. Public Hearing 1. Public Hearing - Chapter 195, Kratom Prohibition Submitted by Town Councilor Nancy Murphy and Prevention, Enforcement & Treatment (PET) Officer Justin Agraz The Town Council will hold a public hearing to review and consider the acceptance of Chapter 195, Kratom Prohibition, to be added as a chapter in the Merrimack Town Code, pursuant to Charter Article V. Legislative Updates from State Representatives Town Manager's Report Consent Agenda Old Business New Business 1. Request for Research Access on Town Land - Native Plant Populations Submitted by Native Plant Trust Community Science Coordinator Elizabeth Johnson The Town Council to consider a request for permission for Native Plant Trust to conduct a survey to monitor specific native plant populations located off of Greens Pond Road. 2. Repair of Souhegan River Access at Watson Park Discussion Submitted by Andrew Duane The Town Council to discuss the request for the repair of the Souhegan River access at Watson Park. 3. Citizen Recognition Policy Submitted by Town Councilor Peter Albert The Town Council to discuss the proposed Citizen Recognition Policy. The Town Council created this goal during the 2026 Town Council Retreat / Goal Setting Session. 4. 2026 Merrimack Liberty Pole Ceremony Proclamation Submitted by Town Manager Paul T. Micali The Town Council to issue a proclamation for the dedication of a flag pole located at the Matthew Thornton Graveyard in honor of the 250 th Anniversary American Independence. 5. Consideration of Changes to Chapter 183, Vehicles and Traffic [First Reading] Submitted by Town Manager Paul T. Micali The Town Council to consider the recommended changes to Chapter 183, Vehicles and Traffic, of the Merrimack Town Code, pursuant to Charter Article V. 6. Direction for 2027/2028 Budget Submitted by Town Manager Paul T. Micali and Finance Director Paul Calabria To ask the Town Council to contemplate budget parameters for the 2027/2028 budget season.
