NEW BUSINESS –

      Discussion and recommendation to the Town Council regarding proposed
       Wasserman Park Fitness Loop Revitalization project with final approval from the
       Town Council – Submitted by Karis Welsh

       Chloe Foss appeared before the Commission to discuss her and her family’s desire to
       undertake a project to improve the Fitness Loop Trail in Wasserman Park. She began by
       introducing her family members and explaining their motivations for this volunteer

       service. Her presentation elaborated on five points that she would be addressing: The
       purpose, the plan, the method, the design, and the future. Building materials for items that
       need to be built would cost around $2,000.00 and a sign quote from Alpha graphics for
       12 X 14 printed aluminum one-sided signs, which comes in around $500.00. If approved,
       the project would begin next Summer and be completed by the Fall at the latest.

       To successfully bring materials onto trails, they would need a UTV or ATV. She added
       that her husband would be donating use of his tools to help complete the project. Ms.
       Foss then went over the proposed design details of the project and spoke about some
       signs and trail markers she was proposing to add to the trail. She said she is not proposing
       any changes to the course or length of the 1.5 mile trail. There was discussion about how
       UTV/ATV access was approved so that materials could be dropped off along the trail. At
       the conclusion of her presentation, the Commission offered suggestions and advice:

             Seek approval from the Town Council to work on areas that are on town land
             Establish contact with Parks and Rec
             Be familiar with the town’s policies about using power tools and transporting
              materials and equipment to the work site
             Keep the Commission apprised about when they will be working on the parcel
             Contact NEMBA (New England Mountain Biking Association) who would be a
              valuable asset
             Become familiar with the town’s volunteer policies.

       Motion by Vice-Chair Kolb to recommend the Wasserman Project by the Foss
       Family favorably to the Town Council.

       Seconded by Commissioner Perry
       Motion Carried
       7-0-0

      Discussion on planning an Earth Month in collaboration with the Library – Submitted
       by Karis Welsh

       Chair Welsh proposed participating in a joint venture with the Town Library and Parks
       and Recreation Department to recognize Earth Day in April of 2027. She said that we
       could create a month-long calendar that would list activities and speakers, and possibly
       post relevant displays in the library. Commissioners Hrobak and Lumens have agreed to
       organize and plan activities.

       Motion by Commissioner Perry to approve planning of an earth month in
       collaboration with the Library and Parks and Rec.

       Seconded by Commissioner Duane
       Motion Carried
       7-0-0

      Commissioner Duane said that he has been working on improving as access point in the
       Souhegan River. It was on the agenda for the next Town Council meeting, but it has been
       removed from that agenda because there are legal issues that would need to be resolved.
       He will meet with Paul Micali to discuss those issues. Previous attempts to undertake
       this task have been abandoned because this access point must be in ADA compliance, a
       challenge that will be difficult achieve. He is meeting with Mr. Micali to come up with a
       plan that will address the ADA requirements. Commissioner Duane also spoke about a
       piece of rebar on the beach that needs to be dug out. Chair Welsh said that digging is not
       allowed in Watson Park.

      Chair Welsh said that there is a Scout interested in doing an Eagle Scout Project. Under
       discussion is working on Invasive Plants removal at Watson Park. She also spoke about a
       planned Girl Scout activity involving Invasive plant removal that was rained out but will
       be rescheduled.

      Councilor Landrigan said that the agenda item involving security cameras for the Sklar
       property was tabled by the Town Council for further discussion. More information is
       needed to address this issue in more depth to make sure all safety considerations are
       taken.

      Chair Welsh said that she is waiting for the Horse Hill and Grater Woods subcommittees
       to give her a list of their maintenance needs. The Commission would like to put out bids
       for any necessary work such as mowing, so that it can begin in the spring.

      Vice-Chair Kolb thanked the Department of Public Works, the Police Department, and
       Boy Scout Troop 22 for the work they did at Horse Hill. There is enough material left
       over to replace the broken boardwalk on the Loop Trail. This project will go before the
       Town Council for approval.

      Commissioner Starr spoke about what the Wasserman LCIP site walk (Land
       Conservation Investment Plan) is and why it is done. He explained that the Commission
       bought Wasserman Park with a grant from the State. The grant required the Commission
       to walk the site for an annual inspection of the park followed up with a written report to
       be submitted to the State. The walk has been scheduled for October 24, 2026 from at
       10:00 am to noon.
