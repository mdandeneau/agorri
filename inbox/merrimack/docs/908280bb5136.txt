Title: Agenda: Fields Farm Subcommittee Site Walk
Fetched: 2026-11-01

FIELDS FARM SUBCOMMITTEE
        A SUBCOMMITTEEE OF THE MERRIMACK CONSERVATION COMMISSION
                             OCTOBER 4, 2026
                                 SITE WALK
                                 10:00 A.M.


You are hereby notified that the Fields Farm Subcommittee will hold a site walk on Sunday,
October 4 at 10:00 a.m. at 221 Amherst Road. Subcommittee members will meet at the
landing space along Amherst Road. The purpose of the site walk is to access the property after
the completion of recent forestry activities. A quorum of the subcommittee may meet but no
motions or decisions will be made at the site walk.
