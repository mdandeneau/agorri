                                 Griffin Free Public Library
                                Performance Review Meeting
                                   September 28th, 2026

Present: Nancy Mayland, Chair; Liz Michaud, Secretary; Cindy Berling, Treasurer; Marilyn
Cavanaugh, Alternate

Absent: Jackie Wood, Alternate; Andrea Galasso, Alternate

Nancy Mayland started the meeting at 6:56pm.

Motion to enter non-public session pursuant to the provisions of RSA 91-A:3,II(a) The dismissal,
promotion, or compensation of any public employee or the disciplining of such employee, made
by Liz Michaud/ Cindy Berling seconded the motion. A roll call vote was taken. Nancy Mayland
voted aye, Cindy Berling voted aye, Liz Michaud voted aye. With all in favor, the motion passed
unanimously.
The meeting room was closed to the public at 7:02 pm.
The Board discussed and wrote the annual review for our Library Director, Dan Szczesny.

A motion to exit the non-public session made by Liz Michaud/ Cindy Berling 2nd. All in favor.


Motion to adjourn the meeting made by Nancy Mayland /Cindy Berling 2nd. All in favor.

Meeting adjourned at 8:14 pm
Liz Michaud
Secretary
