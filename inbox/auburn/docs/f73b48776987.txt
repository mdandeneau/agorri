V. New Business

A. 2027 Budget

Town Administrator Sterndale provided the Board with a spreadsheet dated 10/12/26 for the
proposed FY 2027 municipal budget. The Board reviewed the trust fund balances spreadsheet
also provided.

       i. Planning

       Planning Board Chairman, Jeff Porter, presented the proposed FY 2027 budget for
       Planning & Zoning in the amount of $30,250, a decrease of 5.32%.

Mr. Carroll motioned to approve the proposed FY 2027 budget for Planning &
Zoning in the amount of $30,250. Mr. Bedard seconded the motion. A vote was

ii. Conservation

Conservation Commission Chairman, Jeff Porter, presented the proposed FY 2027
budget for Conservation Administration in the amount of $1,703 a 57.20% decrease.
Mr. Porter noted they are working with NH Audubon.

Mr. Carroll motioned to approve the proposed FY 2027 budget for Conservation
Administration in the amount of $1,703. Mr. Bedard seconded the motion. A vote
was taken, all were in favor, the motion passed unanimously.

iii. Town Clerk

Town Clerk Caitlyn Folia presented the proposed FY 2027 budget for Elections,
Registrations & Vital Statistics in the amount of $175,293, a 14.69% increase.

Mrs. Folia indicated that the software line for motor vehicle registrations is going up by
6%. She noted there is only one election next year. She proposed $8,315 to purchase
new voting booths and noted there is one set up upstairs that the Selectmen can look at
and they are easy to set up. Mr. Leclair asked the number of stations set up for a
presidential election and she indicated 44. Mr. Leclair recommended purchasing ten
from this year’s budget (FY 26) at $1,000 ea. and to go ahead and order them. Mr.
Carroll agreed. The line for Election Expenses for FY 27 was reduced by $8,315. Mrs.
Folia recommended purchasing electronic polling pads at a cost of $12,900 which the
Board did not approve. The line for Election Expenses for FY 27 was further reduced by
$12,900.

Mr. Carroll motioned to approve the proposed FY 2027 budget for Elections,
Registrations & Vital Statistics in the amount of $152,278. Mr. Bedard seconded
the motion. A vote was taken, all were in favor, the motion passed unanimously.

iv. Tax Collector

Tax Collector Patricia Loranger presented the proposed FY 2027 budget for the Tax
Collector (Financial Administration) in the amount of $63,326 which is a 4.63% increase.

Mrs. Loranger explained each line including deed research which is done for properties
that will be liened. There are typically 15-20 on average each year. She noted they are
using a new company who is less expensive. She reviewed the hours of the deputy
collector. She reviewed the line for tax bill printing and explained that the year-to-date
expenses do not reflect half for the July bill and half for the December bill because some
of that is paid in advance.

       iv. Newsletter

       She noted that election printing takes up a full page and not as many full-page

VI. Old Business

A. Elderly Exemption
Town Administrator Sterndale provided the Board with a Memorandum dated Oct 15, 2026
relevant to the request by a resident to review the income limits for the elderly exemption. He
noted the exemption amounts increased in 2023 by age group, and the income and asset
thresholds set at town meeting in 2023. He noted the number of households receiving the
exemption as 13. He compared nine comparable sized towns and four neighboring towns. He
noted those denied are typically due to income, not assets. Mr. Leclair noted that a citizen’s
petition two years ago was procedurally missed. Mr. Leclair noted to change the limits would
require a warrant article and feels it show grow with the CPI. He was in favor of leaving it as is
noting that someone could put a petition in. Mr. Carroll agreed.
B. Finance Software

Town Administrator Sterndale provided the Board with a copy of the proposed lock-in
agreement with Munismart dated October 7, 2026 for review. Mr. Sterndale noted that many
towns have gone with a cloud-based software which Mr. Leclair noted is more expensive. The
Board will table approval to investigate other options.
