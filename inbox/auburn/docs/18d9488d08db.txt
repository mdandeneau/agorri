Title: Minutes: Parks & Recreation Commission Meeting Agenda
Fetched: 2026-11-01

Town of Auburn
                                            Parks and Recreation Commission
                                                Tuesday, October 6, 2026
                                                        6:00 pm
                                           Severance School Community Center

Commissioners Present: Zach Eaton, Chairman; Margie McEvoy, Vice Chairman; Mary Royer; Jennifer Strabone; Carly
Cohen; Tom Carroll, Selectman Representative

Others Present: Amy Lachance, P&R Director

Budget 2027 Meeting Schedule- Our budget has been submitted. We’ll meet with the Board of Selectmen on Monday,
November 30 at 7:00 pm at the Town Hall. Our meeting with the Budget Committee is tentatively scheduled for
Thursday, December 17 at 7:00 pm at the Town Hall. Amy will send out updates if it’s anything different.

Auburn Night & Auburn Day Parade- The Auburn Night set up around back and the new food trucks all worked out
great. So much better with everything in one place. The band was great too. All are planning to return next year. The
duck race went well too and the addition of the parade was fun. If the AHA plans to do the parade again next year, we’ll
participate again.

Tree Lighting- The annual Tree Lighting is scheduled for Sunday, November 29 at 6:00 pm at AVS. Instead of a DJ this
year, we’re going to play our own music on a sound system. Carly will check with the school to see if the chorus would
be able to perform. Also Carly arranged to have Elsa join the event this year.

Facilities Updates- Mary mentioned there were flags in the center of town that need to be replaced or removed. Cole
or Ray can check that out. Portapotties will be removed at the end of the month and trash pick up will end mid-
November.

Auburn Little League- Fall Ball is ongoing. Lots of younger kids this year. Fields are all looking great.

Ms. Royer motioned to accept the minutes of September 1, 2026 of the Parks and Recreation Commission
Meeting. Ms. Cohen seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

Ms. Cohen motioned to change the November P&R Commission meeting from Tuesday, November 3, 2026 to
Tuesday, November 17, 2026 and cancel the December 1, 2026 meeting. Ms. Strabone seconded the motion.
The motion passed unanimously.

Ms. McEvoy has motioned to adjourn the meeting. Ms. Strabone seconded the motion. The motion was passed
unanimously.
