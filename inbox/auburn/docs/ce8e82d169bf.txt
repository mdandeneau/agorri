Title: Minutes: Board of Selectmen Meeting 10-12-26
Fetched: 2026-11-01

Town of Auburn
                                     Board of Selectmen
                                    Stritch Meeting Room
                                          Town Hall
                                      October 12, 2026
                                             7 PM
                                     Approved Minutes

Selectmen Present: Keith Leclair, Tom Carroll and Todd Bedard

Others Present: Finance Director Mimi Friolet, Cemetery Trustee Walter Milne, Herb Tardiff,
Gary Domourad, Town Administrator Chris Sterndale, and Nancy Hoijer, Recording Secretary

I. Call to Order

Mr. Leclair called the meeting to order at 7 PM and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

II. Public Comments

Mr. Leclair asked if there were any comments from the public at 7 PM.

Herb Tardiff asked about updates in the income levels required for elderly exemptions. Mr.
Leclair indicated that process has not started yet this year.

Mr. Tardiff asked about the status of Depot Road and Mr. Leclair indicated the goal was to
acquire the land, which was finalized and that road reconstruction work is done by priority. No
funds were earmarked, just the land swap was approved.

III. Appointments with the Board
IV. Consent Agenda
Mr. Leclair read out loud and offered for inspection the Consent Agenda dated October 12, 2026
which consisted of approval of an employee step increase, and appointments to the ZBA and
Scenic Byway Council.

V. New Business

A. 2027 Budget

Town Administrator Sterndale provided the Board with a spreadsheet dated 10/8/26 for the
proposed FY 2027 municipal budget in the proposed amount of $7,564,425, a 6.7% increase.
The Board reviewed the trust fund balances also provided. Mr. Sterndale noted for upcoming
presentations that the fire department may be looking for more call hours, COLA and health
insurance premium increases are in; winter sand is tricky but the salt numbers are in. Mr. Carroll
noted that Parks & Rec. will be redoing the pickleball courts with focus on regrading and
drainage (removing curbing).

                                                1
i. Cemeteries

Cemetery Trustee Walter Milne presented the proposed FY 2027 budget for Cemeteries
in the amount of $35,150, a 3.61% decrease.

Mr. Milne noted mowing will be over. Finance Director Friolet noted fertilizer costs have
increased. Mr. Leclair asked if anyone had reached out to Plaza. Mr. Milne noted there
may be an increase but he has been happy with their work.

Mr. Milne discussed property maintenance and big projects. He recommended cutting
down on maintenance work on the Auburn Village crypt because it is not used. He
noted some dead trees in the new section acquired from MWW, and a big maple
dropping limbs. Mr. Leclair asked if the maple could be trimmed.

Mr. Carroll motioned to approve the proposed FY 2027 budget for Cemeteries in
the amount of $35,150. Mr. Bedard seconded the motion. A vote was taken, all
were in favor, the motion passed unanimously.

ii. Building Inspector/Code Enforcement

Building Inspector Mark Ekberg presented the proposed FY 2027 budget for Building
Inspection in the amount of $88,360 which is 4.73% decrease.

Mr. Ekberg noted the drop in wages resulting from Ms. Rouleau-Cote’s retirement and
his new hire. He noted engineering services have not been used so he lowered the line
to $1. He anticipates a 5% increase in the software line because PermitEyes was
bought out. The legal line was discussed as to whether this should be separate or in a
pool. Mr. Sterndale noted there is some value in tracking which department is spending
what. Mr. Ekberg noted two conferences, one in the spring and one in the fall.

Mr. Leclair asked about the vehicle and Mr. Ekberg anticipated need of brakes and tires.
There is 130,000 miles and this was part of a turnover to replace a vehicle driven by Ms.
Rouleau-Cote which had higher mileage. Mr. Leclair did not anticipate having a vehicle
out to auction this year, but perhaps next year.

Mr. Ekberg commented on the use of the field device which allows him to bring it along
to inspections and upload photos. He noted the cost is $20/mo. and the IPad only works
with Apple compatible products. He noted use of the cell phone can come down some.
Mr. Sterndale noted, relative to the field device, the efficiency gain is worth it.

Mr. Ekberg reviewed revenue and noted a lot of revenue from the 55 and over units. He
discussed whether fees could be adjusted as there has been no update the past two-
four years.

B. Fuel Purchase

Town Administrator Sterndale provided the Board with bids for fixed heating oil and fixed
propane costs from November 1, 2026 to October 31, 2027.

Irving provided a submittal dated October 5, 2026 with a fixed heating oil price of $4.765/gal.
and a fixed propane price of $1.54/gal which Mr. Sterndale noted was much lower and prices
are based on quantity used last year.

The Board indicated they were okay with Mr. Sterndale entering into the Irving contract.

VI. Old Business
A. Purchasing Policy
The Board reviewed the updated draft of the Purchasing Policy and. Appendix for Credit Card
Users. Mr. Leclair reviewed the revisions.

Mr. Carroll motioned to amend and adopt the revised Purchasing Policy. Mr. Bedard
seconded the motion. A vote was taken, all were in favor, the motion passed
unanimously.

B. Police Department Grant Authorization

Town Administrator Sterndale provided the Board with the Highway Safety Grant Agreement.

                                                3
Mr. Carroll motioned to accept the terms of the State of New Hampshire Highway Safety
Grant as presented in the amount of $8,500, and further authorizes the Chief of Police,
Charles Chabot, to sign all documents related to the grant. Mr. Bedard seconded the
motion. A vote was taken, all were in favor, the motion passed unanimously.

VII. Committee Reports
Mr. Carroll reported that Parks & Recreation met and shifted one of their meetings that was
scheduled prior to the elections.

Mr. Leclair reported that Budget Committee will begin meeting on October 29th and Deborah
Lagano was appointed to the vacant seat.

VIII. Town Administrator Updates
Town Administrator Sterndale reported that the E-Waste event went smoothly on Saturday and
more separate containers were set up. Mr. Leclair noted it was busy when he was there and
naturally the question is whether all participants were residents. He recommended filling out a
form, checking for dump stickers, or checking identification next year. Ms. Friolet noted that it is
not advertised out of town.
Mr. Sterndale reported the generator was installed at the Safety Complex on Thursday.
Mr. Sterndale reported on Griffin Mill Bridge and noted there was no equipment out there.
Mr. Sterndale noted that Elections are on November 3rd. There will be a recognition for Sue
Jenkins.

IX. Minutes

A. September 28, 2026

Mr. Carroll motioned to approve the September 28, 2026 minutes. Mr. Bedard seconded
the motion. A vote was taken, all were in favor, the motion passed unanimously.

B. Consideration of unsealing non-public minutes: June 9 and July 2, 2014

Mr. Carroll motioned to keep sealed the non-public minutes of June 9, 2014 and July 2,
2014. Mr. Bedard seconded the motion. A vote was taken, all were in favor, the motion
passed unanimously.
