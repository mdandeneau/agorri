Town of Auburn
                                     Board of Selectmen
                                    Stritch Meeting Room
                                          Town Hall
                                     September 28, 2026

                                     Approved Minutes

Selectmen Present: Tom Carroll and Todd Bedard

Others Present: Alan Villeneuve, Finance Director Mimi Friolet, Gary Domourad, Mike
DiPietro, Town Administrator Chris Sterndale, and Nancy Hoijer, Recording Secretary

I. Call to Order

Acting Chair Carroll called the meeting to order at 7 PM and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

II. Public Comments

Mr. Carroll asked if there were any comments from the public at 7 PM.

Ray Racicot asked to discuss the snowplow contracts. He noted there is no increase and fuel
charges are extremely high. He made comparisons to other towns like Chester and Candia and
stated they get $5/hr. more and a fuel charge. He noted the insurance requirements in the
contract were new. Mr. Sterndale recommended getting on the agenda to discuss more detail
of the issue with the insurance clause. Mr. Carroll agreed to meet with them and hear their
concerns to bring back to the Board at their next meeting.

III. Appointments with the Board
A. Southern NH Snow Slickers – Mark Pepper
Town Administrator Sterndale provided the Board with a locus map of the area by Exit 2 and
Auburndale Lane.
Mark Pepper requested use of a couple hundred feet to access Auburn Tavern. He noted
Manchester Water Works gave permission, but DOT said no to use along Exit 2 at Route 101.
He explained the route and added a request for permission to use a small part of the road.
Mr. Carroll asked if he had discussions with the police chief and he indicated he had not.
Mr. Villeneuve explained that no one wants snow machines along the highway.
Mr. Sterndale asked if there was resistance to use of the road from neighbors. Mr. Villeneuve
explained there was a section of road with a deep gully.
Mr. DiPietro noted snowmobiles have a long history in Auburn, and recommended holding a
public hearing so everyone could be heard. Mr. Sterndale agreed that in order to use the

roadway they would need to do that. Mr. Carroll proposed a site walk. There should be
signage. Mr. Carroll proposed an area where the police department could be present to enforce.

IV. Consent Agenda
V. Public Hearing – Unanticipated Revenue

Mr. Carroll opened the public hearing at 7:15 PM and asked if anyone had any questions.
Mr. Sterndale provided a brief history of the grant and a decade of design work and noted the
80% reimbursement over the years. He noted it could be accepted as unanticipated revenue to
the fund balance or use some of it to do the landscape design work.

Mr. Domourad asked about quotes and Mr. Sterndale explained that someone is developing the
preliminary design and pricing and then they will get quotes.

Mr. Domourad asked about construction progress and Mr. Sterndale explained the new federal
approval methods.

Mr. Villeneuve asked about funds put aside for the 20% the town was responsible for. Mr.
Sterndale noted funds were encumbered each year coming from funds not spent at the end of
the year. There is no evidence of a reserve fund. Highway grant funds were used. Funds will
come back to the same place they are going back to.

Mr. Carroll closed the public hearing at 7:25 PM.

Mr. Bedard motioned that the Board of Selectmen accept $168,422.65 from NH Dept. of
Transportation as unanticipated revenue and further authorizes the Town Administrator
to spend up to that amount on work related to the area near Griffin Mill Bridge. Mr.
Carroll seconded the motion. A vote was taken, all were in favor, the motion passed
unanimously.

VI. New Business

A. Unanticipated Revenue

Mr. Sterndale discussed the generator at the safety complex which was replaced out of the
facility fund and the police dept. server encumbered in December of 2025 which qualify for
287g.

Mr. Bedard motioned the Board authorize the use of previously accepted unanticipated
revenue from the Dept. of Homeland Security 287g program for the following equipment
for the police department:

       1. For a replacement generator $27,268; and
       2. For police IT equipment, $8,980.

Mr. Carroll seconded the motion. A vote was taken, all were in favor, the motion passed
unanimously.

B. 2025 Audit

Town Administrator Sterndale review the audit results from Vachon, Cluckay for 2025 and noted
two findings: GASBY, future retirement liabilities which the Board decided it was not worth
tracking; and payroll tracking, where forms for increase pay rates need to be signed and
returned by employees. Mrs. Friolet is working on that electronically.
Mr. Villeneuve recommended using a different company every 3-5 years.

C. 2027 Budget Preview and Calendar

Town Administrator Sterndale provided the Board with a list of fund balances, a Budget
Summary by Department dated 9/28/26 noting the percent increase is closer to 7.22%, the
preliminary draft of the FY 2027 budget and meeting schedule for the Board and Budget
Committee.

Mr. Sterndale reviewed COLA projections estimated to be 3.5% per the mid-October Social
Security determination in accordance with the personnel policy. No one has reported new
positions. The new full-time police officer will be a full year next year. Some one-time items are
police cruiser, renovation of fire department kitchen and pickleball courts. He noted there will be
zoning amendments, the library proposal, and annual deposits to the property maintenance ETF
and revaluation fund by warrant article.

Mr. Villeneuve asked if DRA had set the tax rate and Mr. Sterndale indicated no.

D. Library Maintenance Request

The Board reviewed an estimate from BPS for replacement of the children’s room door and
associated work.

Mr. Carroll proposed that the Board could not move on such a high number. Mr. Bedard will
provide more information from another contractor.

E. Police Department Grant Authorization

Mr. Bedard motioned the Board authorize Police Chief Charles Chabot to apply for NH
Dept. of Safety Highway Grants and to sign related documents. Mr. Carroll seconded the
motion. A vote was taken, all were in favor, the motion passed unanimously.

VII. Old Business
A. Purchasing Policy – Tabled
The Board reviewed the updated draft of the Purchasing Policy and. Appendix for Credit Card
Users. The Board agreed to wait for Mr. Leclair to review the revisions.
