Title: Minutes: Board of Selectmen 9-14-26
Fetched: 2026-11-01

Town of Auburn
                                    Board of Selectmen
                                   Stritch Meeting Room
                                         Town Hall
                                    September 14, 2026
                                            7 PM
                                    Approved Minutes

Selectmen Present: Keith Leclair, Tom Carroll and Todd Bedard

Others Present: Mike DiPietro, Herb Tardiff, Town Administrator Chris Sterndale, and Nancy
Hoijer, Recording Secretary

I. Call to Order

Mr. Leclair called the meeting to order at 7 PM and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

II. Public Comments

The Board extended their thoughts and prayers to long term resident and meeting attendee,
Carol Peterson who is at Pleasant Valley rehab with a serious illness.

III. Appointments with the Board
IV. Consent Agenda
Mr. Leclair read out loud and offered for inspection the Consent Agenda dated September 14,
2026 which consisted of step increases for employees and appointment of a ZBA member.
Mr. Carroll motioned to approve the Consent Agenda dated September 14, 2026. Mr.
Bedard seconded the motion. A vote was taken, all were in favor, the motion passed
unanimously.

V. New Business

A. MS-1 Review

Town Administrator Sterndale provided the 2026 MS-1 (Summary Inventory of Valuation for the
Town). He indicated a $1.5 billion-dollar net evaluation for the town and discussed growth and
changes to disabled veteran’s tax credits. He provided examples of commercial and industrial
tax base.

B. Salt Contract

Town Administrator Sterndale provided the Board with 2026 salt bids. The Board decided to go
with the $83.5/ton bid from Eastern Minerals.


                                                1
Mr. Carroll motioned to authorize the Highway Director to enter into contract for winter
road salt with Eastern Minerals. Mr. Bedard seconded the motion. A vote was taken, all
were in favor, the motion passed unanimously.

VI. Old Business
A. Fire Department Trailer Sale
Town Administrator Sterndale reported that the Fire Department is looking to sell their existing
trailer at auction and to replace it with one to be used with the gator. He asked if the Board
would like to see this included in the budget or pay for it from the vehicle fund. The Board
agreed they would like to see it start off in the proposed budget. The Board questioned whether
the Recreation trailer was being used and how often it is moved.
B. Purchasing Policy – Tabled

The Board reviewed the updated draft of the Purchasing Policy and. Appendix for Credit Card
Users. Mr. Leclair recommended edits and discussed preferred payment methods.


C. Griffin Mill Bridge
Town Administrator Sterndale reported on the design change and that there should be more
forward motion. He noted that there may be a binder coat this year but the finish paving will
likely happen next year. He noted that his authorization to sign documents needed to be
updated.
Mr. Carroll motioned that Town Administrator Chris Sterndale is authorized to sign all
documents related to the Griffin Mill Bridge replacement project. Mr. Bedard seconded
the motion. A vote was taken, all were in favor, the motion passed unanimously.
D. Dartmouth Drive
Town Administrator Sterndale reported that they are going to the Planning Board on
Wednesday and will be looking for guidance from the Board of Selectmen on use and closure of
portions of the road while excavating and he is working with counsel on that. The Planning
Board will need to be sure the road work agreement includes overlay at the end and confirm
their travel path under 101 to the traffic circle to the state road and not use of Rockingham
Road. There will be regional impact with Hooksett and Manchester.

VII. Committee Reports

Mr. Carroll reported that Parks & Recreation finalized their proposed budget. Highway Safety
meets on Wednesday.

Mr. Leclair noted that the Budget Committee will be responsible for calculating the default
budgets.


                                                2
VIII. Town Administrator Updates

Town Administrator Sterndale provided the Board with an except from the Town Crier which
reviewed the success of this year’s Auburn Day festivities and parade and thanked the
Historical Association volunteers.
The Crier recognized the efforts of election officials and volunteers were appreciated in the
primary. Mr. Sterndale questioned whether election booths were all in good condition or if some
could be replaced with tables.
Mr. Sterndale reported that volunteers are sought for service on the ZBA. The Town is seeking
plowing contractors for the upcoming season. Electronic waste drop off is Saturday, October 10
from 9 AM to 2 PM at the Safety Complex.

IX. Minutes

A. August 31, 2026 Public & Non-Public

Mr. Carroll motioned to approve the August 31, 2026 public and non-public meeting
minutes. Mr. Bedard seconded the motion. A vote was taken, all were in favor, the
motion passed unanimously.

B. Consideration of unsealing non-public minutes: Dec 2, Dec 4, Dec 30, 2013

Mr. Carroll motioned to unseal the non-public minutes of December 2, 2013, December 4,
2013 and December 30, 2013. Mr. Bedard seconded the motion. A vote was taken, all
were in favor, the motion passed unanimously.

X. NON-PUBLIC SESSION 91-A:3(II) if necessary

XI. Adjournment
Mr. Carroll motioned to adjourn the meeting at 7:45 PM. Mr. Bedard seconded the
motion. A vote was taken, all were in favor, the motion passed unanimously.

Respectfully submitted,
Nancy Hoijer, Recording Secretary
