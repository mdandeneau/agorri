A. F&D Development, LLC
   Planning Board Case #26-04
   Frank Delucia, Owner
   Dartmouth Drive
   Tax Map 6, Lot 18-7 , 18-8, 18-9
   Minor Site Plan Review & Excavation Permit

Frank Delucia and Rocky Delucia presented the application together with Pat McLaughlin, Civil
Engineer from GPI. Mr. McLaughlin described the three lots and 18-acre disturbance area. He
described the roadway behind the barriers as not a public way. He noted it was undevelopable
unless brought to a reasonable site grade. He displayed a map of the three lots. He noted that

state Alteration of Terrain (AoT) has been applied for and he will work with them and Dan Tatem
at Stantec on comments, prior to December.

Mr. Delucia described the phasing of self-contained five-acre disturbance areas and that they
would loam and seed after each but wanted a waiver for the white pine plantings until final
restoration. He described grading intent, phase limits, ongoing stabilization and erosion control.

Mr. Delucia asked the Board about intervals of updates, blasting and groundwater monitoring.

Mr. Prolman indicated the application was complete for review purposes. He noted that the Board

Selectman Bedard commented on the wording from the Hooksett comments concerning the five-
acre limit across all three. Hooksett Fire requested 24-hour notification for blasting. Vice-Chair
Dross indicated that Hooksett asked for notice one hour ahead, not just the day of and she feels
that is excessive, just notice that blasting will be on that day is reasonable. Mr. Delucia stated he
would send a letter and let them know how it would go. Manchester had no comments.

Mr. Prolman asked if the Board would like to act on the waiver requiring restoration plantings of
white pine saplings. Mr. Prolman noted he and Stantec were okay with it. He provided comments
from SNHPC dated September 14, 2026, September 15, 2026 comments from Stantec and
September 14, 2026 comments from the Town of Hooksett.

Vice-Chair Dross asked about the truck route. Mr. Delucia indicated it would go under the bridge
and turn right. Mrs. Marzloff asked if that were written anywhere.

Lynn G. of 201 Rockingham Road asked about her street and Mr. Delucia stated they are not
going down Rockingham Road.

Dan Cronin of 15 Dartmouth Drive asked how long and the start and end.

Selectman Bedard asked how often there would be updates. Mr. Prolman noted the Board would
be copied on inspections. Rene LaBranche of Stantec noted he can submit inspection reports to
the Board.

Mrs. Marzloff asked if there were a turnaround, a legal one, at the end of the road. Mr. Rolfe
noted it was approved. Mrs. Marzloff noted it had expired. Mr. Prolman explained that the Board
of Selectmen are working on an ordinance to allow utilization of the Class VI Road and provided
a handout.

Vice-Chair Dross asked about discussions with the fire department concerning a Knox box, and
about road standard conditions. Mr. Prolman noted that the reconstruction condition will be
different. Mrs. Marzloff asked if a bond would be posted for the non Class VI road. Mr. Prolman

B. Nana’s Kitchen
   Planning Board Case #26-03
   Tax Map 6, Lot 18-3
   Site Plan Amendment

Mike Malynowski, civil engineer, with Allen & Major Associates presented the site plan
amendment for the applicant. He described the proposed seasonal outdoor space and the
elimination of 12 parking spaces. He noted a reduction of impervious surfaces. He described the
angled parking and one way travel and cross walk to the neighboring parcel. The applicant is
working on septic approval and that could be made a condition of approval. There are three waiver
requests. He noted that the applicant owns the abutting parcel and will have shared parking in
addition to what is required.

Mr. LaBranche indicated no objection, it is not affecting the lot site.

Kent Burn, architect, displayed the plan showing 12 seats with firepits and covered pavilion with
26 seats and bar.

Mr. Bedient questioned waivers #2 and 3. Mr. Prolman indicated he had no issue.

Mr. Prolman noted conditions will be NH DES approval and town counsel approval of the shared
parking agreement. Mrs. Marzloff asked if the agreement would be recorded. Mr. LaBranche
noted the application is not dependent on those spaces, they are extra. Mr. Prolman provided
comments from Stantec dated September 15, 2026, copy of parking license agreement and
waiver request letter from CGI.

C. Master Halco
   Planning Board Case #26-10
   6 Beaver Brook Road
   Tax Map 1, Lot 24
   Major Site Plan Review

Chair Porter activated Alternate Paula Marzloff for this application.

Mr. Prolman noted the application was complete for review purposes.

 Chair Porter motioned to accept the application as complete for review purposes. Mrs.
 Marzloff seconded the motion. A vote was taken, all were in favor, the motion passed
 unanimously.


Mr. Prolman provided comments from Stantec dated September 15, 2026.

Mr. Butler presented the application for conditional approval for the major site plan. He posted
the proposed plan and noted AoT permit number. He noted two buildings would replace four and
that one building would be used in the front during construction. He described the variance for
within the 125’ wetland buffer. He noted a new set of review comments were open. He described
storm control measures.

Mr. Butler described the main warehouse, pole barn and new driveway which he noted met the
150’ regulation for the intersection being 200’ away. He described the one way in and one way
out. He showed the existing entrance, drainage and grading. He noted there are no existing
stormwater control measurers on site and they will bring it up to state standards and treat the
runoff to the wetland. He described swales and bioretention ponds and water quality swale. He
noted the 125,000 SF of disturbance and private well on site. He described the fire protection
components once final design is complete.

Bill Frisella of Northpoint discussed the fire protection system and noted that he met with Chief
Williams to discuss the 40,000-gallon cistern with pump house proposed. There will be an
agreement with the town so that the fire department can trip it for on- and off-site use. If the town
uses it, the town will fill the tank. The entire facility will be sprinkled. There will be 2,200 SF of
office space and fabrication and storage area in a pre-engineered building. The roof will slope to
Cohas Brook.

Mr. Porter questioned the two driveways; he asked if they were reviewed by Auburn Police and

Mr. LaBranche noted other than the driveway he had nothing significant, just housekeeping, the

Vice-Chair Dross asked about the if things could be pushed down further. The generator itself

Mr. Prolman will provide contact information for the police department and recommends the fire
