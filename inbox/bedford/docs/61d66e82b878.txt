IV. Public Hearing:

In accordance with RSA 36-A, the Bedford Conservation Commission will hear comments on the
expenditure of up to $335,950.00 from the Conservation Fund for the purchase of 2.4 acres of
land owned by the Marcia A. Marston Trust, located at 555 Wallace Road, Bedford, Tax Map 3,
Lot 2. The Commission will also authorize the use of the Conservation Fund for the completion of

investigations and studies associated with the land purchase including but not limited to
environmental reports, title review, and deed recordings.

Ms. Ports overviewed the proposal stating should the Commission approve the expenditure this
evening, the recommendation will then go to the Town Council to authorize the expenditure of
conservation funds, and then we would start our due diligence process and we are targeting a
closing of December 1st, so this will be pretty quick.

The property is at 555 Wallace Road, which is almost to the border with Goffstown. The Marcia
Marston Trust owns about 60.8 acres maybe. There's two lots with each having a residence and
then the remainder of the acres of the land is all vacant. The property is valuable because of its
proximity to the Marston Dunlap Conservation Area across the street that's owned by the town.
There's the Marston Dunlap Wildlife Refuge to the north, which is owned by the Piscataqua Land
Conservancy. And then the area is surrounded by considerable open space and trails, potentially
even connecting all the way over to the Florence Tar property, which is in Bedford as well.

The purchase and sale agreement includes a memorandum of understanding with some additional
assurances, including a trail lease agreement, which would open up the rest of the lands to public
access after we acquire the 2.4 acres. So the land will remain in the trust for now, but Piscataqua
Land Conservancy and the town will be granted permission to start to develop our trail system.

Our current fund is $2 million. We have about $400,000 in outstanding receipts for current use
taxes for a total of $2.4 million. We have about $682,000 of committed expenses that includes the
Acorn acquisition which just closed last week on Thursday. So we now own the Acorn property.
Our purchase price is $335,950. We need to have a phase one assessment completed. I'm
estimating about $20,000 for legal fees, hopefully less, and title insurance and closing costs, about
$5,000 again, hopefully less. Title insurance on the last one was only about $1,500, so that was
good. And the survey has already been completed, so we don't have that expenditure. So tonight
we will be authorizing the expenditure of the $335,950 and then up to an additional $30,000 for
closing costs. And then we would, assuming the acquisition is successful, we would have a balance
of about $1.4 million.

I'm happy to answer questions or go through anything in the staff report if there's any other
information you guys would like.
