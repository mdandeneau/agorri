Title: Agenda: September 22, 2026 Conservation Commission - PUBLIC HEARING
Fetched: 2026-11-01

Legal Notice
                                         Town of Bedford
                                     Conservation Commission


In accordance with RSA 36-A, the Bedford Conservation Commission will hold a public hearing
at 7:00 pm on Tuesday, September 22, 2026 in the Bedford Meeting Room at BCTV, 10
Meetinghouse Road, Bedford, NH, to hear comments on the expenditure of up to $335,950 from
the Conservation Fund for the purchase of 2.4 acres of land owned by the Marcia A. Marston Trust,
located at 555 Wallace Road, Bedford, Tax Map 3, Lot 2. The Commission will also authorize the
use of the Conservation Fund for the completion of investigations and studies associated with the
land purchase including but not limited to environmental reports, title review, and deed recordings.


Any person requiring reasonable accommodation to participate in the hearing, please call Christine Szostak at 792-
1304 at least 72 hours in advance.
