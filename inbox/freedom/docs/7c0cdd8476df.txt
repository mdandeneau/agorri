Town of Freedom
                                  Selectmen’s Meeting
                                Monday, October 5, 2026
Present: Selectmen Les Babb, Alan Fall, and Gary Williams; Stacy Bolduc, recording
secretary. The meeting was called to order at 6:50 p.m. following the Regional Ambulance
meeting at Town Hall, 16 Elm Street.

Absent: Chief Cunio
Department Heads Present: Chief Laferriere, Scott Brooks, Alan Libby, Kyle Thomas
Public Present: Sue Hoople and Jen Ulrich
Municipal Property and Priority Repairs: The Board reviewed property and repair
priorities, including a potential road or right-of-way issue, roof repairs, and coordination
with Kyle and other staff before the following week.

Department Head Updates:

Kyle Thomas, Municipal Facilities Maintenance: Kyle will shut off the water at the
Masonic Lodge before freezing weather and coordinate with the Police Department to paint
its office at the Public Safety Building.

Highway Department: Scott reported that storm cleanup continues on Rice Hill. Winter
sand is stocked, and the plows and sanders are ready. Rocks are exposed on Pauli Point
Road, and the Haverhill Street layout must be reevaluated for widening for plowing. The
Morton Salt contract has not yet been received.

Les asked whether Alan F. had reviewed the list of urgent Town building repairs. Alan had
not.
Motion: Les moved that Alan F. coordinate property maintenance with Kyle. Gary
seconded. Motion carried.

Priorities include replacing the roof over the Town Hall furnace room and replacing roof
screws at the Masonic Lodge and Highway Garage.

Scott reported that most roads have been graded.

Transfer Station: Alan L. reported that the Transfer Station will be closed on Columbus
Day and open during regular hours on Tuesday, October 13. Mount Carberry
communicated trash and construction-debris disposal rates will increase November 1,
along with additional separation requirements for construction debris.

Police: Chief Laferriere reported that Alan Libby assisted with cleaning the sally port and
that the older six-wheeler is operational.

Office: Stacy requested authorization to release infrastructure funds for gravel and
aggregates. Scott reported that Colemans will continue hauling through November. The
Board tabled the request until all invoices are received.
School Board Coordination: The Board agreed to attend an upcoming School Board
meeting together after completing its own research. The public officials meeting will be
moved to October 26 to accommodate the discussion.
Tax Rate Planning: Stacy reported that the process remains on schedule, tentatively
targeting Wednesday during the week of October 19, with the following week as a backup.
Stacy reported that Rod the assessor has no PILOT agreements in the communities he
serves and considers the proposed depreciation schedule reasonable.

Public Comment-
Sue Hoople asked whether the Board had further considered metering to address potential
septic overflows and questionable septic designs at Wabanaki. Les stated that the Board
remains focused on stormwater management. Sue noted that the issues should be
consolidated before Wabanaki appears before the Planning Board to avoid delays. She also
expected the application to have been heard in March because the agreement was signed
in January. Sue will raise these questions with the Planning Board. Les noted that Sue’s
remarks were received as public comment only because Mr. Salvati had requested to be
present for any discussion concerning Wabanaki.

Gary made a motion to accept the minutes of 9/28/26 with the following correction:
Under Masonic Hall replace “inspecting the wiring under the eaves” to “inspecting all
the wiring” Alan seconded the motion; Motion passed unanimously.
