Town of Freedom
                                  Selectmen’s Meeting
                             Monday, September 21, 2026
Present: Selectmen Les Babb, Alan Fall, and Gary Williams; Stacy Bolduc, recording
secretary. The meeting was called to order at 6:30 p.m. at Town Hall, 33 Old Portland Road.

Absent: Chief Cunio

Department Heads Present: Chief Cunio, Chief Laferriere, Brian Fontain, and Kyle Thomas.

Public Present: None

Trust Fund Reimbursements: The Board approved withdrawals of $28,325 from the Public
Library trust fund for roof replacement and $4,475.79 from the Fire Department Equipment
CRF for HSE Fire/Safety Equipment. The Board also noted receipt of approximately $35,000
in FEMA grant funds.


Les moved to withdraw $4,475.79 from the Fire Department Equipment CRF for the
unexpected HSE Fire/Safety Equipment expense. Gary seconded. Motion carried.

Les moved to withdraw $28,325 from the Freedom Public Library ETF for the library roof
replacement. Gary seconded. Motion carried.

Minutes: Alan moved to approve September 14, 2026, public and nonpublic minutes as
written. Gary seconded. Motion carried unanimously.

Department Head Updates:

Zoning— Brian reported that permit volume is 50 below the same point last year and
revenue was down $2,000.

Police— Chief Laferriere asked whether approximately $115,000 in salary savings from an
unfilled position could fund equipment or vehicles. He also discussed future staffing and
cruiser and tactical-equipment deployment. The Board agreed that a new vehicle should
be proposed by warrant article and considered returning unused funds to fund balance
while separately evaluating a speed trailer. Clayton presented the department’s new rifle
and thermal equipment and discussed potential night-vision grants. The spike strip policy will
return to the agenda next week.

Fire— Chief Cunio reported 19 medical calls, one motor-vehicle accident, three fire
alarms, two service calls, and one police standby. He also reported that a second Zodiac
boat trailer with a smaller motor could support ice rescue and serve as a backup; vehicle
inspections and servicing have begun; Patrick and Heather Cunio participated in the
September 11 stair climb in New Jersey; and preparations have begun for the burn at 50
Eaton Road. Les requested interior photographs before the burn.

Hired Truck Rate: The Board approved a temporary $10-per-hour increase for hired trucks
that supply their own fuel, effective through December 31, 2026. The rate will be reviewed
in January 2027 after confirmation of the State rate. Stacy will update the truck contracts.
The increase applies only to hired trucks.

Town Property Gift: The Board deferred action on Noreen Sullivan’s proposed 4,000-
square-foot property gift pending Scott’s review of potential municipal use by the Highway
Department.

Town Office- Stacy reviewed the correspondence:
   • State emergency messaging options and the police department’s new Facebook
      page as additional channels for communicating road closures, accidents, severe
      weather, and other urgent information to residents.
   • Camp Cody donated $5,000 to the rec department. Jen will write a thank you letter.
   • State Education Property Tax
   • NHMA updates.
   • Spike Strip Policy will be on the agenda next week to accept.
   • Website proposals: The Board will review the Madison, Eaton, Jackson, Northwood,
      and Gilford town websites for preferred layout features.
   • Public Officials meeting will be on October 19th.
   • Stacy and Nicole are in training Thursday the 24th.
   • Les asked Stacy to get town reports from Ossipee, Tamworth, Effingham and
      Madison.
   • Updating the transfer station ordinance is on hold.
   • Stacy will send Linda’s notes on the Employee Manual.

Town Hall Painting: Stacy will contact the contractor because the work may not be
completed by September 30.
