Town of Freedom
                                  Selectmen’s Meeting
                             Monday, September 14, 2026
Present: Selectmen Les Babb, Alan Fall, and Gary Williams; Stacy Bolduc, recording
secretary. The meeting was called to order at 6:30 p.m. at Town Hall, 33 Old Portland Road.

Absent: Chief Cunio

Department Heads Present: Scott Brooks, Chief Laferriere, Alan Libby, Brian Fontain, and
Kyle Thomas.

Public Present: Sue Hoople, Jen Ulrich, Brian Taylor, Nicole Riccarrdi, Libby Priebe, Darwin
Moulton, Linda Mailhot, and Jason Earle.

Minutes: Alan moved to approve August 31, 2026, minutes as written. Gary seconded
the motion, which passed unanimously.

Department Head Updates:

Transfer Station—Alan L. reported that more than 15,000 vehicles have used the transfer
station since May. The Board will review proposed ordinance changes before authorizing
the required seven-day public-hearing notice. The sharps box remains unused.

Police—Chief Laferriere presented a $6,000 estimate to paint the police station. Les Babb
recommended placing the expense in next year’s budget. New rifles are expected this
week. The Chief also discussed staffing needs related to employee burnout and Freedom’s
population growth.

Zoning Officer—Brian Fontain reported difficulty listing the municipal vehicle on
Municibid. The Board agreed to try for one more week and, if unsuccessful, advertise the
vehicle in the newspaper. Les deferred participation because he intends to bid.

Bookkeeper—Linda Mailhot reported that DES paid the first-quarter New Hampshire
surcharge. The second-quarter submission is due September 28.

Road Agent—Scott reported that 1,300 yards of sand were screened at Watts Pit, with
additional material potentially available next year.

Fire Department—Kyle Thomas reported completion of the annual fire-alarm inspections
and Town Hall septic pumping.

Town Office—Stacy reported that the Eaton contract was ready for signature; hazard-
mitigation and Joint Loss Committee meetings were scheduled for September 15; the
office had transitioned to Nextiva phones; and she and Nicole would attend budget training
on September 24 and 30.

Town Clerk—Libby reported receiving Goodhue Boat Company’s Utility Dealership
Application to sell Smoker craft boat trailers. The Board discussed missing documentation,
ownership information, and possible municipal concerns.
Libby also reported that her computer was replaced and that she was adjusting to the new
phone system.

Municipal Buildings: Les cited the cost of proposed projects, including Ossipee Lake
Road and any future costs that FEMA will not cover, and recommended assessing all town-
owned buildings before setting priorities. He opposed opening the foyer floor before related
structural work and recommended review in the spring.
Alan F. reported that Jason Earle considered the floor stable under current use. Jason
recommended temporary shoring that could be done after the November election and a
spring evaluation. Alan F. proposed a March warrant article for permanent repairs.
The Board scheduled a town-building walkthrough for Wednesday at 7:00 a.m.; Kyle will
attend.
Scott reported that the Highway Garage electrical panel and metal doorframes require
replacement.
Fuel Bids and Interdepartmental Contracts: The Board reviewed sealed and emailed fuel
bids; see attached.
Gary moved to award the fuel contract to C.N. Brown. Alan F. seconded the motion,
which passed unanimously.
Nonconforming Lots: Brian Taylor asked about small lots created by historical road
changes. Officials stated that private owners cannot be required to merge them and
summarized the Town’s prior tax-deed process.
Short-Term Rentals: Brian reported few complaints and identified online listings. Officials
recommended addressing verified nuisance or safety violations individually rather than
adopting broad enforcement.
