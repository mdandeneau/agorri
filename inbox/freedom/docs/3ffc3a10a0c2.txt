Town of Freedom
                                  Selectmen’s Meeting
                               Monday, October 19, 2026
Present: Selectmen Les Babb, Alan Fall, and Gary Williams; Stacy Bolduc, recording
secretary. The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m. following the School Board meeting
at the Freedom Elementary School located at 40 Loon Lake Rd.

Absent: Chief Cunio, Chief Laferriere
Department Heads Present: Scott Brooks and Alan Libby
Public Present: None

Don Johnson reviewed election staffing, bipartisan appointments, ballot-processing
procedures, and oversight for the November 3 election. Don and Libby will meet with the
ballot clerks, also serving as inspectors of election, on November 1 at 1:00 p.m. to review
procedures. The Deputy Town Clerks will oversee the exit checklist.

The six ballot clerks will be Jane Davidson, Michelle Keenan, Sara Butterfield, Rick
Davidson, Kim Reese, and Diana Talkington, representing a bipartisan balance of
registered Democrats and Republicans.
Diana Talkington, a registered Democrat appointed by the Democratic State Committee,
and Michelle Keenan, a registered Republican, will assist Don with processing absentee
ballots.

Kali Eldridge and Jo Williams, both registered Republicans, will serve as Deputy Town
Clerks.

Les made a motion to appoint Jane Davidson, Michelle Keenan, Sara Butterfield, Rick
Davidson and Kim Reese as ballot clerks (inspectors of the election) representing a
bipartisan balance of both registered democrats and republicans, motion seconded
by Gary; Motion passed unanimously.

Alan F. stated that Kyle Thomas will be his proxy at the election until he can get there.

Volunteers, including student council members, will help Kyle set up the polling tables at
the Elementary School. On Tuesday, the day of the election, election workers will arrive at
7:00 am to prepare for an 8:00 am opening. Polls will close at 7:00 pm at which time the
space will be set up for ballot counting and a sandwich supper will be provided in another
room. The food pantry will be soliciting food donations in another room separate from
where the polls are.

The polling place will be reset up for the school to hold a mock election on Wednesday;
Don will be asking for volunteers to help reset the polling stations.

Department Head Update

Transfer Station: Alan L. reported that 66 households participated in Hazardous Waste
Day, at a cost of $3,025.56. Separating wood from other construction-and-demolition
waste eliminated one container shipment and saved $1,200. The wood will be shipped
separately for chipping at the solid waste facility.

FEMA Disaster Reimbursement: Scott reported that FEMA reimbursement work is
ongoing. Required next steps include a call with a FEMA representative, documentation of
permanent projects, and calculation of eligible costs before funding is finalized.

Highway Department
Scott reported that work on Rice Hill is complete. Dirt-road work on Young’s Hill is
complete but work on the paving end remains. Additional work is needed on Scarboro Road
and the west side of Wintergreen Ridge. Projects to consider for a future warrant articles
include:

   •   Huckins Road chip sealing
   •   Culvert and header replacement on Rice and Swett’s Hill
   •   Cragged Mountain culvert

Infrastructure Funding Strategy: The Board discussed allocating available and future
funds to road and infrastructure projects, including setting aside at least $500,000
annually to reduce reliance on borrowing.

Municipal Asset Bids: The Town reviewed public bids for a truck and a car, confirmed the
Monday, October 26 deadline, and scheduled the bid opening for the conclusion of the
public officials meeting.
