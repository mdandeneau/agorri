2. Continued
   Subdivision of Land, Conditional Use Permit
   Moulton Avenue (Map R9, Lot 8K-1: Residential)
   Owner/Applicant: Michael Gill
   The owner/applicant is proposing to subdivide a 39.12 acre parcel into 10 residential lots. The
   proposed roadway will be approximately 1,371 linear feet in length. The project includes approximately
   1,528 square feet of impact within the 25-foot to 50-foot Limited Cut Buffer. No direct impact to the
   wetlands is proposed.

3. New
   Site Plan Review
   1460 Greenland Road (Map R21, Lot 44B: Commercial District C, RCIM District)
   Owner/Applicant: New England Development
   The owner/applicant is proposing two retail spaces in the area initially approved for a 70,500 square
   foot grocery store. One space will be approximately 49,000 square feet and the other is approximately
   11,000 square feet, for a total area of approximately 60,000 square feet.

   required in the RCIM District to allow the Motor Vehicle Sales/Leasing use.
