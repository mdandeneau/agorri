DRAFT MINUTES

       Monday, Sept 14, 2026


   Meeting called to order at 6:35 p.m. by Kathleen​
   Roll call: Jean Eno, Bill Bilodeau, Cindy Hoohout, Kathleen Babin-Johnson (Ch), Scott Estey​
   Absent: Chip Hussey (called in at roughly 7:45), Alice Passer​


1.​ Moulton Ave​
    Michael Gill was present with Stephen Haight, PE, from Civilworks New England seeking a
    positive recommendation from the CC for a conditional use (CU) permit for 1500 sq. ft. of
    wetland buffer impact that the applicant will be seeking from the planning board (PB) for a
    public road as part of a subdevelopment project off of Moulton Ave. Several CC members
    (no quorum) previously walked the area with the applicant and engineer. The area of note
    will see no direct wetland impact; the CU is strictly for impact to a portion of the buffer zone
    for grading a bioretention pond at the end of a proposed cul de sac. Engineer Haight stated
    no stormwater would flow into the buffer beyond the detention pond. The plans follow all
    Greenland regulations and the HOA that will be associated with the subdivision will maintain
    all stormwater and the detention pond. The applicant expects to be back to the PB in early
    October. Jean motioned for a positive recommendation for the CU permit. Cindy seconded.
    Bill abstained. All others in favor.​

2.​ Approval of July and Aug Minutes​
    Cindy moved to accept the July Minutes, Kathleen seconded. All in favor. Scott moved to
    accept the August Minutes, Kathleen seconded. All in favor.​

3.​ Quarterly Newsletter​
    Members discussed specifics for a fall newsletter regarding leaves, organisms that depend
    on overwintering in leaves, and how detrimental mainstream methods are regarding general
    cleanup. Scott offered to draft an outline with published references and have it to the rest of
    the board in time for emailing no later than September 30th.​

4.​ Strategic Plan​
    Once again discussion revolved around the who’s, what’s and when’s for generating a
    strategic plan. Jean laid out what she saw as obstacles, method of communication being a
    biggie. Scott’s input included the issue of having no repository for working documents and
    suggested using Google Drive as a place where all can access and comment on working
    documents. Kathleen suggested going back to the Town Admin to see why such a method
    cannot be utilized, as now the town has its own server. Scott offered to seek what the Town
    uses and whether the CC can use the same. ​

5.​ Thompson Brook​
    Chip called in to discuss last minute Thompson Brook expenditure approvals. There is a
    shortfall of $172 from what had been approved on the warrant article. Chip suggested two
   ways to approach the shortfall: either hold off on the weed barrier cost ($200), or ask the
   Selectmen if they can cover the shortfall. Cindy cllarified we need to approve Chip going to
   the Selectmen to ask that they expend funds from the [2024?] warrant article to cover the
   costs of the Thompson Brook Trail Update addendum proposed by Chip, and that the weed
   barrier fee can be removed if they will not cover it. Scott asked if the motion is to expend
   $1866…? Cindy further clarified that it is for $1866 plus the $172 shortfall. The shortfall
   equation = the balance of the project (+$317.50) minus contracted mowing costs ($450) and
   material costs for repairing a trail bridge ($40), which leaves a negative of $172.50. Cindy
   motioned to ask the Selectboard to expend these funds to cover the shortfall as proposed on
   the Update. Kathleen seconded. Jean abstained. All others in favor.​

6.​ Old Business​
    Jean reviewed Lowe’s help for the next DoT roadside cleanup on October 7 beginning at
    9am in front of Lowe’s. Volunteers from Lowe’s and Target will handle the actual picking of
    trash and debris; Cindy, Kathleen and the Enos will use their personal trucks to collect the
    bagged trash (Jean will connect with the transfer station to cover that aspect) and act as
    route captains (distribute water, bathroom breaks, etc.). The entire 2-mile stretch will be
    covered and the clean-up is expected to end by noon. ​

7.​ Kathleen reported that Tara approved the requests for proposals (RFP) for the trash
    collected off of easement land. The CC’s budget is due. Cindy and Kathleen will review last
    year’s budget and replicate it for 2027.​

8.​ Adjournment​
    Bill motioned to adjourn at 8:10p.m. Cindy seconded. All in favor. ​
