Bob Dion opened the Planning Board meeting at 6:30 p.m. A roll call was taken. It was announced that a
quorum was present, and the meeting was being recorded and live streamed.

MOTION: M. Goodman moved to appoint M. Adams as a voting member for this meeting. Second – J.
McDevitt; all in favor. MOTION CARRIED

1. Town Center Zone Overlay District

The Planning Board debated whether to discontinue, revise or advance the Town Center Overlay District
proposal, weighing limited public attendance, infrastructure impacts, development flexibility, and
concerns from affected property owners. M. Wassell left during this portion of the meeting.

Public Feedback: Approximately 70 houses were notified, but only 10 residents attended the meeting on
Thursday, October 01st. J. McDevitt stated that another opportunity should be provided to address the
comments, while other members felt that residents directly affected had been given notice; the Board
should not assume the absent property owners supported or opposed the proposal.

Overlay Benefits and Costs: Board members who supported the Overlay District described the proposal
as a way to improve the appearance and availability of the Rt. 151/Rt. 33 entrance area. They also
acknowledged that current residents could continue ordinary additions under existing building codes.
Concerns included possible increases in emergency services and other infrastructure costs if additional
businesses or homes were built. Clear information about the potential impacts was requested.

Public Hearing Process: It was clarified that the work session on October 01st was not a public hearing. A
public hearing is required before the proposal can be moved forward to a Town vote. There was a
discussion whether a broader Town notification was necessary. Options included the Town website,
meeting notices, or using a less expensive Town-wide mailing process; no decision was reached.

Proposed Document Changes: The Board reviewed community comments that were included in their
information. Language was revised for lighting, signage, and sidewalk requirements to match the Site
Plan Review Regulations. The recommendation for the liberal use of glass in windows and doors was
removed.

MOTION: H. Droesch moved to add language to the Town Center Zone Overlay District proposal stating
that lighting, signage and sidewalk requirements are governed by the wording in the Site Plan Review
Regulations. Second – M. Goodman; all in favor. MOTION CARRIED

DISCUSSION: Public comments included sidewalks that connected across separate projects (no ‘sidewalks
to nowhere’). Members discussed adding that sidewalks be consistent with design, construction and
appearance in the Site Plan Review Regulations (Section V – Design and Construction Requirements,
Subsection 5.8 – Sidewalks), which calls for sidewalks at least 6 inches above grade, protected by curbing
and accessible to people with disabilities).

MOTION: A motion was made and seconded to strike the recommendation for the liberal use of glass
(Windows and Doors). All in favor. MOTION CARRIED

DISCUSSION: It was noted that it was only a recommendation and the Board could address glass when
individual buildings come before them.

The fencing provision regarding visibility of stored materials, business vehicles and exterior design
features was removed.

MOTION: M. Adams moved to strike the fencing provision reading ‘The use of fences should be to protect
the visibility of stored materials or business vehicles or create an exterior design feature that enhances
the overall look of the building’ (Fencing and Site Walls). Second – D. Moore; all in favor. MOTION
CARRIED

DISCUSSION: Members of the Board found the sentence to be unclear and noted that fencing was required
under other language in the document.

The Board also reviewed instances of ‘should’ and ‘to the extent possible’ in the proposed document.
Specific changes to ‘shall’ were made where requirements were intended; ‘should’ remained where
flexibility was needed.

MOTION: A motion was made and seconded to change ‘should’ to ‘shall’ where requirements were
intended, leaving ‘should’ where flexibility was needed. MOTION CARRIED

Building Footprint: The Board discussed whether the proposal’s zero open space reference and setbacks
could allow excessive building coverage on 10,000 square foot lots. A tentative 60% maximum footprint
motion was withdrawn; members preferred parcel specific analysis and more information about setbacks,
wetlands, parking and the effects on existing lots.

MOTION: A motion was made and withdrawn by H. Droesch to add a maximum building footprint, not to
exceed 60% of the lot area under Dimensional Criteria.

DISCUSSION: 60% was suggested by comparison with the Commercial District A (Zoning Ordinance Article
IV – Dimensional Requirements, Section 4.2 - Table of Dimensional Requirements, Item ‘j’, CA, CB, CC
Zone).

Next Overlay District Discussion: Further discussion was continued to the work session on Thursday,
December 03rd: document changes made at this meeting and the building footprint issue required review.

M. Fougere updated the proposed Zoning Ordinance amendments for Board review. The Board

subdivision, with related utilities and septic systems, was required by state law or intended for specific

to the work session on Thursday, December 03, 2026. Second – H. Droesch; all in favor. MOTION CARRIED
