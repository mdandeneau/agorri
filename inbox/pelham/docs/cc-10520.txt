  1)   Pelham School District Site Review - Starts at 8:00am outside of Pelham Elementary School

         Superintendent McGee provided the committee members in attendance with a packet that
         included an agenda overview, a copy of the CIP worksheet, and a report on a commercial
         assessment of the boiler for the school district. The group began with a review of a 1st floor
         classroom at PES where they discussed the agenda and plan for the morning and then
         discussed aspects of the room that were relevant for replacement in the next few years. This
         included furniture and air conditioning. They then visited the common area to discuss the
         need for furniture to support gathering and instruction in those areas. This is where the school
         hosts presenters/speakers when they come in to speak with classes. Finally, within the
         building they visited the gym which is also included as phase 3 of the AC project.

         Next, the committee visited the PES playground to discuss the condition of the equipment
         and play chips, particularly their depth in ‘landing areas’ or "high-impact areas". They then
         moved to Goyette Field, where Superintendent McGee pointed out the need to better care for

         the middle school soccer/baseball fields, citing concerns over injuries.  They stopped to
         check Harris Field and briefly discussed future plans to resurface the track. This was
         mentioned as being a number of years out, but it is important for the district to continue to
         point out as a larger looming expense.

         The group then moved to the athletic fields behind Pelham High School and discussed the
         need for new field lights. The project is not being taken on this year and the district noted that
         it has resulted in the cancellation of approximately 8 home night games throughout fall
         sports.
         Within PHS, the committee reviewed the older set of boilers near the gym/cafeteria and also
         the need to replace cafeteria round tables – moving away from plastic tables that warp and
         bend over time. Finally, at PHS the committee reviewed the parking lot with permeable and
         non-permeable concrete and discussed the plan to sealcoat the non-permeable areas.

         Note, Ms. Debbie Ryan and Mr. Michael Carter departed the group at this time and left the
         meeting.

         The committee continued on to PMS where the boiler, library (where they experienced a small
         fire last year), and a second classroom ventilator were reviewed.
