45    1. Pelham  Memorial  School Budget
46           Dr. Sarah Marandos presented several new budget requests for Pelham Memorial School. The
47           first item was a request to add a part-time math tutor without benefits to provide direct support
48           services to students performing below grade level in math. Dr. Marandos then highlighted a
49           textbook replacement purchase for a new English Language Arts curriculum called CKLA
50           Amplify, which the school is piloting in sixth grade. She explained that the program is paper-based
51                 rather than digital in an effort to be mindful of increased screen time for students. Vice
52           Chair Paul Grant asked about a kid blog subscription and whether it involved students actively
53           writing, to which Dr. Marandos confirmed it was a writing tool that allows students to publish their
54           work while meeting privacy requirements. She noted that the middle school had seen significant
55           increases in reading and writing scores this year using an approach called Think SRSD, which
56           encourages students to practice extensive writing through multiple mediums. Dr. Marandos also
57           drew attention to equipment and furniture requests for the middle school. She highlighted an
58           equipment purchase on page  18, noting that the middle school band program has experienced
59           increased enrollment to pre-COVID numbers, with approximately thirty percent of students
60           participating. Director of Finance Brian Krueger explained how the district spaces out equipment
61           replacements year to year and characterized the piano purchase as a one-time expense. Dr.
62           Marandos  then addressed additional furniture purchases, noting that several original requests
63           had been reduced at the superintendent level. She explained that the school sometimes
64           purchases flexible seating to support students who may need or benefit from non-traditional
65           seating arrangements. Mr. Krueger clarified the distinction between one-time equipment
66           purchases and recurring replacement cycles for furniture, which the district attempts to spread
67           evenly across budget years.

68    2. Pelham  High School  Budget
69           Dr. Sarah Marandos highlighted several staffing and programmatic changes at Pelham High
70           School. The district reassigned an English position to Pelham Elementary for grade one and
71           reduced a halftime French position due to declining enrollment, effectively eliminating the French
72           program as currently structured. While Spanish remains the primary world language offering
73           beginning in eighth grade, students have access to Virtual Learning Academy Charter School
74           (VLACS)  courses for additional languages. Vice Chair Paul Grant asked whether the district
75           would attempt to rehire a French teacher, and Dr. Marandos explained that enrollment numbers
76           do not justify the expense of an additional half-time position. Current French students will
77           complete their courses this year, with support offered through VLACS or transitions to Spanish.
78           Member  Bob Sherman   sought clarification on whether students would need to use VLACS to
79           fulfill foreign language requirements and who pays for such courses. Dr. Marandos noted that
80           VLACS  is a public charter school and that the district is working through logistics to provide
81           VLACS  support in-building for affected students. School Board Representative Greg Smith
82           commented  on the challenges of operating a relatively small school, noting difficulty both in
83           offering breadth of curriculum and in recruiting part-time teaching staff. Chair Michael Carter
84           observed that VLACS  classes, while requiring student self-discipline, can provide valuable
