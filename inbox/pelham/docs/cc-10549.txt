   3)    Discussion regarding perpetual care trust funds


                               • The Trustees discussed developing a repeatable process for disbursing funds from
                                 perpetual care trust funds. Following discussion, Trustee St. Germain made a motion,
                                 seconded by Trustee Soucy, to send the proposed draft memorandum, incorporating
                                 the modification suggested by Trustee St. Germain, to the Cemetery Director, with
                                 copies to the Town Administrator and Finance Director, for their review, feedback,
                                 and further discussion. Motion approved 3-0-0.
                               • Input from Rockland Trust will be sought in developing a repeatable annual
                                 disbursement process to confirm that the process is workable for Rockland Trust,
                                 including the updating of its records for individual perpetual care trust funds as annual
                                 disbursements are made, and to determine whether any additional fees would apply.
                               • Trustee Soucy discovered documentation indicating the Mary Lyons Perpetual Care
                                 Trust Fund should be segregated from the 1,000+ perpetual care trust funds because
                                 its funds are designated for the Lyons portion of the Coburn/Lyons Cemetery, rather
                                 than solely for the Mary Lyons gravesite. Trustee Soucy made a motion to segregate
                                 the Mary Lyons Perpetual Care Trust Fund from the 1,000+ perpetual care funds and
                                 establish it as a separate line item on the MS-9 report. Trustee St. Germain seconded
                                 the motion. Approved 3-0-0.
                               • The Currier, Raymond, and Koehler/Cahill trust fund dates need correction based on
                                 the town reports from 1943 through 1966. Trustee Soucy made a motion to correct
                                 the dates in the MS-9 (Rockland Trust action item) and trust origin cover sheets
                                 based on the historical records found, seconded by Trustee Germain.

                                 Motion Approved 3-0-0.

                                          • Correct the date for Henry Currier Trust from 1966 to 10/17/1943.
                                          • Correct the date for E & A Raymond Trust from 1956 to 10/2/1953.
                                          • Correct the date for Koehler/Cahill Trust from 1966 to 9/16/1957.
