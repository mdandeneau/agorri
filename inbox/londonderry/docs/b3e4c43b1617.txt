E. NEW BUSINESS
1. Discuss and approve an expenditure from the Cable Equipment Capital Reserve Fund
to the Londonderry School District in the amount of $8,000.
Controller Sarah Jardim-Lee presented Order 2026-19 regarding the annual withdrawal from the
Cable Equipment Capital Reserve Fund. Under the franchise agreement with Comcast, the town
receives $32,500 annually for PEG access support, of which $8,000 is allocated to the
Londonderry School District for educational television programming and studio equipment
(including hard drives, microphones, and streaming headsets).
   Vice Chair Faber moved that the Londonderry Town Council hereby approves the withdrawal request
   and directs the Town Treasurer to disburse $8,000.00 from the Cable Equipment Capital Reserve Fund
   to the Londonderry School District for educational programming equipment. Seconded by Councilor
   Paul. Motion carried 4-0-0. Chair votes in the affirmative.

4. Receive a presentation from the Department of Public Works and Weston & Sampson
regarding Municipal Separate Storm Sewer System (MS4) stormwater requirements.
Deputy Director of Public Works James Danis and Jaurice Schwartz from Weston & Sampson
delivered a detailed presentation on Londonderry's Municipal Separate Storm Sewer System
(MS4) permit compliance and surface water quality requirements under EPA regulations. The
presentation covered the six Minimum Control Measures (MCMs), ongoing catch basin cleaning
and street sweeping programs, illicit discharge detection and elimination (IDDE) protocols,
stormwater pollution prevention plans (SWPPP) for municipal facilities, and chloride reduction
strategies. Discussion followed regarding catch basin cleaning equipment efficiency, maintenance
costs, and budget tracking.

5. Receive a fiscal update for Fiscal Year 2026 year-end results and the unaudited first
quarter of Fiscal Year 2027.
Finance Director Justin Campo presented the unaudited financial status for Fiscal Year 2026
year-end and the first quarter of Fiscal Year 2027. For FY2026, total municipal expenditures and
encumbrances totaled $45,076,231 against a budgeted appropriation of $45,269,995, ending the
fiscal year $193,764 under budget (99.57% spent). For FY2027 Q1 (July 1 – September 30, 2026),
departmental expenditures totaled $12,148,212 against a budgeted appropriation of
$48,217,746 (25.19% spent, exactly tracking the 25.21% benchmark). In addition, ongoing water
expansion projects received $1,500,000 in state grant funds and $1,565,806.79 from the state
PFAS Remediation Loan Fund.

6. Review the initial drafts of projected warrant articles for the 2027 Town Meeting.
Finance Director Justin Campo and Acting Town Manager Kim Bernard presented a preliminary
overview of draft warrant articles for the March 2027 Town Election (FY2028). Key draft articles
include: Warrant Article #2 (Leach Library Renovation Bond for $1,418,117 with first-year debt
service of $209,173); Warrant Article #5 (IAFF Local 3160 Collective Bargaining Agreement);
Warrant Article #6 (Teamsters Local 633 Library CBA); Warrant Article #8 (Establishing a Town-
Wide Communication Systems and Equipment Expendable Trust Fund for $300,000 from
Unassigned Fund Balance to eliminate radio dead zones); Expendable Maintenance Trust Fund
($800,000); Roadway Maintenance Expendable Trust Fund ($650,000); Fire Department
Equipment Capital Reserve ($600,000); Fire Truck Capital Reserve ($400,000); Information
Technology Capital Reserve ($350,000); Establish and Fund a Revolving Fund for Fire Alarm
Services; Leach Library Non-Capital Reserve ($20,000); Transportation Capital Reserve; and
Water System Capital Reserve ($200,000). Key statutory deadlines noted: January 8, 2027
(petition articles with bond), January 12, 2027 (standard petition articles), February 6, 2027
(Deliberative Session), and March 9, 2027 (Election Day).
