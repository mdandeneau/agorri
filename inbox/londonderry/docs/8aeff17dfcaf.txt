E. NEW BUSINESS
  1. Discuss and approve an expenditure from the Cable Equipment Capital Reserve Fund to the Londonderry School
     District in the amount of $8,000.

           (Controller Sarah Jardim- Lee)

           The Town receives $32,500 from Comcast each year as a franchise fee, which is deposited in to the Cable Capital
           Reserve Fund each year after the voters approve the relevant warrant article each March during the Town election. $8,000
           of that is for use by the Londonderry School District. This item removes the money from the Cable Capital Reserve Fund
           to pass on to the schools.


       Documents:

  2. POSTPONED TO NOVEMBER 2, 2026 Discuss and authorize the Town Manager to execute the Off -Site Improvement
     Agreement for the Procopio project.

           (Acting Town Manager Kim Bernard)

           Per the notice of decision issued by the Planning Board on April 8, 2026, the Town Council needs to approve an Off-Site
           Improvements Agreement for the new development proposed by Procopio at 36 and 44 Pillsbury Road. This Agreement
           describes the developers' obligations for improving public infrastructure to include roadway improvements, signage,
           pavement marking, and other traffic management measures.

  3. POSTPONED TO NOVEMBER 9, 2026 - Receive a presentation from the Department of Public Works regarding the
     Pavement Condition Index (PCI).

           (Deputy Director of Public Works James Danis)

           The Department of Public Works contracted with a company to complete a review of all the pavement in Londonderry to
           get an accurate representation of the current conditions. This presentation will describe the results and takeaways, as
           well as how the DPW will use this information moving forward.

  4. Receive a presentation from the Department of Public Works and Weston & Sampson regarding Municipal Separate
     Storm Sewer System (MS4) stormwater requirements.

           (Deputy Director of Public Works James Danis and Weston & Sampson)

           MS4 reporting is the Town ’s annual report to regulators showing what it has done to manage stormwater, reduce
           pollution, and protect local streams, ponds, and other waterways. It is required by the Environmental Protection Agency
           for communities covered by the federal MS4 stormwater permit, including municipalities in New Hampshire.
