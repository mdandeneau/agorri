E. NEW BUSINESS
1. Receive a High Range Road water main update.
Councilor Paul returned to the dais at 8:04 p.m.
Deputy Director of Public Works James Danis presented a comprehensive update on municipal
water and sewer projects. High Range Road water main installation is complete to Alexander
Drive with pressure testing and chlorination underway; paving and striping completed on
southern sections (Pillsbury to Route 102). Old Derry Road water main is complete and scheduled
for paving. South Road water main testing is ongoing. The first Saint Gobain water main extension
into neighborhoods will extend water main along Royal Lane, Imperial Lane, Tyler Drive, and
Colonial Drive. The Ross/Tokenal Drive EPA-directed superfund water line extension is moving
forward. On sewer projects, Pillsbury Road pump station wet well construction is starting,
Bancroft Rd gravity sewer is complete/tested, and Hardy Road force main testing is wrapping up
with winter shimming scheduled. Addressed property damage restoration along Hardy/South
Road construction corridors.
Administrative Services Director Kirsten Hildonen updated the Council on House Bill 1588
(effective September 13, 2026), which reformed state law governing Water Special Assessment
Districts. HB 1588 allows town governing bodies to initiate special assessment districts directly
with 60% resident assent, eliminating the requirement to track property assessed values. Council
consensus was granted to present the draft Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) on November 2,
2026.
Chair Dunn introduced Police Captain Patrick Cheetham, who recapped the 5th Annual Senior
Resource Expo held Saturday, October 3. The event featured over 70 vendors and approximately
400 attendees, supported by the Senior Resource Committee, ALERT volunteer team, local
business sponsors, and founding organizer Acting Town Manager Kim Bernard. Captain Cheetham
reaffirmed that the Londonderry Police Department stands fully behind Acting Town Manager
Bernard and assured residents that LPD operations remain seamless under Deputy Chief and
Captain leadership.

2. Discuss and schedule a Public Hearing for the proposed Zoning Ordinance
amendments.
Administrative Services Director Kirsten Hildonen presented proposed amendments to the
Londonderry Zoning Ordinance developed by the Southern New Hampshire Planning Commission

F. OLD BUSINESS
1. Discuss and approve the inter-municipal agreement between the Town and the
School District for the lease of the School District Office building addition to the Town
Hall.
School Board Chair Bob Slater, Board Member Tim Porter, and Superintendent Dan Black
presented the long-term intermunicipal lease agreement alongside Acting Town Manager Kim
Bernard for the School District Office addition at Town Hall. Discussion emphasized
intergovernmental collaboration, shared maintenance responsibilities, DPW snow removal and
salting for 70 new parking spaces, and establishing a safe pedestrian walkway for students
between the High School campus and Town Hall.
   Councilor Bouchard moved that the Londonderry Town Council hereby authorizes the Acting Town
   Manager to enter into the School District Office Intermunicipal Lease Agreement and to take all
   actions necessary to effectuate the agreement. Seconded by Councilor Combes. Motion carried 4-
   0-0. Chair votes in the affirmative.

H. OTHER BUSINESS
1. Liaison Reports

Councilor Ted Combes reported on the Manchester-Boston Regional Airport 10-year Master Plan
open house and announced a new direct flight from Manchester to Nashville, TN. Councilor Deb
Paul reported on Beautify Londonderry roadside cleanups and volunteer road sponsorships.
Councilor Dan Bouchard commended Leach Library staff for resilient operations out of LPD/Town
Hall spaces and commended DPW Director Dave Wholley for project management on the School
District addition

2. Town Manager Report
Acting Town Manager Kim Bernard presented an operational report highlighting fiscal
management, transparency, and departmental communication. Key updates included restricting
the Town Council distribution email to councilors only, committing to itemized legal billing in a
effort to accurately project legal expenses, and recommending the deferral of the police
department roof replacement project to protect Emergency Maintenance Trust Fund reserves.
Discussion followed between Councilors Paul, Bouchard, and Acting Town Manager Bernard
regarding notice of town emergency incidents (firefighter injury/structure fires), solar panel
rebate incentives associated with roof projects, and advance agenda material delivery.
