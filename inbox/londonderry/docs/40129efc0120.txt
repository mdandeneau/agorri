24    II.   New Business
25          A. Project Overview and Presentations - DPW
26
27              1. DPW CIP Presentation
28                 Mr. Danis the reconstruction plan for the lower section of Bancroft Road, which is
29                 the first project in the formalized road program.
30
31              2. Facility, Cemetery, and Fuel System
32                 Mr. Wholley reviewed the plan for decommissioning and removing the Central Fire
33                 fuel tank and building.
34
35                 Mr. Wholley reviewed the plan to replace the DPW facility, including removal of
36                 substandard buildings, improving working conditions, installing environmental
37                 controls, and housing/maintenance of equipment.
38
39                 Mr. Wholley noted the need to expand the cemetery and explained that a bid will
40                 be put out this winter to provide another 224 plots. Future buildouts are planned to
41                 complete the project.
42
43              3. Roads, Bridges, Corridors, and Intersections
44                 Mr. Danis reviewed the Hall Road bridge reconstruction project; this is a red-listed
45                 bridge. It will be funded 80% by the Municipal State Aid Bridge Program and 20%
46                 by the State Aid Block Grant Program.

47
48                 Mr. Danis reviewed the Stokes Road bridge project, which will most likely involve
49                 removal of the bridge. This will be paired with the NHDOT Route 28/Route 128
50                 intersection reconstruction in 2029. They will be looking for ARM funding for the
51                 wetlands restoration and bridge removal aspects. There should also be State Aid
52                 Block Grant Program funds remaining from the Hall Road bridge project.
53
54                 Mr. Danis reported the Traffic Management Working Group developed a list of 46
55                 substandard intersections, which has been narrowed to 17 critical locations. A Safe
56                 Streets 4 All grant was applied for, which would pay for a global study of these 17
57                 locations as well as critical corridors. Other corridor studies will be developer
58                 funded.
59
60                 Mr. Danis explained the Town received a $2 million Congressionally Directed
61                 Spending capped grant for the Pettengill Road corridor to identify projects and a
62                 path forward. The Town must match 20% of the grant funds for a total project cost
63                 of $2.5 million. The grant is time capped.
64
65                 Mr. Danis reviewed three Pettengill Road sewer improvement/expansion projects:
66                 Century Village, which will bring 344 units with substandard septic systems online
67                 to the sewer system; Commerce Park, a community development project that
68                 includes sewer extension; and South Londonderry Interceptor Phase II, which will
69                 address failing septic systems and install a pump station.
70
71              4. Fleet
72                 Mr. Wholley reviewed the truck replacement program and functions the new
73                 equipment would serve, highlighting the mini-excavator skid steer intended to
74                 primarily maintain Town stormwater drainage systems.
75
76              5. Water and Sewer
77                 Mr. Danis reviewed the water main expansion program, describing 13 sequenced
78                 projects extending 8" and 12" mains to 464 homes in areas with significant PFAS
79                 contamination issues. They plan to package projects to lower the costs and intend
80                 to leverage as many funding opportunities as possible.
81
