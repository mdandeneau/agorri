23    II.   New Business
24          A. CIP Committee Deliberations
25              1. Project Scoring, Prioritization, and Placement
26
27   Ms. Hildonen explained projects already in motion have been removed from the CIP. The
28   Committee questioned the status of projects slated for 2027. Ms. Hildonen said these are ongoing
29   projects, or items that need to be ordered in 2027 with funds being withdrawn later.
30
31   The Committee agreed on the need to balance the total amount for projects across years.
32
33   The Committee discussed moving either the DPW facility or the High School auditorium out a
34   year, to avoid an $80 million hit in 2029. Mr. Bernard noted that departments could work together
35   to respond to the Committee's prioritization of larger projects. Mr. Gray said pushing the start of
36   the high school wing reconstruction project to 2030 would not place the school in dire straits; also,
37   more work is needed on that project.
38
39   The Committee discussed the need to align removal of the Stokes Road bridge with the NHDOT's
40   reconfiguration of the Route 28/Route 128 intersection, and agreed it could be moved to 2028.
41   This will be marked for further discussion by the Planning Board.
42
43   The Committee agreed to combine all security access control systems into 2028, as they are an
44   immediate need. They also proposed removing $20,000 for the baseball/softball dugouts in 2028
45   and spreading it over the subsequent four years.
46

47   The Committee recommended changing the Nelson Road tennis courts restoration from an
48   essential to a critical need due to safety concerns, and keeping it as a two-year phased project.
49
50   The Committee noted that any items listed as critical were categorized in the correct year.
51
52   The Committee agreed on the following:
53    ERP and HRIS finance system should stay in 2028.
54    Fire apparatus needs to be ordered in 2027; it will be paid for at a future date.
55    Leach Library: Children's Room renovation will be completed over several years; a bond
56      warrant article will be presented for the Library renovation project in 2028.
57    The DPW facility will stay in 2029, which will most likely be a bond and possibly spread
58      across multiple years.
59    The Committee agreed to the purchase of a skid steer and mini-excavator in 2028, due to it
60      being an essential need.
61    The Committee agreed having the water project on the CIP shows a need, which will help
62      obtain grants. They agreed on the proposed schedule as presented.
63    Pillsbury Cemetery expansion should be moved to 2030, to wait for a plan and concrete
64      numbers.
65    The Committee discussed pushing the Central Fire fuel tank and building removal to 2030.
66      Mr. Bernard questioned whether this project made sense fiscally, and in terms of creating
67      additional work and possible safety issues by fueling vehicles offsite.
68    The School turf field and turf project will be moved to 2028.
69
70   Ms. Hildonen noted there is a public hearing on the CIP at the Planning Board on October 14th
71   and presented to the Town Council on November 2nd.
72
73   Ms. Burbidge made a motion to accept the summary of capital projects as presented in the
74   spreadsheet reviewed at this meeting. Councilor Paul seconded the motion. The motion
75   carried 4-0-0.
76
77   Mr. Gray will present the CIP to the School Board. The Committee volunteered Mr. Penta, in his
78   absence, to present it to the Planning Board on October 14th. Ms. Burbidge offered to present to
79   the Town Council on November 2nd.
