A. CALL TO ORDER
Chair Dunn called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.
Special Presentation & Employee Recognition: Dave Wholley (Director of Public Works) and the
Town Council recognized John Trottier, PE, Director of Engineering and Environmental Services,
upon his retirement after 25 years of service on September 25, 2026. Councilor Bouchard read
Resolution 2026-18 commending Mr. Trottier for his dedicated leadership and public service to
Londonderry. Mr. Trottier expressed his appreciation to the Council and citizens.
Special Action Regarding Town Manager: Vice Chair Faber presented a motion to remove Town
Manager Shaun Mulholland from office for cause pursuant to the Town Charter, citing open
defiance of Council directives, unauthorized disclosure of attorney-client privileged documents,
obstruction of an ongoing investigation, unapproved departmental reorganization, failure to
implement the Council-voted Veterans Tax Credit adjustment, and unauthorized legal
expenditures. Councilors Paul and Bouchard objected and requested Councilor's Privilege to
delay the vote. Chair Dunn ruled the request denied as the item pertained to an executive
personnel matter.
   Vice Chair Faber moved that the Londonderry Town Council hereby removes Shaun Mulholland from
   the position of Town Manager for cause, effective immediately. Seconded by Councilor Combes. Motion
   carried 3-2-0, with Councilors Paul and Bouchard voting in opposition. Chair votes in the affirmative.
   Councilor Bouchard moved to publicly release the written attorney-client privileged legal opinion
   received by the Council regarding the removal of the Town Manager for cause. Seconded by Councilor
   Combes. Motion carried 5-0-0. Chair votes in the affirmative.

D. PUBLIC HEARINGS
1. Receive public input, discuss, and approve the Conservation Commission’s purchase
of 144 Rear Wildwood Road (Map 003, Lot 099) for the protection of Black Brook
Conservation Commission Chair Marge Badois and Alternate Member Mike Speltz presented a
proposal to acquire 144 Rear Wildwood Road (Map 003, Lot 099) from owner Ken Soroco for
$23,000 using funds from the Open Space Fund. Mr. Speltz explained that acquiring the wetland
parcel permanently protects Black Brook water quality and continues an established conservation
corridor.
   Vice Chair Faber moved that the Londonderry Town Council hereby approves the purchase of the
   property located at Map 003, Lot 099 for $23,000 and authorizes the Acting Town Manager to
   execute all documents necessary to effectuate the purchase from the Open Space Fund. Seconded
   by Councilor Combes. Motion carried 5-0-0. Chair votes in the affirmative.
   Councilor Combes moved to close the public hearing. Seconded by Councilor Bouchard. Motion
   carried 5-0-0. Chair votes in the affirmative.

2. Receive public input, discuss, and approve the proposed revisions to the Londonderry
Zoning Ordinance (LZO) to incorporate the 2024 ICC International Building Codes as
amended by the State of NH.
Administrative Services Director Kirsten Hildonen and Chief Building Official Nick Codner
presented Ordinance 2026-14 to update LZO Sections 9.1 and 9.2, adopting the 2024
International Code Council (ICC) building codes as mandated by state legislation (HB 1159) while
streamlining local administrative permitting procedures.
   Councilor Combes moved to open the public hearing. Seconded by Vice Chair Faber. Motion carried
   5-0-0. Chair votes in the affirmative.
Chair Dunn asked for public comment; there was none.
   Vice Chair Faber moved that the Londonderry Town Council hereby approves Ordinance 2026-14
   amending sections 9.1 and 9.2 of the Londonderry Zoning Ordinance. Seconded by Councilor
   Combes. Motion carried 5-0-0. Chair votes in the affirmative.
   Councilor Combes moved to close the public hearing. Seconded by Vice Chair Faber. Motion carried
   5-0-0. Chair votes in the affirmative.

E. NEW BUSINESS
1. Receive a presentation from the Utilities Committee regarding the status of the
Community Choice Electricity Aggregation Program.
Utilities Committee Energy Subcommittee Chair Lynn Wiles, committee member Paul Ramsay,
and Nick Shostak (Freedom Energy Logistics) presented a comprehensive update on the
Community Choice Aggregation (CCA) Program. Since launching on April 1, 2026, the program
has achieved over 90% participation (~9,500 enrolled meters), generating $893,294 in cumulative
savings through August 31, 2026, with forecasted savings reaching $1,692,392 through January
2027. Mr. Wiles highlighted a newly signed 12-month supply agreement fixed at $0.13523/kWh
effective November 2026 through October 2027, protecting residents against expected rate
increases. Informational presentation; no formal Council action required.

2. Discuss, review and approve the renewal of the Motor Vehicle Junkyard Dealer’s
Licensing of Junkyard, located at 55 Hall Road, Londonderry, to Grillo Auto Salvage LLC
pursuant to RSA 236:121.

Councilor Deb Paul recused herself from this agenda item and stepped away from the bench.
Assistant Building Inspector Brad Anderson presented the license application for Grillo Auto
Salvage LLC (owner Joseph Grillo) at 55 Hall Road following the ownership transfer from Murray's
Auto Recycling (Edward Dudek Jr.). Mr. Anderson confirmed a site inspection on September 15
showed full compliance with NHDES Best Management Practices and state licensing conditions
under RSA 236:121. The Council amended the motion title to reflect a new license rather than
renewal.
   Councilor Bouchard moved that the Londonderry Town Council hereby approves the request of Mr.
   Joseph Grillo for the Junkyard License for Grillo Auto Salvage LLC, located at 55 Hall Road,
   Londonderry, NH. Seconded by Councilor Combes. Motion carried 4-0-0, with Councilor Paul
   recused. Chair votes in the affirmative.
Councilor Paul rejoined the bench following the vote.

3. Discuss, approve and authorize the Town Manager to enter into a long-term lease
agreement in accordance with NH RSA Chapter 53-A for the School District Office
addition to Town Hall with the Londonderry School District.
Chair Dunn announced that this agenda item was postponed to the October 5, 2026 meeting to
allow Acting Town Manager Kim Bernard adequate time to review the proposed intermunicipal
lease agreement.
