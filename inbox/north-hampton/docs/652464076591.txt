Title: Minutes: Recreation Commission Meeting Agenda
Fetched: 2026-11-01

NORTH HAMPTON RECREATION COMMISSION DRAFT MEETING MINUTES SEPTEMBER
                               2026

In attendance: Joe Manzi, Carrie Becker, Al Brown, Taryne Connery, Jim Maggiore, Jane
Morse, Cam Reinbold, Yvonne LeBlanc

Al called the meeting to order at 6:07.

There were no citizen’s comments.

Old Home Day review:
Carrie suggested that an event/events for tweens be included in OHD. It was met with
agreement that there should be an event to attract this age group and will be followed up
next year when planning the next OHD.

The food truck had fewer options and served in a more timely manner than last year. The
feed back was excellent.

The 5k race was run well but it was felt more water should be made available to the
runners. Perhaps the addition of a time clock will be helpful.

The trolley ride was a great success and the NHPL was happy to sponsor this. All agreed
this should be an annual event at OHD. Thank you to Rob Southworth for a memorable
tour of our town.

Joe would like to include more participants in this event and will seek out new potential
businesses to join the events.

Yvonne commented how involved the kids were at Dearborn with the available games
such as bocci, volleyball, tennis and pickleball, etc. and that kids were playing together
having fun without screens. We all agreed that is so positive! Yvonne also posited the
idea of a banner crossing Atlantic Ave with the OHD date on it as a reminder of the
upcoming event.

Time capsule: At Dearborn Park we received many contributions for the capsule and Jim
brought them to the meeting.. The items will also include the North Hampton Declaration
signed by the families and will also include the Boards and Rec Commission members.
The final selections will be made at our next meeting and a final determination will be
made as to the placement of the capsule.

The Town Crier was played by Jim Maggiore and all gathered around to listen to the
meaning of the North Hampton Declaration. The period costume added to the fun event
and tied into the 250th celebration. Thanks to Jim for a great performance!!
Golf tourney: It will take place on 9/21 Monday at Sagamore Golf Course with tee times
available rather than a shot gun start.Currently we have 17 golfers and expect many more
to register. Our sponsors include RB Allen and Cactus Graphics. A donation has been
given to the Parks and Rec from Hockey East of 2 tickets to the semi final.There will be a
putting contest, goodie bags with snack and water. A BBQ catered lunch will follow.

The Commission By-Laws will be updated and voted on at the next Commission meeting.
The possibility of creating a Friends of Parks and Rec was discussed and would be
considered a 50C3 organization with anticipated increase in donations to sponsor
scholarship programs. More information will follow.

Joe is increasing our presence in social media to increase awareness of the programs.

Al made a motion to adjourn at 7:02, seconded by Taryn and agreed by all present.
