PUBLIC HEARING
                                               TOWN OF NORTH HAMPTON
                                                    Planning Board
                                            Tuesday, November 17, 2026 at 6:30pm
                                             TOWN HALL, 231 ATLANTIC AVENUE


Notice is hereby given that the North Hampton Planning Board will conduct a Public Hearing on
Tuesday, November 17, 2026 beginning at 6:30 pm at the North Hampton Town Hall. The Planning
Board will consider the following amendments to the Town of North Hampton Zoning Ordinance:
1. To amend Section 201.2 Zoning Districts by retitling the High Density and Medium Density
    Residential Districts and further clarify the associated permitted uses, as follows:
201.2 Zoning Districts
The Township Town is divided into the districts stated in this Ordinance as shown by the district
boundaries in the Zoning Map. The districts are: *3/12/1968
A. R-1 High Density Residential District: The high-density R-1 district is designated for land to be
   used primarily for smaller single family or duplex residential dwellings. with minimum yard space
   where central water and sewer facilities are available or where the installation of these facilities is
   feasible Only one single family residence or one duplex residence is allowed on a lot. Permitted
   uses and uses granted by special exception as listed in Section 202.2 are allowed in the R-1 district
   after receiving all required Town approvals. **3/10/181, 3/10/2020, 3/12/2024
B. R-2 Medium-Density Residential District: The medium-density R-2 district is designated for
   land which is to be used primarily for medium to large single-family residential dwellings. with
   maximum yard space which will make possible the handling of the individual family’s water and
   sewage disposal needs where central water and municipal facilities are not now available or where
   the immediate installation of these facilities is now immediately feasible Only one single-family
   residence is allowed on a lot. This district also includes areas where agriculture and other open
   land uses are appropriate and natural conditions made the land unsuitable for intensive
   development. Permitted uses and uses granted by special exception as listed in Section 202.3 are
   allowed in the R-2 district after receiving all required Town approvals. #3/12/1068, 3/10/2009,
   312/2024
2. To add a new Section 202.10 Non-Conforming Lots as follows:
    202.10 Non-Conforming Lots.
    A. The lot lines of a non-conforming lot shall only be adjusted so as to create a conforming lot.
