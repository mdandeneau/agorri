Title: Minutes: Planning Board Long Range Planning Committee
Fetched: 2026-11-05

North Hampton Planning Board
                              Long Range Planning Committee
                          Meeting Minutes – September 24, 2026


Committee chair Jennifer Kotzen called the meeting to order at 11:15 AM. In attendance were
Committee members Shep Kroner and Nancy Monaghan. Also in attendance were Select Board member
James Sununu and Planning and Zoning Administrator Rick Milner.

Ms. Kotzen gave a brief review of public comments at the September 15 Planning Board special session
on potential upcoming Zoning Ordinance changes, most of which were on the Accessory Dwelling Unit
size. Mr. Kroner presented a proposed amendment to our ADU ordinance that would allow ADU size to
be between 600 and 950 square feet. The Committee discussed the issue of referencing “footprint” in
any change to the Ordinance, with no resolution. The Committee discussed the issue of the size spread
suggested by Mr. Kroner, and there was general agreement that a range between 600 and 900 or 950
seems to make sense taking into consideration all the pros and cons of smaller ADUs that have already
been discussed.

The Committee came to consensus that we will take the ADU issue to the Planning Board at the October
20 meeting, with one more committee meeting to finalize our recommendations to the Board.

The minutes of the September 10, 2026 meeting as amended were approved 2-0-1 with Mr. Kroner
abstaining. The next meeting is October 15 at 11:15 a.m.


Respectfully submitted,

Nancy Monaghan
