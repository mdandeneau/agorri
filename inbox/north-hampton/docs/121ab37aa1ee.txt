AGENDA
                                       TOWN OF NORTH HAMPTON
                                       Planning Board Work Session
                                   Tuesday, September 15, 2026 6:30pm
                                      TOWN HALL, 231 ATLANTIC AVENUE


I.   Special Public Input Session
     1. The Planning Board invites residents of North Hampton to learn about and provide input
        regarding areas of the town ordinance that are currently being considered by the Board for
        possible updates. The primary topic will be minimum size allowance for Accessory Dwelling
        Units.

II. Public Hearing
    1. Public Hearing regarding amendments to Site Plan Review Regulations Section XII: Parking
       Requirements.
       The intent of the proposed revisions is to better align parking requirements and land uses in the
       Site Plan Regulations with the permitted uses listed in the Zoning Ordinance.

     2. Public Hearing regarding amendments to Site Plan Review Regulations Section V: Site Review
        Requirements.
        The intent of the proposed revisions is to clarify the criteria used to determine the scope of
        review required to develop, expand, or change the use of a non-residential or multi-family
        dwelling site.

III. Continued Business
     1. Discussion of zoning ordinance amendments for the 2026-2027 amendment season.
        Lot line adjustments on non-conforming lots.

IV. Other Business
    1. Committee Updates
       a. Long Range Planning Committee
       b. Rules and Procedures Committee
       c. Capital Improvements Committee
       d. Select Board
       e. RPC Commissioner and Circuit Rider Planner
       f. Planning and Zoning Administrator

     2. Minutes – September 1, 2026 meeting

     Respectfully submitted,
     Jennifer Kotzen, Vice Chair


Applications are available at the North Hampton Town Office, 237A Atlantic Avenue,
