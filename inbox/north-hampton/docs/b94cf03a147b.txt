Title: Minutes: Long Range Planning Committee Agenda
Fetched: 2026-11-05

North Hampton Planning Board
                            Long Range Planning Committee
                           Meeting Minutes September 10, 2026


Committee Chair Jennifer Kotzen called the meeting to order at 11:15 AM. In attendance were
committee member Nancy Monaghan and Planning Board member Phil Wilson. Also in
attendance were Town Administrator Michael Tully, Planning and Zoning Administrator Rick
Milner and Lisa Wilson, Chair of the Conservation Commission.

Ms. Kotzen gave a brief recap of the last meeting which was to review reasons for allowing
smaller accessory dwelling units as an exemption to section 203.14 of the Town of North
Hampton Zoning Ordinance which requires that all dwelling units shall have a minimum ground
floor or lower living area of be at least 720 square feet. Today's The plan for the meeting was
planned to take a look at the unintended consequences of allowing smaller ADUs such as with
footprints of 400 or 500 square feet for those who may want to have a small ADU over a two car
garage for instance.

Mr. Wilson said he read the Town’s Master Plan and did not find any evidence of compatibility
with by such small ADUs as with the Master Plan outlines community goals of non-dense
population, maintaining the Town’s rural atmosphere, protecting wetlands, protecting open space
and preserving water quality. The Committee discussed a desire to provide housing for older
parents or adult children as well as its inability to regulate any ADU rentals and their
affordability. Also discussed were was the inability of even a small family to live moderately and
comfortably in a 400 square foot residence. and also the A concern was expressed that some
ADUs will likely be used as short term rentals and thus be useful to the tourists but not to be
enriching to the housing stock of North Hampton. Lisa Wilson spoke on behalf of the
Conservation Commission about the potentially significant negative environmental impacts of
increased density, especially the impact of increased septic system loads on already densely
developed small lots. She indicated that should would send a letter to the Planning Board to
capture the Commission’s thoughts.

Ms. Kotzen noted that this topic of smaller ADUs will be the center of discussion at a public
input session of the Planning Board September 15 for questions and input from the public. The
next meeting will be September 24 at 11:15. The meeting was adjourned at 1:00 p.m.

Respectfully submitted,
Nancy Monaghan
