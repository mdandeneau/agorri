Title: Minutes: Planning Board Long Range Planning Committee Meeting
Fetched: 2026-11-05

Long Range Planning Committee
                             Meeting Minutes, October 15, 2026
                                Town Offices conference Room

Shep Kroner called the meeting to order at 11:15. In attendance were Committee Member Nancy
Monaghan along with Planning Baord Member Phil Wilson, Conservation Commission Chair
Lisa Wilson, and Town Administrator Michael Tully.

Mr. Kroner showed a presentation he had put together that offered environmental information.
renderings of different size accessory dwelling units, pros and cons and general information
related to ADUs. The key debate was whether hour impermeable coverage in town was 9% or
12%, statistics gleaned from two different sources. The standard is that if there's 10%
impermeable surface the quality of water begins to degrade and thus indicates concern about
increasing density with a large number of ADUs.

The committee was in agreement that we should propose an amendment to the ADU ordinance to
change the permissible size range to 600-950 square feet and clarify that ADUs are exempt from
Section 203.14 of the Zoning Ordinance requiring a 720 square foot first floor for all dwellings.

Miss Monahan suggested perhaps there is a way to use the workforce housing ordinance to allow
small single unit affordable apartments of 400 to 600 square feet that would be available to
those who need that size and would be regulated by a conditional use permit. M's Monahan will
call NHMA legal department to determine if We can do this.

Mr. Kroner will present our findings so far to the Planning Board on Tuesday October 20. Our
next meeting is Octobr 29 at 11:15. The meeting was adjourned at 12:40 p.m.


Respectfully submitted,
Nancy Monaghan
