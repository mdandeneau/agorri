Title: Minutes: Recreation Commission Meeting Agenda
Fetched: 2026-11-12

NORTH HAMPTON RECREATION COMMISSION DRAFT MEETING MINUTES 10/07/2026

In attendance: Joe Manzi, Cameron Reinbold, Carrie Becker, Yvonne LeBlanc, Al Brown,
Jane Morse

There were no citizen’s comments.

Al made a morion to accept the September minutes. It was seconded by Carrie and
approved by all.

Yvonne mentioned the potential use of a banner to be placed at an Atlantic Avenue
location highlighting the upcoming Old Home Day event. Jim Maggiore will look into the
feasibility of accomplishing this.

Director’s report: Joe reported that the funds were down slightly to 3800.00. These funds
help support Parks and Rec programs as well as the scholarships available made
available to NHES students. Joe continued that volley ball will begin again on 10/19,
Monday and Wednesday nights and on Monday and Wednesday mornings at 9:30 the
walker’s group will meet at the rail trail. The turkey bowl annual fun event is scheduled
for 11/27. Colette Travel held an informational meeting on 9/29 to discuss the upcoming
trip to Ireland next spring and the Smokey Mountain trip as well. The senior luncheon
group continues to meet and this month is on 10/20 at 11:30 with a guest speaker.
Following the luncheon bingo contest will be held at a cost of 5.00. Bingo will continue to
be held after the senior luncheon each month. Our annual Trunk or Treat will be held on
Sunday 10/25. This event includes decorated cars, candy for T and T as well as fun
games for the kids. There will be a costume contest and best trunk decorations as well.
Basketball registration is underway.
The pickleball tourney did not have as many participants as hoped, but still a success.

The golf tourney on 9/21 had fewer participants, but still considered a success. The next
tourney will the 5th annual and Joe said it takes the long to establish the tradition and
notoriety and he expects the tourney to grow. Yvonne questioned the cost to provide the
tourney as well as the volunteers needed and the need for additional sponsors.
Additional discussion included providing better items in the gift bag. Monogrammed golf
towels were suggested.

By Laws discussion was tabled until next month.

A yard sale planned for next spring was discussed as Newfields sponsored another
successful town wide yard sale which had many more participants than last year. We
would like to duplicate that success for our town. The yard sale was organized to register
each house, highlight their primary sale items, pay a fee and a digital map was provided
for those homes and posted on Craig’s List to further enhance the success. We all
agreed this would work for our town and looked at potential dates for next May. Looking
at potential conflicts to avoid, it was determined that 5/15 would be the best choice.
Further discussion to follow.

No further information on the Friends of P and R.

Joe asked for volunteers for Trunk or Treat.

Al made a motion to adjourn at 6:40, seconded by Jane and agreed by all.
