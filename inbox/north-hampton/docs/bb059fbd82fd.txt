NEW BUSINESS

Discuss Default Budgets and Budget Committee’s new responsibilities in setting both the School and
Town Defaults - James Sununu

Selectman Sununu said previously what the School Board or Select Board would submit for Default was
reviewed by the Municipal Budget Committee, but not set by the Budget Committee, and appeared in the
final warrant on the Ballot. This summer Default Budgets were put under the purview of the Budget
Committee to approve the number that goes on the ballot and not the governing body. He said the default
was prepared as it has always been.

Mr. Dionne said it is still coming from the same source with the same process from department heads.
Mr. Heitman said the School also did the budget the same way with the exact same process. Town
Administrator Tully said assuming the Select Board does not elect to hold a special meeting for that issue
it automatically goes to the Default Budget. Selectman Sununu said the School has had defaults that go
over in the case of a reduction in staff. Finance Director Ryan Cornwall said a position has to be officially
eliminated for it not to go on the Default, otherwise it has to mirror the proposed for that position.

First Review of Library FY2028 Budget
Library Director Liz Herold said she would first review a snapshot of the Library and the services they
provide, explaining major increases and decreases. She said the Library is open 48 hrs/week, 6 days/week;
the current building was built in 2021 with about 12,000 sq ft and provides a wealth of resources to be
the heart of our community. She said the Library ended the year with 3,106 patrons including 187 new
patrons; meeting rooms were used 1,353 times; total circulation was 42,048 items.

Library Budget: Proposed FY2028 Budget $546,258; FY2027 Budget $523,584 with increase of $22,674;
FY2028 Default Budget: $531,851. Major expenditures: Salaries $423,752; Utilities $34,465; Programs
$3,552; Maintenance $26,492; Operations $18,495; Media $26,492. Total Proposed FY2028 Budget:
$546,258.

Decreases: Bookkeeping/Payroll Services/DVDs; other lines unchanged. Line Items: Staff Salaries 3.5%,
$6,849; Staff Hourly 3.5% $4,000; Health Expenses 15.7%, $8,190; Dental $311, 2.3%; Telephone $148,
3.7%; Postage $15,000 increase; Computer Services $200, 2.5%; Water $250, 5%; Electricity $1,375, 5%;
Maintenance & Repairs $2,500, 25%; Nonprint/Online $100, 2.2%; Periodicals/Newspapers $200, 5%;
Digital Media $200, 3.3%. Total Salaries $17,740, Operations: $363, 2%, Utilities $1,615, 4.9%;
Maintenance $2,500, 6.8%; Media: $447, 1.7%. Increases for costs: staffing, operating, and building
maintenance.

Budget Overview: Salaries: breakdown of expenditures by proposed, default, approved 4.4%. Total
FY2028 approved $423,762, Default $414,270, FY2027 approved $406,000, 4.4% change from 2027.
Proposed: $546,258, 25%; Default: $531,85; FY2027 approved $523,584, total percent change: 4.3%.
Summary: Proposed FY2028 Budget adds $22,674 or 4.3% growth; goal is to maintain services and
resources while responding to increased costs and operating library responsibly.

Mr. Stanton asked that a copy of these slides be provided to the Budget Committee, and asked if meeting
room usage is decreasing. Director Herold said meeting room usage was increasing and the Library does
not charge for meeting room usage, but has a non-appropriated account from the donation jar. Friends
of the Library are their own 401C-3. Mr. Dionne asked about electricity and Director Herold said they are
on the same plan as the town; Selectman Sununu said the contract with Colonial Power was not renewed
due to higher rates and has defaulted back to Eversource. Mr. Stachow asked about cooperation of area
libraries.

Town Administrator Tully said there were budget savings from retirement contributions ($132,424); State
stopped contributing in 2011-2012. Legislature passed a bill to fully fund Retirement System by investing
in the market with people coming in paying for people going out and rates are down this year; Workers
Comp and Property Liability also went down this year by $20,473. Proposed FY2028 Operating Budget:
$10,204,125, FY2028 Default Operating Budget $10,470,171, difference $266,046 or 2.61%.

FY2028 Proposed Operating Budget: FICA/Medicare/Health-Insur/NHRS: +$190,924, Gas +$31,107; Legal

Town Administrator Tully said under Highway the $190,000 figure is for a new position at $104,000 with
immediate savings of $18,047 and future savings on Police Vehicle maintenance, Highway Equipment
maintenance, and Building maintenance with savings in multiple departments. Pie Chart: Debt Service
$916,884, 9%; Fixed Overhead $326,936, 3%; Other spending $525,269, 5%; Personnel $7,599,237, 73%.
Current inflation rate is 3.8%, Social Security set for 3.6% increase; cost increase FY2028 Budget 1.8%.
Proposed budget is at rate of less than 50% of inflation.

Mr. Stanton asked if new inhouse maintenance would add one more year to Police Vehicle lifespan. Town
Administrator Tully said we are down to one cruiser/year and believes it can be pushed out from 5 to 6
years with good maintenance. Mr. Miller asked if the Police Department is now at full complement; Town
Administrator Tully said the department is still down 4 officers, but is fully funded in this budget. Mr.
Miller said Parks & Rec synopsis on page 11 is unclear; Town Administrator Tully said when Joe Manzi
came the department was in the red, but we have now been able to fund part of his salary and add
programs, and we are also constantly looking for a recreation building,

Mr. Stachow asked whether the Water Commission would be included in future conversations about
hydrant rates and brought up PUC’s recent rate increase. Town Administrator Tully said the Water
Commission will be part of conversations per the Town Charter, and said the Town has planned for a new
rate case. He said hydrant rates for North Hampton are much higher than for other towns and it would be
helpful to be able to get the breakdown. Mr. Stachow said he would request a breakdown at the next
Water Commission meeting.

Mr. Stanton asked about leasing a facility for the recreation department. Selectman Sununu said if the
lease is for more than a year it has to go on a warrant but they can enter into a short-term lease. Mr.
Dionne said the Table of Contents for Municipal Resources in the budget book is from 2019. Town
Administrator Tully said that is when the Pay Study was done and they continue to add COLA raises. Ms.
O’Brien asked if the School and the Town have the same benefits provider. Town Administrator Tully said
they do and also explained how the Town was able to get into a smaller group category for more savings
by not considering retirees as fulltime employees.

Town Administrator Tully said further questions can be forwarded to him and replies will be provided to
the Budget Chairman for distribution to members. The Budget Committee needs to approve the Town
and Default Budgets by January 21, 2027.
