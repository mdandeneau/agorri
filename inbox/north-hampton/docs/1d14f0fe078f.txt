NORTH HAMPTON RECREATION COMMISSION DRAFT MEETING MINUTES NOVEMBER
                              2026

In attendance: Joe Manzi, Kathy Lociatto, Carrie Becker, Al Brown, Jum Maggiore, Taryn
Connery, Jane Morse, Yvonne LeBlanc, Cam Reinbold

Al Brown called the meeting to order on 11/04/26 at 6:07

Al made a motion to accept the October minutes, ot was seconded by Carrie and agree
upon by all.

There were no citizen’s comments.

Director’s updates:
Joe provided a detailed update on existing as well as future programs. The before school
continues every morning. A mini golf after school day as well as a bowling and pizza
party day was held this past month. The Halloween dance had 69 participants and the
Trunk or Treat held Sunday 10/25 was a success. There are new upcoming after school
programs including gymnastics and tumbling. The Turkey Bowl flag football event will be
held on 11/22. The annual and well loved Breakfast with Santa will be held 12/12 from 8:00
to 10:30 seatings available. Registration is required and children eat free and adults are
10.00 each.
Senior/adult programs: Volley ball is every Monday night at NHES gym and Thursday
night basketball at the gym. A walking group meets at the head of the rail trail every
Monday and Wednesday mornings at 9:30. There is a comedy night upcoming with
registrations required. There is a holiday light trip to Louden on 12/2 and a trip to the
Rochester Opera House to see A Christmas Carol.

Member’s update: Carrie suggested that commission members share varied
responsibilities for each program and to ensure there is less obligation to be present at
every event. We all have work and family commitments that divide our time.

Commission by-laws: These were reviewed at our last meeting and changes were agreed
and voted on to amend a few words. In article 4 be changed to reflect the commission to
include any high school student to be appointed an associate member rather than a
Winnecunnet student. Article 5 will be amended to reflect the requirement the Secretary
will be provide the record of the meeting within 5 days. Article 6 line C will be struck.

The yard sale date is 5/12/27 and will be promoted through all social media including
Facebook, Craig’s List. Cam is researching all options. This is a town wide event with a
fee of 20.00 per house. Maps will be available. More information will follow in the next few
months.
Jim Maggiore provided a time capsule review. It was decided the capsule will be
assembled and placed in the designated area within the town. It will be sealed and not to
opened for 50 years. There were many contributors during Old Home Day with maps,
letter and pictures portraying out town in 2026. The closure of the capsule will be filmed
at our next meeting in December.

The Friends of the Commission: There was no further update tonight.

Al made a motion to adjourn at 6:39, seconded by Taryn and agree to be all.
