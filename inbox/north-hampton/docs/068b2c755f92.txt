Title: Agenda: Planning Board Public Hearing Notice
Fetched: 2026-11-19

PUBLIC HEARING
                                               TOWN OF NORTH HAMPTON
                                                    Planning Board
                                          Tuesday, December 15, 2026 at 6:30pm
                                           TOWN HALL, 231 ATLANTIC AVENUE


Notice is hereby given that the North Hampton Planning Board will conduct a Public Hearing on
Tuesday, December 15, 2026 beginning at 6:30 pm at the North Hampton Town Hall to amend
Section 401 of the Town of North Hampton Zoning Ordinance (Accessory Dwelling Unit) as
follows:
1. To expand the allowable square footage from a maximum of 800 square feet to a minimum of 600
    square feet and a maximum of 950 square feet; and
2. To define how living space is calculated.

Complete copies of the proposed revisions are available for public review at the North Hampton
Town
Office, 237A Atlantic Avenue, Monday — Thursday 7:00am to 4:00pm and Friday 8:00am to
12:00pm
