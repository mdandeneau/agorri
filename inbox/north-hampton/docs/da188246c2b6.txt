Title: Minutes: Planning Board Long Range Planning Committee
Fetched: 2026-11-13

Long Range Planning Committee
                          Meeting Minutes, October 29, 2026
                           Town Offices conference Room

Committee Chair Jennifer Kotzen called the meeting to order at 11:15. In attendance were
Committee Members Nancy Monaghan and Shep Kroner, and Planning Board Member Phil
Wilson, Conservation Commission Chair Lisa Wilson, and Town Administrator Michael Tully.

Ms. Kotzen said she and Mr. Kroner had received e-mail correspondence early that morning
from Selectman James Sununu, who has expressed an interest in amendments to the Accessory
Dwelling Unit ordinance. She read Mr. Sununu’s e-mail, which outlined his desire that the
minimum square footage for an ADU be reduced to 400 square feet to further expand
opportunities for more housing development in North Hampton. He cited a survey of garage
sizes in town that showed more than 400 garages in the 400 to 600 square foot range that could
be used for ADUs if the minimum ADU size were 400 square feet.

Following up on a lengthy conversation at an earlier Long Range Planning Committee meeting
about the town's total impervious coverage as it relates to increasing density, Mr. Sununu cited a
third, different study and said he believes it will be decades before North Hampton’s impervious
coverage reaches levels that will affect the quality of drinking water.

Mr. Wilson reminded the Committee that individual opinions cannot be taken to represent the
view of the town more broadly, and that the Planning Board must always be guided in its
decision making by the Master Plan. For decades residents have repeatedly prioritized
preservation of the town’s rural character and natural resources as key objectives of that plan.
Noting the potential for significant impact that ADU development may have on both the town’s
character and its natural resources, Mr. Wilson renewed his suggestion that, before determining
whether 400SF ADUs are appropriate, we do a town survey as has been discussed several times
at the Planning Board, and include specific questions about ADUs.

The Committee continued a lengthy discussion of the issues, and Ms. Monahan suggested we
respond to Mr. Sununu that we hold off on considering any movement to a 400 square foot
minimum until we have had a chance to complete a survey of town residents, (in time for the
2028 town warrant).

Our next meeting is November 12 at 11:15 a.m., at which time we must finalize our proposed
amendments to present to the planning board for approval that will go to a public hearing in
December. The original proposed amendment expanding ADU size to 600 to 950 square feet
remains as proposed. The meeting was adjourned at 1:20 p.m.

Respectfully submitted,
Nancy Monaghan
