MINUTES OF THE NOVEMBER 4, 2026 SPECIAL SELECT BOARD MEETING

MEMBERS PRESENT: Board Members Chair Allison Knab, Vice Chair Joe Anderson, Tedd
Tramaloni

ALSO PRESENT: Town Administrator Tim Roache, Finance Administrator Lori Ruest

The Select Board convened a special meeting to establish the 2026 municipal tax rate and review
recent bids received for the town's trash collection contract.

Mr. Roache presented a revised budget spreadsheet that includes a new summary format, with
expenditures organized into separate departmental worksheets, including administration and
elections. The board reviewed tax-rate projections and discussed adjustments to the fund balance
and overlay. Based on the MS-1 report revenue estimates and a $100,000 overlay, the
preliminary municipal tax rate was projected at approximately $2.42. Board members discussed
the need to provide a buffer for anticipated increases in Fire Department expenses and potential
cost-of-living adjustments. After reviewing several scenarios, the board determined that a
municipal tax rate of $2.22 would provide an appropriate balance between funding needs and
taxpayer impact. Mr. Anderson motioned to set the 2026 municipal tax rate at $2.22, with
corresponding adjustments to the fund balance and overlay. Mr. Tramaloni seconded the motion.
All voted in favor.

The board also discussed factors contributing to overall property tax increases, noting that
school-related costs represent a significant portion of the tax rate. Members cited recently
approved teacher contracts, school renovation bond payments, and other voter-approved
expenditures as drivers of the increase. The board emphasized the importance of educating
residents about these costs, as well as deficiencies in state funding, and explaining that many of
the increases result from previously approved commitments and inflationary pressures.
Discussion also included concerns about the effect of rising costs on residents living on fixed
incomes and the uncertainty surrounding the impact of future Social Security and Medicare
adjustments.

The board thanked Ms. Ruest for her assistance with the tax rate. She was excused from the
meeting.

The board then reviewed bids submitted by Casella and ELM Services for the town's trash
collection contract. Following discussion, members agreed that both proposals were deficient
and did not adequately meet the requirements of the request for proposals. Mr. Tramaloni
motioned to reject both the Casella and ELM Services proposals and pursue a new RFP process
at a later date. Mr. Anderson seconded the motion. All voted in favor. The board directed that
both vendors be notified of the decision.

Several items remain under review, including correcting the budget spreadsheet formula used to
calculate changes in the fund balance, determining the financial impact of adjusting Social
Security placeholder figures, and identifying when the assessor's work will be finalized so that
tax bills can be prepared and mailed.
Action items discussed included distribution of the revised budget spreadsheet, calculation of the
budgetary impact of a potential cost-of-living adjustment, review of tax projection formulas,
development of public communications regarding tax-rate changes and school-related costs,
initiation of a new trash contract RFP process, and notification to Casella and ELM Services that
