                                  Notice of Public Hearing
                         Town Budget and 2026 Town Meeting Warrant
                               Town of Stratham Select Board

There will be a public hearing held on Monday, February 2, 2026, at 7:00 p.m., in the Hutton
Room of the Stratham Municipal Center, 10 Bunker Hill Avenue, concerning the Town of
Stratham's 2026 town budget and 2026 town warrant.

Specific items to be discussed include but are not necessarily limited to the following:

   1. Proposed 2026 Town operating budget.

   2. Proposed 2026 Appropriation for Capital Improvements.

   3. Proposed funding of certain Capital Reserve Funds for 2026.

   4. The funding of fire department training, vehicles, equipment purchases, and
      advance life support services by authorizing the use of funds from the “Stratham
      EMS Special Revenue Fund.”

   5. Municipal Buildings Keyless Access Systems

   6. Maintenance and Improvements to Fire Tower

   7. Distribution of Funds Received from the Sale of Cemetery Lots

   8. Discontinue Right of Way Route 33

   9. Modify Optional Veterans Tax Credit

   10. Any other special warrant articles to appear on the 2026 warrant.

   11. Any other petition warrant articles to appear on the 2026 warrant.

   12. Any other items to appear on the 2026 town warrant.

If necessary due to weather or other unforeseen event, the public hearing will be rescheduled to
Monday February 9, 2026 at 7:00 pm at the same location. If the rescheduled date is
necessary, the rescheduling will be made at the call of the Select Board Chair and posted
accordingly in advance of the scheduled start of the February 3rd meeting. For preliminary
budget materials and other town meeting information, please visit
www.StrathamNH.gov/town-meeting
