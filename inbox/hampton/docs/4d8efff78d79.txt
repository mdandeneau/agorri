 IV.    Updates on Action Items from October 20
        a. Job descriptions and findings from other communities. Sharon Mullen reported on
           economic development job descriptions and findings from other communities.

 V.     UNH Extension Community Business Engagement (CBE) Program
        The group was informed that Hampton has been accepted into the UNH Extension
        Community Business Engagement program. The town is awaiting a memorandum of
        understanding (MOU) from UNH and the Select Board’s appointment of a local steering
        committee.
        The group reviewed Exeter’s CBE report, its recommendations, and the composition of
        Exeter’s steering committee. Discussion included:
        •   Possible membership for Hampton’s steering committee, such as representatives
            from the Chamber of Commerce, Rotary Club, Master Plan Implementation
            Committee, etc.
        •   Expected deliverables from the program, including a list of local businesses and their
            contact information.
        •   Ensuring the survey questions are preserved so the town can re-run the survey in
            three to five years and measure change over time.
        •   “Hamptonizing” the process to fit the town’s seasonal economy, for example by
            meeting with beach business owners in early May and mid-to-late September, ideally
            on rainy days when businesses are less busy.
 VI.   Discussion: Economic Development Coordinator Role
       a. History and options. The group reviewed the history of the proposal, including last
          year’s warrant article, and alternatives including a three-year coordinator pilot funded
          through a separate warrant article with public reporting requirements.
       b. Questions raised on October 20. The group discussed responses to questions
          raised at the October 20 meeting regarding the number of town positions,
          accountability for performance, budget risk, and the balance between supporting
          existing businesses and attracting new ones.
       c. Whether or when to propose an Economic Development Coordinator. The group
          discussed the feasibility of proposing a position this year versus after the conclusion
          of the CBE report.
       The group also discussed the possibility of an Economic Development Committee e.g.,
       as an alternative to, or in combination with, a coordinator position.

 VII. Timeline and Outside Review
       The group reviewed its timeline for completing recommendations:
       •   Drafting is scheduled to be completed before Thanksgiving week.
       •   The economic development directors of Exeter and Derry, along with Nate Bernitz of
           UNH Extension, have offered to review drafts.
       •   Recommendations are due to the Select Board on December 14, 2026.
