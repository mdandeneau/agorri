    o Suggested Changes for 2027 Permitted Uses in the Town Center Districts
Mr. Pope explained that the Planning Board is reviewing a possible amendment or amendments for
uses in the Town Center Districts and would like to receive public feedback before proceeding. The
Committee discussed how to go about getting that feedback (simple posterboard survey) and what
locations:
    o Blue Harbor Coffee
    o Lane Public Library
    o Town Hall lobby
    o Parks and Recreation building
    o Hampton Academy open house
    o Hampton Chamber Business After Hours
Ms. St. Jeanne arranged for the Committee to set up at the Hampton Academy open house
tomorrow night. Mr. Pope will work with the Planning Office to get a posterboard survey ready.
Mr. Sheridan noted his wife may be able to assist with making the posterboard surveys as well.
