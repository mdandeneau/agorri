       I. CALL TO ORDER
   John Tirrell (Vice Chair) commenced the meeting at 7:00 PM. The Pledge of Allegiance was said.
   The Committee members introduced themselves.

   II. PUBLIC COMMENT

III.     APPOINTMENTS

IV.    OLD BUSINESS
   Implementation Priorities for 2026
       o Housing Opportunity Planning (HOP) Update
   Flexible Sizing Dwelling Units Amendment
   Purpose: To encourage diversity in housing types and sizes, consistent with the Hampton Master
   Plan, without increasing the size of developments.
   A public hearing was held on November 4th and the amendment was moved to ballot. There may be
   a public education opportunity for this Committee, regarding Flexible Sizing Dwelling Units, ahead
   of the March Town Meeting.

   HOP Grant Regulatory Work
   Adding Workforce and Affordable Housing income criteria to Site Plan and Subdivision
   Regulations and review of the Site Plan and Subdivision Regulations for modifications to support
   desired changes in the Town Center. There is some continued work that needs to be done with this
   but there could be a public hearing on it in the early part of 2027.

   o Priorities to Carry-Over for 2027
Mr. Bliss provided an update from the Select Board on items that could impact the priorities:
       • Municipal parking lot Warrant Article
       • Wayfaring Signage for Downtown
       • Removal of diagonal parking spaces in front of Blue Harbor Coffee and converting those
           to parallel parking spots
       • Creation of the Economic Development Advisory Committee and participation in the
           UNH Cooperative Extension Community Business Engagement Program
       • RSA 79-E Advisory Committee

Ms. Dionne suggested carrying over action item 4.19 into 2027.

Mr. Nyhan noted that the Chamber is working with the State Parks concerning EV charging stations
at the beach and suggested this be looked into for the municipal parking lot. Mr. Bachand explained
that the Planning Office is working with Clean Energy NH on a grant for this.

Public Engagement
   o Summary of Activities from September and October
       • Mr. Pope conducted a dot survey regarding the Town Center outside of Blue Harbor
          Coffee.
       • Mr. Bachand gave a presentation to the Winnacunnet High School cultural geography
          class on Town Planning and a presentation at the NHMA Conference on the Cottage
          Court Ordinance.

Mr. Bachand asked for volunteers to assist Mr. Pope with the dot surveys. Ms. Grier, Ms. Dionne,
and Mr. Sheridan volunteered. Mr. Bachand will notify Mr. Pope.

Mr. Nyhan suggested having members of the Committee attend the Chamber’s Business After
Hours events.

Ms. St. Jeanne suggested the PTA meetings and will reach out to the PTA representative on the
School Board.

Mr. Tirrell will speak to Mr. Pope about drafting talking points for members to have ready during
public engagement events.

   o Zoning Amendments for March 2027 Town Meeting
Mr. Bachand reviewed the 2027 Zoning Amendments page on the Town website with the
Committee, noting the amendments that were moved to ballot during the November 4th public
hearing and the upcoming public hearing on November 18th.

      o Other Public Engagement Opportunities
   The Committee discussed electioneering and the 2026 changes to the electioneering law.

   Mr. Bachand reviewed the Stormwater Resources page on the Town website and discussed the
   Stormwater Survey that is part of the public engagement and outreach work being done regarding
   the updates to the Town’s Stormwater Regulations.

       o Committee Member Responsibilities and Timeframe

 V.  NEW BUSINESS
   Implementation Priorities for 2027
     o Member Suggestions for Next Priority Items
         • Committee members are to review the Master Plan Implementation Action Items
           spreadsheet and pick out their top five priorities and send to Mr. Bachand by December
           21st. There will be a work session during the January meeting to set the 2027
           Implementation Priorities.

   • Member Reports from their Boards/Commissions/Committees
   SAU 90 School Board: Ms. St. Jeanne noted that the SAU is working with the Conservation
   Commission on a Warrant Article to transfer ownership of Batchelder Pond from the School to
   Conservation. Ms. St. Jeanne also discussed the passing of question #2 on the ballot at the
   November election and the impact this will have on the School budget.

   Budget Committee: Mr. Sheridan noted that in December/January the Budget Committee will
   begin meeting twice a week. He encouraged folks to pay attention to the budget discussions by
   attending the meetings or watching on Channel 22 and to see the amount of time and thought that
   goes into the budgets by the Department Heads.

   Hampton Area Chamber of Commerce: Mr. Nyhan explained that the Chamber has a Policy
   Committee that has been focused on legislation at the Federal and State level but will now also have
   a focus at the local community level.
