III.   ASSESSING PROCESS FOR COASTAL RESILIENCE INCENTIVE ZONE (CRIZ)
       PROJECTS
       Ms. Glover spoke with Paul McKenney from MRI who stated he is not familiar with CRIZ
       but he will research properties in Hampton that have had similar improvements to see
       how that has impacted the assessed value. He will also reach out to associates in the
       assessing community.
       The Committee discussed Ms. Pullium’s review of financial documents submitted with
       applications. The objection being to determine if the project could or could not move
       forward without the RSA 79-E relief. The Committee discussed having a third-party
       review of financial documents, paid for by the applicant, done if needed.
      The Committee reviewed the response from legal counsel on whether CRIZ could have
      a separate set of public benefits. It was the consensus of the Committee that CRIZ can
      have its own separate set of public benefits and that this will need to go to ballot to be
      modified.
IV.   COVENANT TEMPLATES FOR CONVERSION PROJECTS AND CRIZ PROJECTS
      Ms. Novotney reviewed the draft templates with the Committee. It was the consensus of
      the Committee to move forward with the template that allows for the extension of the
      completion deadline if the applicant has been granted a one-year extension of the
      conditional approval from the Planning Board or a one-year extension of the building
      permit from the Building Department.
      Ms. Novotney reviewed the draft applications with the Committee (traditional RSA 79-E
      and RSA-79:4-d). It was the consensus of the Committee to move forward with both
      draft applications.
