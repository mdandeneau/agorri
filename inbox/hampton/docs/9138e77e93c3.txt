Title: Minutes: Budget Committee Tour of Parks & Recreation
Fetched: 2026-11-01

Hampton NH Municipal Budget Committee
                         Meeting

                                        October 14, 2026

Present:      Anne Marie Galanis, Chair
              Alan Barclay
              Fred Diana
              Don Bliss, Select Board Alternate Representative
       Absent (excused): Mike Plouffe Patrick Sheridan; Scott Blair; Bob Ladd, HBVD
       Representative; Molly St. Jeanne, SAU 90 Representative


The Budget Committee met with René Boudreau, Director, Parks and Recreation Department at
34 Park Ave at 9:00 am. Mr. Boudreau reported that the new van and plow-equipped truck have
been received. New flooring will be installed in the larger building over the next several weeks.
During the tour of the facility and several parks, committee members were shown areas requiring
maintenance, including the facility entrance driveway, building siding, ballfields, and
playgrounds. The cost of maintaining the department’s facilities and parks continues to be a
challenge.
Mr. Boudreau drove the committee members present to several area parks maintained by the
department. Staffing was also discussed. The department has three full-time recreation staff
members and one full-time parks foreman. During the summer, two part-time employees are
typically hired to assist with park maintenance. However, due to the default budget this year, one
of those positions was eliminated in August. Mr. Boudreau explained that staff hired to support
recreation programs are funded separately through the Recreation Revolving Fund.
The tour ended at 10:09 a.m.
