IV. NEW PUBLIC HEARINGS
 PUBLIC HEARING FOR ZONING ORDINANCE AMENDMENTS
 1. Required Zoning Ordinance Amendments for Compliance with 2026 Changes to the New Hampshire
    Revised Statutes Annotated (RSA’s):
     a. Amend Article 1 - General Provisions, Section 1.5 Definitions (“Attached Accessory Dwelling Unit”
        and “Detached Accessory Dwelling Unit”) and Article 6 - Specific Use Regulations, Section 6.1
        Accessory Dwelling Units to Single-Family Dwellings (Section 6.1.5) to comply with changes in
        State law as prescribed in NH RSA 674:71-72 regarding Accessory Dwelling Units.
     b. Amend Article 7 - Procedures, Section 7.2 Construction Provisions (Sections 7.2.2.4 and 7.2.2.5) and
        Article 8 - Administration and Enforcement, Section 8.1 Board of Adjustment (Section 8.1.3) to
        comply with changes in State law as prescribed in NH RSA 155-A regarding Building and Fire Code
        Provisions.
     c. Amend Article 1 - General Provisions, Section 1.5 Definitions (new definition of “Charter School”)
        and Article 5 - General Use Regulations, Section 5.2 Use Definitions and Requirements (Sections
        5.2.7 and 5.2.43) to comply with changes in State law as prescribed in NH RSA 194-B:3 and 674:54
        regarding Charter Schools.
     d. Amend Article 5 - General Use Regulations, Section 5.2 Use Definitions and Requirements (Sections
        5.2.13, 5.2.14, 5.2.15, and 5.2.16) to comply with changes in State law as prescribed in NH RSA
        674:16 and 674:81 regarding Child Care Centers.
     e. Amend Article 2 - Zoning Map, Section 2.2 Dimensional Requirements (Modify Section 2.2.9);
        Amend Article 3 - Zoning Districts, Section 3.2 Town Center District (Modify Sections 3.2.3.1,
        3.2.3.20, and 3.2.4); Amend Article 4 - Overlay Districts, Section 4.1 Wetland Conservation District
        (Modify Section 4.1.7.C.4); Amend Article 6 - Specific Use Regulations, Section 6.8 Multi-Family
        Dwellings (Modify Sections 6.8.1 and 6.8.2, and add a new Section 6.8.3). These amendments are
        set forth to comply with changes in State law as prescribed in NH RSA 674:16 and 674:80 regarding
        Multi-Family Residential Development on Commercially Zoned Land.
 2. Amend Article 3 - Zoning Districts, Section 3.2 Town Center District through modifications to Section
    3.2.3 Permitted Uses in the Town Center-Historic District and Section 3.2.6 Permitted Uses in the Town
    Center-South and Town Center-North Districts. The substantive changes involve prohibiting auto dealers
    and motor vehicle repair and service stations under Section 3.2.6 (these uses are already prohibited under
    Section 3.2.3), and requiring that uses with drive-up windows (which are only permitted under Section
    3.2.6) contain their vehicle stacking within the subject property. Additional text adjustments are included
    for the purpose of clarifying and refining existing uses. These changes are intended to facilitate future
    improvements in the Town Center District, consistent with the Town of Hampton Master Plan and
    recommendations from the Plan NH Community Design Charrette.

    3. Amend Article 7 - Procedures, Section 7.1 Building Permits and Inspection to add a new Section 7.1.3.1
       to require notification to the Building Department of any non-residential ownership changes, use
       changes, expansions of use, changes in occupancy use, and any other changes that may impact any
       applicable building code, fire code, or life safety code even when such changes do not require permits or
       other review. The Building Inspector, Health Inspector, and Fire Chief, or their designee(s) shall have the
       right to inspect the property consistent with applicable federal, state, and local laws to ensure
       compliance. This amendment is intended to ensure that non-residential operations in the Town of
       Hampton maintain safe and sanitary conditions, which is essential to protecting the public interest.
