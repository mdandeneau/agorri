HAMPTON BEACH VILLAGE DISTRICT
                                   MEETING
                               November 11, 2026


Present
Chuck Rage, Chairman & Commissioner
Maureen Buckley, Commissioner

The meeting was called to order at 5:30 p.m.

The Pledge of Allegiance was said.

Chairman Rage asked for a Moment of Silence for Murial Bouchard and Marilyn Henderson
who have recently passed away.

Chairman Rage thanked all veterans past and present.

Chairman Rage said Bob Ladd is not here tonight and is going to retire at the end of the
year. It will be necessary to appoint someone to take his place on the Board. Catherine Roy
has agreed to do this. She is very knowledgeable about all aspects of the Beach.

Chamber of Commerce

John Nyhan, President of the Chamber of Commerce, said he was here in regard to their
proposal for 2027. Once again this year there was a great deal of preparation concerning
what would be presented to the Commissioners.

Mr. Nyhan said they have been talking with NBC Universal Advertising. They have given us
a very good proposal for advertising. Another is Food and Travel magazine. They have
proposed a full page ad and an editorial. We are looking at expanding the Albany Times and
the Union Leader. Also looking at advertising opportunities with the Concord Monitor,
Albany Times and Eagle. Mr. Nyhan said he wanted the Commissioners to have a full
picture of the advertising we are doing.

Mr. Nyhan said last year the Chamber asked for $87,101. There will also be the $25,000
from the GPP at the end of the year. NH Travel and Tourism will also help in funding. This

Entertainment

Amanda Morneault, Entertainment Director, said she had good news and bad news. The
bad news is that the Salem Boys will not be returning next year. They do hope they will
come back in the future. The good news is that she secured a country artist for July 9th. Ms.
Morneault said she has received a good number of inquiries from people regarding what
they would like to see. She said she has reached out to Recycled Percussion. The cost is very
high. We will see what can possibly be done. A possibility would be sponsors. Ms.
Morneault said she will be looking into it.

Beautification

Chairman Rage said Linda Gebhart was not able to attend the meeting. She gave him
several items to present. A new tree is needed on High Street. There is a need for someone
to paint the buoy.

New Business

There was no New Business.

Old Business

Chairman Rage discussed the trash situation. The Selectmen voted 4 to 1 to charge
businesses for trash pick up. This may cause problems.

Public Comment

There was no Public Comment.

Approval of Minutes

Moved by Commissioner Buckley, seconded by Chairman Rage, to approve the Minutes of
October 14. 2026.

Vote: 2 yes, 0 no. Motion passed unanimously.
