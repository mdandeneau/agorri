HAMPTON HERITAGE COMMISSION
                                       DRAFT MINUTES
                                  Thursday, October 8, 2026
                                  Select Board Meeting Room

MEMBERS PRESENT:
     Ann Carnaby, Chair
     Andy Morse
     Keith Lessard, Planning Board ex-officio
     Donald Bliss, Select Board ex-officio

MEMBERS ABSENT:
     Christine Bushway
     Ufuk Cav, Alternate Member

The meeting was called to order at 6:06pm by Chair Carnaby. Ms. Carnaby led the Pledge of
Allegiance.

PUBLIC COMMENT. No public present

APPROVAL OF DRAFT MINUTES

       MOVED by Morse, SECONDED by Bliss to approve the minutes of the September 10, 2026
       meeting. Approved 3-0-0 (Lessard joined the meeting after the approval of the minutes).

ONGOING PROJECT UPDATES & OLD BUSINESS

       HISTORIC HOUSE MARKERS. No applications for new markers. The members agreed to
       ask the Select Board for permission to install a marker on the blacksmith shop. Ms.
       Carnaby and one other commissioner will represent the Commission, depending on
       availability Mr. Bliss will place the request on the October 26 Select Board agenda.

       WHATZIT-WHEREZIT Map of Hampton. Ms. Carnaby offered additional maps to be
       distributed around town. Mr. Morse took some.

BLACKSMITH SHOP.
      The Commission continued its discussion concerning the potential restoration of the
      Blacksmith Shop on Barbour Road. The group discussed the possibility of installing an
      informational sign in front of the shop. Mr. Morse will develop a draft narrative.

     None reported.

     No new business.

in the Town Manager’s conference room, 2nd floor.

MOVED by Mr. Morse, SECONDED by Mr. Lessard to adjourn. Vote: 4-0-0. The meeting was
