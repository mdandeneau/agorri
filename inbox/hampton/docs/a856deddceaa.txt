Title: Minutes: Budget Committee tour of SAU 90
Fetched: 2026-11-01

Note: Draft minutes are subject to corrections, additions, and deletions


                                       September 9, 2026
Present:      Anne Marie Galanis, Chair
              Alan Barclay
              Scott Blair
              Fred Diana
              Patrick Sheridan
              Don Bliss, Select Board Alternate Representative
              Molly St. Jean, SAU 90 Representative
       Absent (excused): Mike Plouffe; Bob Ladd, HBVD Representative

The meeting/tour started at 1:00 pm at Marston School, SAU90 administration. Met with Dr.
Lois Costa SAU90 Superintendent, Mariah Curtis, SAU90 Finance Director, and Tim
Harrington, Facilities Director. The committee toured each school, beginning with the
administrative area at Marston School and then moving through the school. A question was
asked about the number of children attending all three schools and total capacity. Dr. Costa
reported there are 940 students across all three schools; total capacity is 1200 (approximately 400
each school). Enrollment has remained stable for several years.

At Marston school, Mr. Harrington pointed to the new flooring that has been installed as part of
the annual maintenance plan. Roofing was also discussed as committee members noted stained
ceiling tiles. A new roof will be on the warrant this year as further repairs are no longer
considered viable. The committee toured the build-out of the New England Center for Children
(NECC) program, which was approved by voters this past March.

At the Academy, the committee toured areas addressing STEM education as well as the NECC
program. There was a brief discussion with the Information Technology group regarding cyber-
security initiatives. Mr Sheridan and Mr. Blair left the tour at this point (2:08pm).

The tour concluded at Center School. Mr. Harrington discussed the window project, which voters
approved. Ninety-nine new windows have been installed, and the project is nearing completion.
The committee was also shown the new heating system currently being installed.

The tour ended at 2:30 pm.

Minutes prepared by A. M. Galanis
