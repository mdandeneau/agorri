Title: Minutes: Budget Committee Tour of DPW
Fetched: 2026-11-01

Note: Draft minutes are subject to corrections, additions, and deletions


                                         September 9, 2026
Present:      Anne Marie Galanis, Chair
              Alan Barclay
              Scott Blair
              Fred Diana
              Patrick Sheridan
              Don Bliss, Select Board Alternate Representative
              Molly St. Jeanne, SAU 90 Representative
       Absent (excused): Mike Plouffe; Bob Ladd, HBVD Representative

Meeting started at 9:05 am in Director Jen Hale’s office in the administrative building of the
Department of Public Works, 1 Hardardt’s Way. Also present was Joe Lynch, Deputy Director

The Committee asked questions regarding the DPW facility study. An RFP for the study has
been issued, and an internal needs assessment has been conducted with each department division.
The study will assess current operations and propose options for what is needed to run efficiently
and ensure employee safety.

The impact of the default budget was discussed. The Director noted that all area communities are
facing similar pressures. Some projects have been delayed due to staff shortages and the need to
reallocate existing staff to higher-priority needs, such as trash collection. Areas affected include
changing street signs and addressing sewer, drainage, and catch basin issues.

The Capital Improvement Plan and the possibility and timing of a warrant to rebuild the DPW
facility were also discussed. At this point, it is unclear when a warrant article will be presented to
the voters.

Trash collection staffing and the current condition of the department’s trucks were discussed.
The truck approved by voters this year has been ordered.

The cost and process of obtaining a commercial driver’s license (CDL) for staff were discussed.
For those positions where it is a job requirement, the department pays for the training. The
Director reported CDL training costs have increased significantly and she is working with the
Fire Department to develop an in-house training program that will help reduce the costs.

This portion of the session ended at 9:50 a.m. Mr. Sheridan and Mr. Blair left the session.

Director Hale then took the remaining committee members on a tour of the garage and storage
areas, as well as the sewer and drainage office area. At 10:45 am, Ms. St. Jeanne left the tour.

The remaining committee members met with Mike Carle and Mike Dube for a tour of the
wastewater treatment plant. Plant operations and Phase 2 construction were discussed.

The tour concluded at 11:30 a.m.
