I.    PUBLIC COMMENT
      Chair Bliss opened the meeting for public comment.

      Pat Noone from Kane Company appeared before the Committee to discuss the Liberty
      Lane project and the public benefit it can offer. The project commenced in 2025, and the
      first units ready for occupations will be in 2027. These units will be in the building that
      was converted from former office space. There may be future phases of build-out at this
      property but currently there are no firm plans for additional development. The Kane
      Company would be willing to discuss with the Planning Board what makes a project with
      affordable or workforce housing feasible.

      PUBLIC COMMENT CLOSED.
