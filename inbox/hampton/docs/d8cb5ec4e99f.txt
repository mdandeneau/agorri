      Impervious Surface (Conservation Commission)
Brianna Hagan, Conservation Coordinator, appeared before the Board. Ms. Hagan explained that
since last appearing before the Board she brought this amendment back before the Conservation
Commission for additional discussion. Ms. Hagan reviewed the proposed new language with the
Board:
A deck may only be calculated as pervious if it meets the following criteria:
   a) It is constructed with planks not greater than 8 inches wide with at least 1/4 inch of space
       between each plank or it is constructed with a suitable pervious material.
   b) It is not covered by a roof or enclosed above or below, and the area underneath remains
       entirely pervious (with no use as a storage area).

Patios and decks that are 6 ft or less above ground and located within the Groundwater protection
District of the Wetland Conservation District and are constructed to be pervious according to the
standards above shall only be calculated as pervious up to a maximum of 160 sq ft, any portion
exceeding 160 sq ft shall be calculated as impervious surface.

The Board reviewed and discussed and after legal review will move to public hearing.

      Clarification: Replacement or Repair of Existing Structures in the Wetland Conservation
       District (Conservation Commission)
Ms. Hagan reviewed the proposed new language with the Board.
Replacement or repair of an existing structure (including but not limited to sheds, decks, and
homes); provided that:
   a. The structure has an approved Town Wetlands Permit that aligns with all aspects of the
       proposed replacements or repair. The structure shall also have received a Town Building
       permit, if one was required, and have constructed in compliance with all applicable
       regulatory requirements at the time of the original application. This provision applies to
       Town Weland permits that are not more than five years old, or if greater than five years old,
       are supported by a wetland delineation not more than five years old showing the wetlands in
       the same location or no closer to the proposed work.
   b. The square footage of temporary and/or permanent impacts on the parcel is not increasing
       or encroaching further into the WCD than was originally approved;
   c. The proposed work must comply with Town Ordinances and regulations at time or
       replacement or repair. (If the Building Inspector determines that the proposal is not
       consistent with the purposes of the WCD Ordinance, the proposed activity shall only be
       permitted if the landowner obtains a new Town Wetlands Permit).

The Board reviewed and discussed and after legal review will move to public hearing.

      RSA Amendments (Multi-Family Housing in Commercial Districts, Child Care Centers,
       Charter Schools, ADU’s)

Multi-Family Housing in Commercial Districts - Mr. Bachand explained that HB 1588 and HB
1010 were signed into law this year and have made significant impacts to RSA 674:80 and the
Town’s multi-family ordinance:
    It has been amended to prohibit municipalities from requiring accessory residential parking
      spaces to be garaged.
    Expand the requirement for municipalities to allow mixed-use and multi-family
      development, and better define how a Planning Board can determine whether there is
      adequate infrastructure for those developments and what it may require the applicant to
      provide to prove adequate infrastructure exists.
          o Municipalities shall permit multi-family dwelling on commercially zoned land by
              right, provided that adequate infrastructure is available, in accordance with RSA
              674:80.
          o Municipalities must permit pre-existing nonconforming structures on land in zones
              that permit office and retail commercial uses to be converted to multi-family
              dwelling or mixed-use provided that the “structure’s envelope is not altered to
              further violate the zoning district dimensional requirements”.

Mr. Bachand noted that the Site Plan Review Regulation powers have not been amended.
