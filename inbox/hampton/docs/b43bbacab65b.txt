ONGOING BUSINESS
- COASTAL RESILIENCE:
        • Discussion of Coastal Resilience Incentive Zone (CRIZ) NH RSA 79-E:4-a which
allows us to set up an advisory to allow qualifying projects by meeting the requirements
established by the town. The town determines which projects qualify and to what extent they
qualify for a tax abatement for a substantial rehabilitation. The Selectboard will hear Ben Ritt's
presentation on Monday night, and those details should help clarify a lot of questions for the
public. Need to watch - stay tuned!
Commissioner Kravitz asks if there is a time frame, and is told there will be more detailed
information at the Selectboard Meeting on Monday


- REFINE POSSIBLE AREAS OF COOPERATION WITH DOT / DNCR:
Chair notes that this study was done 3 years ago but there are some suggestions to work on...
       • ADA Accessibility - discussion - the parking spaces nearest the ocean at the bandshell
       could be better utilized
       • Wayfinding Signage - discussion - needs improvement - new kiosk posters have better
       points on them - to be discussed
       • Historical Markers - discussion - more needed
        • Dune Management - living shorelines are planned for installation in Sun Valley at the
base of the new bridge to mitigate the erosion


- BRIDGE LIGHTING:
Commissioner Loiseau shared that DOT hired a designer for the lights and the state is obtaining
extended federal funds. A major concern is the relative lifespan of various types of equipment.
There is optimism re getting the funds but there are many styles and lots of res tape. They are
aiming for October of 2027. Plan is that DOT will come up with the options & presents them to
the town (selectboard)
There will be a tour to view the new bridge while the old bridge is still standing for six
representatives of the Heritage Commission, the Hampton Historical Society, HBAC to view the
two bridges while they are both still standing.
Commissioner Preston notes that even though it is not completed yet, the view of the bridge from
the Seabrook side is stunning!
There has been a request to involve the US DOT for a debrief meeting on the NH DOT BUILD
grant application in view of our non-selection for the third time.
Commissioner Watson explains the need to confirm that all details have been addressed, so it
should be a quick turnaround.
Loretta Doughty, 1A project lead engineer, is working on the Finding of Occasion meeting
which is to seek approval from the commission members to proceed to final design with the
alternative from the Public Hearing.

NEW BUSINESS:
       • Economic Development - general discussion of various opportunities for an annex to
the HBAC Master Plan, but there may be some other way to combine funding sources &
opportunities including support for a town economic development committee.
Loiseau reports much work on zoning changes & priorities by MPIC - recommendations are for
improvements in the downtown parking lot and wayfinding signage. Bachand mentioned that
even though the improvements are physically in the downtown they will positively affect the
visitors to the beach as well. Bachand states the effort to coordinate activities that will have an
impact in several ways, satisfying criteria that may appear in several sets of goals simultaneously
and may support a housing and economic summit in early spring of 2027.
Discussion continued re:
- Possible Zoning changes include changing the building height limit & combining the two
business seasonal with one height limit for both
- a late Aug letter was sent in support of Hampton's Opportunity Zone 2.0 Nomination
Application and included the following points:
- public vs. private investment could be a good thing to pursue
- as we transition to more year round living in the future there is a possibility we may need to
consider more comprehensive workforce readiness
- we are now nurturing a positive environment; Police and Fire Depts and State Parks personnel
do an outstanding job
- soon we're going to lose a lot of parking, and we need to keep price of parking to a reasonable
limit District lots were filling early then another fill later in the day
For our next meeting:
look at our meeting schedule - is it meeting our needs?
chair will send out options for discussion ahead of Oct meeting
