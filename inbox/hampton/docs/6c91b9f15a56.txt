ONGOING BUSINESS:
Coastal Resilience Incentive Zone
        (CRIZ) amendment under the town freezes the assessed values in accordance with 79-E:4-a
        - provides tax relief incentive for substantial rehab of properties
        - assure consistency with the Hampton Master Plan
        - tax relief includes those in the coastal resilience zone where the town freezes the assessed value
for 5or more years depending on the property
       - relief may last 5 or 9 years (for historic relevance) then assessment returns to what it was -
sample proposals were distributed demonstrating qualifying properties
        - the town is still working on the proposal for the location of the zone


NEW BUSINESS:
        Ad hoc Economic Development working group
        There is an ad hoc working group addressing economic development and their deliverables are
        due in early December. This group is optimistic and feels that the town is in a good place. Chair
        is willing to represent HBAC but will step aside if another commissioner wants to step in.

        Meeting schedule:
        Choices available for our meeting schedule - do they meet our needs?
        Chair sent out options for discussion ahead of this meeting and discussion ensued,
        conclusion was that it be kept the same
        Bridge opening:
        On Oct 27th there is a one-way soft opening for the winner of a raffle ticket to benefit the
        Hampton Historical Society/Hampton Firefighters "Save Our Sanford" (antique fire
        pumper in need of restoration).
        State Parks reports that the end-of-season review meeting will be held at the State Park
        Pavillion is planned for Tuesday, November 10, 5 - 6:30.
        Commissioner Watson reports that Rte 1A project Report of the Commissioner is being
        completed and the next step is the Finding of Occasion meeting. Once that is complete,
        hopefully by the end of the year, the design process can begin in the new year. Hampton
        DPW will work with NH DPW
        Election of officers:
