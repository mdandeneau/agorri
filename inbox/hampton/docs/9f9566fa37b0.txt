The meeting/tour started at 10:00 a.m. at the Hampton Police Department 100 Brown Ave. The
Committee toured the Police Department and met with Captain Azarian. Committee members
asked questions regarding staffing, capital improvements, and building maintenance needs.
Correction to address made 10/20 and approved by Committee

Captain Azarian reported that full-time staffing is currently at 37 of 39 positions, with one offer
pending. Part-time staffing continues to be a challenge, with 20 of 70 positions filled, although
offers will be extended to several individuals. The department will continue to advertise
available positions on its website. Training for part-time officers is provided through the
Academy, which requires a minimum of 10 participants from across the state to hold a class. If
fewer than 10 participants enroll, the class is not held. Once recruits complete their Academy
training, the Hampton Police Department provides additional training for the new officers.

Capital improvements are needed for the 20-plus-year-old building. One of the major safety
concerns is the need to clean the ductwork, which has never been done.

The tour ended at 10:55 a.m.
