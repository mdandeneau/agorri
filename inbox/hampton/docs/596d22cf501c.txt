HAMPTON BEACH VILLAGE DISTRICT
                                   MEETING
                                October 14, 2026


Present
Chuck Rage, Chairman & Commissioner
Maureen Buckley, Commissioner
Robert Ladd, Commissioner

The meeting was called to order at 5:30 p.m.

The Pledge of Allegiance was said.

Chairman Rage asked for a Moment of Silence for Bev Hollingsworth who recently passed
away. Ms. Hollingsworth was a State Senator and did great work. The Commissioners
decided it would be a very good thing to have a bench dedicated to her.

Moved by Commissioner Ladd, seconded by Commissioner Buckley, to purchase a bench
dedicated to Bev Hollingsworth.

Vote: 3 yes, 0 no. Motion passed unanimously.

Chamber of Commerce

John Nyhan, Director Hampton Chamber of Commerce, said he would give an overview of
marketing in 2026. The focus has been keeping Hampton Beach visible. Advertising was
exceptional. We had a contract with Heart Company through WMUR and they did two
major projects for us. A story they formatted drew over one million clicks.

There was an increase in Canadian visitors this summer. Our group continued promoting
the large events such as the sand sculpture and the Country Music Festival.

Mr. Nyhan presented a complete breakout of every penny given to the Chamber by the
Village District. Mr. Nyhan said they are now looking forward to 2027 and would be
honored to present their proposal at the November meeting. Mr. Nyhan said they would
hope to have the proposal approved in December and be on the ballot in March.

Mr. Nyhan said this has been a successful advertising partnership. Mr. Nyhan said they also
have a program where the hotels, etc. ask people how they heard about Hampton Beach.
Mr. Nyhan said they believe they have a solid foundation for 2027.

Commissioner Ladd said he would like to compliment the Chamber. This has been a very
difficult summer with the tariffs, gas prices, etc.

Richard Reniere asked how the revenue from entrance fees for the Seafood Festival is
distributed. Mr. Nyhan said the cost to run the festival this year was $600,000. It is difficult
because 30% to 40% of people there are just walk-ins who did not pay.

Marketing

Lisa Martineau, Co-Marketing Director, spoke about the winner of the Photo Contest. The
survey has been posted. Ms. Martineau said this year’s Fire Show was the best she has seen.
A problem with the Light Show was that people could not see. Everyone agreed to think
about this and come to some conclusions. Ms. Martineau said she is working on next year’s
events. She is starting to update the website. She said there will be a Chili Cookoff on
November 19th sponsored by the Fire Fighters.

Entertainment

Amanda Morneault, Entertainment Director, thanked everyone for a great year. She said
she has quite a few people booked for next year. There will be one tribute show every week
in the season.

Old Business

There was no Old Business.

New Business

Chairman Rage said there will be a public hearing on Monday at 6:00 p.m. to discuss trash
pick-up.

Public Comment

There was no Public Comment.

Approval of Minutes

Moved by Commissioner Ladd, seconded by Commissioner Buckley, to approve the
Minutes of September 9, 2026.
