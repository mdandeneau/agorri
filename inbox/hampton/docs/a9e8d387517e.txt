The meeting was called to order at 6:00pm by Chair Carnaby. Ms. Carnaby led the Pledge of
Allegiance.

PUBLIC COMMENT. No public present

APPROVAL OF DRAFT MINUTES

       MOVED by Lessard, SECONDED by Carnaby to approve the minutes of the August 13,
       2026 meeting. Approved 6-0-0.

MEMBER RESIGNATION

       Ms. Carnaby announced that Katherine Harake has resigned from her position as
       alternate member. MOVED by Lessard, SECONDED by Bliss to accept her resignation
       with regrets and thank her for her service. APPROVED 6-0-0. Bliss will notify the Select
       Board that we are in need of another alternate member.

ONGOING PROJECT UPDATES & OLD BUSINESS

       HISTORIC HOUSE MARKERS. No applications for new markers. The marker for the
       Blacksmith Shop is ready for installation. Mr. Lessard volunteered to install the sign. He
       and Ms. Carnaby will meet at the Blacksmith Shop to identify the best location. DPW will
       be consulted.


       WHATZIT-WHEREZIT Map of Hampton. Members reported that they had distributed
       copies of the map to various locations throughout town.

BLACKSMITH SHOP.


                                                                                                1
       The Commission continued its discussion concerning the restoration of the Blacksmith
       Shop on Barbour Road. Mr. Morse and Mr. Bliss offered written suggestions on how to
       proceed. The group consensus is to explore the feasibility of an L-CHIP planning grant
       to hire an expert to create a restoration plan and estimate the cost.

       Commission members will reach out to other communities that have historic blacksmith
       shops to learn how they restored their sites and how they currently operate.
       Assignments are: Bushway: Newmarket; Lessard: Brentwood; Bliss: Gilsum and
       Londonderry.

DEMOLITION PERMITS. No new permits.

       The Commission continued its discussion concerning the creation of an oral history of
       the Casino Ballroom before it is demolished. Ms. Carnaby has had discussions with
       Winnacunnet High School about student participation in the project and will follow-up.

ANNOUNCEMENTS & UPCOMING EVENTS
     None reported.

NEW BUSINESS

The next meeting will be on Thursday, October 8 at 6pm in the Select Board Room.

ADJOURNMENT

MOVED by Ms. Bushway, SECONDED by Mr. Morse to adjourn. Vote: 6-0-0. The meeting was
adjourned at 7:22pm.
