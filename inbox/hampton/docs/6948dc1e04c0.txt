   •   Other Potential Warrant Articles
          o RSA 674:18-a - Alternate Procedure for Adoption of Zoning Ordinance
Mr. Pope reviewed the RSA and explained he would like to research it to understand, if it was
passed, must all Zoning amendments then be adopted via Select Board public hearing or could some
be sent to Town Meeting and some be sent to the Select Board.
Mr. Lessard expressed his concerns about it taking away the voting process, especially regarding
citizen petition Zoning amendments. If the Town wants to change its form of government and have
a City Council form of government, then that is the action that should be taken, otherwise we should
keep the process we have.

It was noted that no other towns have adopted this yet.

The Board discussed the idea, and it was the consensus to have Mr. Bachand get legal counsel’s
opinion before proceeding further.

           o Phase III Zoning Studies - Consultant Services for Dimensional Requirements
             Analysis, Suitability for Greater Housing Density Analysis, and Form Based Code
Phase III Zoning Improvement Project Warrant Article would consist of a dimensional requirements
analysis and a suitability for greater housing analysis and the third item would be the corresponding
Zoning changes. The wording for the Warrant Article will need to be drafted soon. The Board was
provided with three examples of a Build-Out Analysis (Fremont, Plaistow, and Rye).
    • Planning Board Member Reports (re: Select Board, Coastal Hazards Adaptation Team)
Select Board - Mr. Grip noted the following from the most recent Select Board meeting:
    • Public Hearing concerning commercial trash pick up was held. The issue will be revisited at
       their next meeting.
    • Voted to accept monies from the Federal Government under the Swift Current Grant to raise
       four houses in the special flood hazard zone.
    • Voted to accept congressionally directed grant through the Pre-Disaster Mitigation from
       Homeland Security.
    • Held first hearing on the request to remove the fence height deed restriction at 1 12th Street.
    • Approved Chief Reno’s request to extend the Public Information Officer pilot project at
       part-time through the first quarter of 2027.
    • Heard from DPW Director Hale about a budget request. Approved an expenditure from the
       Wastewater Development Charge account for the replacement of the existing Bar Screen
       Programmable Logic Controller.
    • Authorized Director Hale to proceed with the removal of the diagonal parking spaced on
       Lafayette Road.
    • Authorized the sale of leased land at 5 Epping Ave.
    • Heard a report on the meeting to apply for the UNH Cooperative Extension Community
       Business Engagement (CBE) Program.
    • Solar energy landfill project – Kearsarge Energy has received approval for connection from
       Unitil.
    • Agreed to set up a meeting of the Board of Health.
