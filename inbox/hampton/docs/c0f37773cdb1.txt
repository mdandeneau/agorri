    2. Amend Article 1 - General Provisions, Section 1.5 Definitions and Article 5 - General Use
       Regulations, Section 5.2 Use Definitions and Requirements to comprehensively address
       regulatory language and organizational issues that could not be completed as part of the (non-
       substantive) May 2025 Zoning Ordinance Reorganization. While these changes are mostly
       “housekeeping” in nature, definitions have been added to Section 1.5 for enumerated uses that
       were previously undefined, and use requirements have been added to Section 5.2 for uses that
       were only defined. The New Hampshire Revised Statutes Annotated (RSA’s) were referenced,
       and corresponding language was incorporated as needed to ensure conformance. The
       overarching intent is to relocate all regulatory standards currently within the definitions section
       to the use requirements section, and to provide greater clarity and consistency between the two
       sections, which upon adoption would conclude the reorganizational work associated with the
       Town’s ongoing three-phase Zoning Ordinance improvement project.
