3:30pm Vice Chairman Morgan Hollis—Calls the meeting to order.

Ken Lennox presents “Notice of Meeting” to Morgan Hollis (Vice Chairman).

3:32pm The Turnstone team is here to discuss the new building timeline, plans and budget.

Aaron G. informs the Trustees that the fencing for the building will arrive Friday and demolition is
slated to start on Monday 11/2.   The abatement work has been completed.

Trailers are all installed and work fine for both the office/breakroom and storage.

Tony D. speaks to the Trustees about the leveling sheets.  How to read them and what they are for
and when we will receive them.

The first Leveling sheet we received is for Geo Tech Testing Services.  This will be a direct relationship
with the Cemetery.  Turnstone has recommended SW Cole for these services.

Andy A. motions to accept the recommendation to go with SW Cole at the prices presented or below
subject to Carl A and Ken calling SW Cole to try and reduce some of the pricing.
2nd by Carl A.
Motion Passed.

Morgan proposes to give Ken L the proper authority to sign off on recommended subcontractors.

Carl A. motions to allow Ken L to sign off on subcontractors.
2nd by Andy A.
Motion Passed.

Tony D. goes over budget with the Trustees.  He addresses any area that either went up or down on
price.
We discussed the paving.  The Trustees agree to add the 2 Alternative Add locations for paving near
the new parking area and near the circle North of the Greenhouse.
The team also discusses the thickness of the Gravel and Paving of the new road.  The team decides
to go with 12” of Gravel and 2 ½ inches of Paving.

Doug G motions to do the additional areas of paving (reference Alt #1 on budget sheet) and the
Option of 12” of Gravel and 2 ½” of Pavement (reference Alt #1A and 1B on budget sheet)

2nd by Carl A.
Motion Passed.

4:10pm Ken L. discusses paving areas at both Evergreen ($2500) and Edgewood ($2000).  Crisp
contracting is the preferred bidder.

Carl A. motions to have Crisp Contracting pave areas at Evergreen for $2500.00 and at Edgewood for
$2000.00 to be taken out of the Whitman Fund and Jackman Fund respectively.
2nd by Andy A.
Motion Passed.
