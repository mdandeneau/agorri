To: Board of Aldermen
From: Liz Hannum, Economic Development Director
MEMO Re: Disposition of 342 Main Street


The City recently issued a Request for Proposals for the purchase and redevelopment of 342 Main Street, a
tax-deeded property owned by the City. Prior to issuance of the RFP, the City completed all required notices
and procedures associated with disposition of the property, including providing any parties with applicable
repurchase rights the opportunity to exercise those rights.

The City received one proposal in response to the RFP, submitted by Habitat for Humanity. Habitat
proposes to purchase the property and construct an owner-occupied duplex, creating two new affordable
homeownership opportunities at the site.

Following review of the proposal, the City is recommending award of the project to Habitat for Humanity,
subject to approval by the Board of Aldermen. The intent to award letter is attached. The proposed
redevelopment will return a tax-deeded property to productive use, add new housing, support
homeownership opportunities, and return the property to the tax rolls.

City staff will bring a Purchase and Sale and Master Development Agreement in the coming months for your
approval.
