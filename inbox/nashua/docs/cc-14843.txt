                 Community Services Department Highlights
                    •  Community Health Workers (CHWs): Reported 831 client interactions during the
                       reporting period, involving various types of services, including job application
                       assistance. In one case, a family lost a member who had been paying the
                       household bills, resulting in utility expenses exceeding $22,000. Staff worked with
                       utility companies, enrolled the family in assistance programs, and connected them
                       with a church payee to help manage payments.
                    •  Housing Services Officer (HSO): Increased the number of clients receiving Tenant-
                       Based Rental Assistance (TBRA) due to housing instability.
                    •  Public Health Emergency Preparedness (PHEP): Staff completed CPR instructor
                       training and will provide training to other staff members.
                    •  Resource Center staff have been hired, and planning for the warming station
                       continues.
