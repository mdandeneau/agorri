6. Mobility Management Presentation
Donna from the Nashua Regional Planning Commission presented information about mobility
management and regional transportation resources.
Purpose of Mobility Management    •  Mobility management focuses on connecting people with transportation so they can
      reach essential destinations.
   •  Transportation needs may include:
         o  Medical appointments
         o  School
         o  Shopping
         o  Employment
         o  Other essential community services
   •  Particular attention is given to seniors, people with disabilities, low-income residents,
      and culturally diverse communities.
   •  Mobility managers help identify available transportation programs and connect
      residents with appropriate services.
Regional Coordination
   •  The Nashua Regional Planning Commission works with 13 communities in the Greater
      Nashua region.
   •  Each region of New Hampshire has a mobility manager.
   •  Residents who need transportation outside the Greater Nashua area may be connected
      with mobility managers in other regions.
   •  The Regional Coordinating Council helps coordinate transportation resources and
      programs.
   •  The next regional transportation meeting is scheduled for December 10.
Nashua Transit Resources
   •  Nashua Transit System continues to be an important local transportation resource.
   •  Two primary forms of service were discussed:
         o  Fixed-route bus service
         o  Paratransit service
   •  Paratransit is available to eligible seniors and people with disabilities and requires an
      application.
   •  Riders generally need to schedule paratransit service in advance.
   •  Individuals age 60 and older may qualify for free bus service.
   •  Nashua Transit also offers travel training to help new riders learn how to use the bus
      system.
   •  Committee members were encouraged to share information about travel training with
      immigrant and newcomer communities.
Volunteer Driver Program
   •  A volunteer-driver transportation program is available through Catholic Charities.
   •  Riders must meet eligibility requirements.
   •  Transportation depends partly on the availability of volunteer drivers.    •  Committee members were encouraged to:
         o  Refer eligible residents who need transportation
         o  Encourage community members who have time to consider becoming volunteer
            drivers.
Gate City Bike Co-op
   •  The Gate City Bike Co-op refurbishes donated bicycles and provides them to eligible low-
      income residents.
   •  The program may currently have additional bicycles available.
   •  Residents interested in receiving a bike may obtain a voucher through the mobility-
      management contact.
   •  Bikes are ﬁtted to each recipient and may also include a helmet, lights, and a lock.
   •  Children's bicycles are also available.
   •  The program is currently looking for a larger or more suitable space for bicycle storage
      and repair workshops.
   •  Rivier University or other local organizations may have potential space that could be
      explored.
   •  Committee members were asked to share possible facility leads.
Commute Smart and Other Transportation Resources
   •  Commute Smart was discussed as a rideshare option that may help residents connect
      with people traveling in the same direction.
   •  The program may help reduce transportation costs, particularly when fuel prices are
      high.
   •  Committee members were encouraged to share the resource with their networks.
Transportation for Opioid Use Disorder
   •  A transportation program is available for individuals with opioid use disorder who need
      transportation to treatment.
   •  Assistance may include:
         o  Bus passes
         o  Transportation to Manchester
         o  Other transportation options
   •  Committee members were encouraged to share information about this service because
      there is signiﬁcant community need.
Transportation Referrals
   •  Residents who do not qualify under opioid-related transportation funding may still be
      referred for assistance.
   •  Other transportation funding may be available based on factors such as:
         o  Age
         o  Disability          o  Medical condition
         o  Financial circumstances
         o  Veteran status
         o  Other program eligibility
   •  Committee members were encouraged to refer anyone with transportation needs so the
      mobility-management program can determine what resources may be available.

7. Multicultural Festival Debrief
The committee reviewed the October Multicultural Festival and discussed successes, challenges,
and lessons for future events.
Weather and Emergency Preparedness
   •  The original festival date had to be canceled because of weather concerns.
   •  This situation highlighted the need for a formal emergency-preparedness protocol.
   •  A protocol was developed in coordination with the City and Mayor's Office.
   •  Future events will follow the City's emergency-preparedness procedures for weather and
      other safety situations.
Rescheduling and Food
   •  The festival was rescheduled to October 3.
   •  The rescheduled date created challenges because:
         o  Other festivals were taking place on the same day.
         o  Many food trucks were unavailable.
   •  Instead of canceling the event, organizers returned to an earlier festival model using
      food samples from local restaurants.
   •  Environmental Health agreed to expedite restaurant applications under the
      circumstances.
   •  The food-sampling approach was considered highly successful and received positive
      feedback.
   •  Committee members expressed interest in continuing the food-sample model at future
      festivals.
Attendance and Overall Success
   •  Festival attendance exceeded expectations.
   •  Organizers estimated more than 2,000 attendees between noon and 5:00 p.m., with
      overall daily attendance estimated at more than 4,000.
   •  The weather on the rescheduled date was excellent.
   •  Overall community feedback was very positive.
   •  Organizers considered the festival successful despite the last-minute date change and
      logistical challenges.
Performers    •  Most performances went smoothly.
   •  Two groups experienced significant delays, partly because of parking and difficulty
      navigating the new venue.
   •  Some scheduled Indian Association performers were unable to perform because they
      arrived too late.
   •  Future performer communications should include clearer information about:
         o  Parking
         o  Arrival procedures
         o  Check-in locations
         o  Venue navigation
   •  The committee noted that leadership changes caused by the rescheduled date also
      contributed to communication difficulties.
Volunteers and Festival Operations
   •  Volunteers received very positive feedback.
   •  Volunteers from community organizations and local schools played an important role in:
         o  Food service
         o  Festival setup
         o  Table coverage
         o  Decorations
         o  General event support
   •  DPW's delivery of tables was considered essential to the success of the festival.
   •  The committee noted that future events should continue coordinating with DPW for
      tables whenever possible.
Parking and Accessibility
   •  Parking was one of the main challenges at the new venue.
   •  Attendees with mobility limitations had difficulty because accessible parking filled
      quickly.
   •  Some parking options required attendees to cross a busy road or walk a significant
      distance.
   •  Vendors and performers also had difficulty unloading equipment because of limited
      vehicle access.
   •  Possible improvements discussed included:
         o  Better accessible-parking arrangements
         o  Shuttle transportation
         o  Use of paratransit or other transportation support
         o  Clearer parking instructions before the event.
Signage and Check-In
   •  The committee identified signage as another area for improvement.    •  Because the venue was new and had multiple entrances, attendees, performers,
      sponsors, and community organizations sometimes had difficulty knowing where to go.
   •  Future festivals should consider:
         o  More directional signs
         o  Table banners
         o  Clearly marked check-in areas
         o  Better communication regarding where different groups should report.
Children's Activities and Cultural Displays
   •  The international flag displays were well received and helped create a welcoming
      multicultural atmosphere.
   •  The new flag scavenger hunt was considered successful.
   •  Children received passport-style activity sheets and visited different country displays to
      collect stamps and learn cultural facts.
   •  The children's area also included activities offered by community organizations,
      including art and cultural activities.
   •  Feedback from participants indicated that the interactive activities added significant
      value to the festival experience.
Stage and Vendor Area Layout
   •  One concern with the venue was that the performance stage and community/vendor
      areas felt separated.
   •  People working at tables were sometimes unable to see the performances.
   •  Committee members discussed possible improvements, including:
         o  More announcements from the DJ
         o  Better coordination of the performance schedule
         o  Planned breaks to encourage attendees to move toward the stage
         o  Reconsidering the overall site layout
   •  The committee agreed that the venue has both significant advantages and challenges
      and should be evaluated carefully before deciding whether to use it again.
Future Festival Planning Committee

   •  Meeting times may need to change because Friday aﬅernoon meetings do not work for
      all interested members.
   •  Members interested in helping with the 2027 festival were encouraged to participate.
   •  One member volunteered photography support for future events.
