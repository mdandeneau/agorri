                Ambulance Advisory Committee Meeting Minutes
                       Thursday, October 15, 2026
     Meeting held at 177 Lake Street in the 2nd Floor Conference Room
       Co-Chaired by Bobbie Bagley and Fire Chief Steve M. Buxton

The meeting was called to order at 3:00 p.m. by Assistant Chief Adam Pouliot.

AAC Members Present:

Bobbie Bagley (HCS), Adam Pouliot (NFR),Chris McCarthy (PHEPC), Clark

AMR Representatives:

   •  Jason Preston
   •  Al Gardella


Others Present:

Jessica Connors Housing Services Officer, City of Nashua Division of Public
Health & Community Services

Approval of Previous Minutes:

   •  A motion was made by Stacey Carroll to accept the August 2026 meeting
      minutes.
   •  The motion was seconded by Kevin Blinn.
   •  The minutes were approved.

AMR Reports:

   •  Jason Preston presented the AMR activity report thru September & noted
      that September was the slowest month of the year so far with 1,050 calls.
   •  Jason Preston presented the CQI summary report provided by AMR.
   •  Question and discussion regarding the number of call types listed as
      “unknown “on the report.
   •  The AMR reports were accepted.


        Hospital Reports:

   •  Nothing new to report from either hospital.

Committee Member Comments:

   •  Public Health reports that respiratory infections are up, and waste water
      testing indicates high levels of COVID. (Neither hospital is seeing any
      increase in COVID patients)
   •  NFR discussed increasing use of 911 requests for ambulances from several
      medical facilities/ urgent cares in the City. The feeling is that many of the
      patients are stable and should be utilizing an interfacility transfer ambulance
      versus the 911 system.  This warrants further discussion with both hospitals
      and urgent care facilities. No action to be taken at this time.
   •  Also discussed a 911 call from the freestanding ER on Amherst Street.
      Patient needed blood and the Freestanding ER does not have blood
      products on site. Wanted to make sure everyone was aware of this.

Public Comment:

   •  Jessica Connors (Housing Services Officer for the City), advised that they
      had a soft opening for the new resource center on High Street on October
      5th. The purpose of the center is to help the unhoused with services. This is
      not a shelter and there is no laundry, bathing or sleeping facilities.

Adjourn:

   •  A motion to adjourn was made by Kevin Blinn.
   •  The motion was seconded by Stacey Carroll.
   •  The meeting was adjourned at 3:25 p.m.

Date and Location of the Next AAC Meeting:

      Lake Street.
