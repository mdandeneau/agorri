Plans for upcoming work & meetings (copied from prior minutes):
– Continue work on co-occurrence mapping & scoring to help set priorities for conservation in the two towns.
    We had discussed taking the final categories & beginning to…
        1) Determine which categories & sub-categories we want to definitely include; try to make it as simple as
             possible, with fewer layers.
        2) Then, with GIS expert contracted help, we will determine what layers have mapping data / layers online
        3) Then figure out weighting and scoring amongst the categories & subcategories
        4) Finish building data layers
        5) Try applying our developing new system to 2 potential conservation properties in Tamworth & 1 in
             Sandwich that landowners have discussed easements on.
    Items identified for discussion:
        • David notes that USVLT does not cover Tamworth & Sandwich, so their co-occurrence mapping does
             not have layers for our towns, so we’ll have to build the layers ourselves. And he is not sure about what
             data layers we may be able to find for climate resiliency, etc. LRCT may be able to share some layers
             for our towns.
         •   Consider having an overall top focus be on preserving the mosaic of habitat types; doing that protects
             wildlife inherently, meaning that we may not need specific data layers for particular animals or plants –
             they come with the habitat
         •   Consider using a flowchart matrix in the assessment process…. For example, if there are top
             overarching priorities that outweigh all others, such as shoreland protection, then the flow chart will ask
             the question “Does this easement/purchase protect significant water frontage/shoreland? If yes,
             proceed with the project and only cursorily document other values.”
         •    For each resource, what % in each town is already protected? If, say, 70% of a resource is already
              protected, then that may mean we don’t need to include that layer/resource in our strategy, in our
              priori>za>on.
