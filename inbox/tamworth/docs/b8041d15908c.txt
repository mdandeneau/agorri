F.        Old Business:

A report was given by Polly Mahoney, regarding a conference she attended in St. Louis. The
purpose was to bring a lot of librarians, directors, staff and some board members from rural
areas to share ideas. The theme was 250 – Telling America’s Story. This was the 6th gathering
they had held this year. She emphasized the connections that were made. Polly gained a huge
appreciation for Tamworth, and how the Library is supported by the Town. Others spend a lot
of time fundraising for their libraries. She came back with a lot of programming ideas, and ways
to deepen the work that we are doing here. One thing she brought back with her is Seeing a
mother read is more indicative of academic performance. There is a way to show parents how
to read to their children. Literacy for adults in our community is something to think about.

   1.      2027 Library Budget – see draft 2 – the draft was reviewed. It is not remarkably
           different from Draft 1. Carryover funds will change, but last time was too soon to
           have an idea. The carryover number offsets the Town appropriation. Two grants are
           carrying over – they are mostly programming grants. Mary is considering applying to
           the Tamworth Foundation for funds for a couple of projects she is considering. Re:
           solar – we are at 22% right now, but it will pick back up in January. The budget
           shows a pretty significant increase. There are a couple of places to adjust if needed –
           not to hire, no extra cleaning hours. The other place would be not to do some of the
           projects we are planning on. Library materials line shows a significant jump. Mango
           languages is being added this year. There will also be a children’s version of it.
           Courtney asked if we should ask the Friends for more money. Mary feels that this
           could be a conversation to be held. The Friends started an endowment through the
           Tamworth Foundation. The endowment should bring in approximately $7000 per
           year. A conversation should be held soon in order to learn how to budget for that
           money going forward. Courtney suggests setting up a small meeting with the Friends
           for this discussion. The budget will be taken to the Selectboard in December. Allie
           feels that it is important to keep up with the increases on the full-time salary. We
           have not reached the median level yet. Jennifer would like to know how the 7%
           increase compares to other years. Karl noted that the tax rate can now be set, and
           also spoke about unexpended funds that can offset the taxes. $1.74 is the contract
           price we got for propane. Kevin spoke about the technology budget. The website is
           being transferred to a catalog website. They also spoke about switching to a .gov
           website. This covers security issues. There is a grant that will cover this, as well as
           printing new materials. Mary will find out when the budget will be seen by the
           Selectboard.

   2.      2027 Warrant Article for History/Archive Room window removal and heat pumps -
           $52,000. This is to mitigate the risk of flood, and provide some dehumidification
           through the heat pumps. USDA Facilities has a grant that may apply to this. She feels
           we need better estimates and a better idea of exactly what is needed down there.
           The grant would provide between 55 and 75% of the cost of the program. Also, the
             propane tank that is underground is over 25 years old, and will need to be addressed
             at some point. Mary is going to call the USDA rep. to chat about these projects.
