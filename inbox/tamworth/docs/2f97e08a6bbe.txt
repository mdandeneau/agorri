Title: Minutes: Conservation Commission Meeting - Minutes Draft
Fetched: 2026-11-24

Tamworth Conservation Commission
             Draft Minutes – November 09, 2026, Tamworth Town Office & online
             Draft minutes to be reviewed and approved at a subsequent meeting.
                                  Action items underlined.


Call to Order – Kit Morgan called the meeting to order at 7:04 pm. Commissioners present were
       Linda Medeiros, Jeremy Phillips, Kerryn Hullstrung (left early), Dylan Alden and Charlie
       Townsend. Also present: Lucy Gatchell (alternate via Zoom), Kelly Goodson (alternate
       via Zoom), and Anna Paddock. Two absentees: Ned Beecher and Richard Doucette.

Approval of Minutes of October 19, 2026, meeting: Jeremy moved to accept as presented, 2nd
     by Linda. All approved. Charlie abstained.

                                What's happening in nature?
   •   Commissioners and attendees shared recent wildlife observations including a red-
       shouldered hawk, deer, bluebirds, and a flock of approximately 20 robins. Commissioners
       discussed snow forecast for Friday.

                              Administrative and Announcements
   •   Charlie will coordinate the quarterly landfill monitoring with Ned.
   •   Britt reviewed the Member Handbook webpage and links. PDF documents were replaced
       with links to the corresponding webpages to provide access to the most current
       information as resources are updated. Additional updates will be made following
       finalization of the Master Plan in the spring.

                                       Financial – Charlie
   •   As of the end of last month, $4,030 remained of the $7,380 total budget.
   •   Discussed an estimated annual cost of $2,000 for Landscape software; pricing has not
       been confirmed. No decisions were made. The group will continue exploring the options
       and consider potential costs for next year's budget.
   •   Charlie discussed preparing a budget worksheet/template for review at an upcoming
       meeting.
   •   Approximately $1,000 is anticipated for water quality monitoring at Green Mtn. We have
       not received the invoice yet.

                                 Representative Reports – Anna
   •   Anna reported a few applications had been received, but only one was approved: a
       subdivision application on Chinook Trail (113A), located along the river. The subdivision
       involved two lots, one with a cabin and the other with a house.
   •   Fall inspection reports for White Lake Woods and Baron Trust were received. Both
       properties looked good, and all items passed inspection.
•   Master Plan update: Over 400 responses were received through the online survey, and
    approx. 50 people attended the community poster session. Another session focused on
    transportation is scheduled for November 19 at 6:00pm at the school. Following that
    session, the Lakes Region Planning Commission will compile the survey responses and
    other input and use its template to develop a draft plan, anticipated in January or
    February. The draft will then go through a few rounds of revisions, with the goal of
    completing the plan in the Spring.

                    Bylaws & Member Handbook Ad-hoc Committee
•   Reviewed proposed bylaw updates and highlighted items needing input from the full
    Commission.
        o Discussed whether membership should require Town residency or taxpayer status,
            and whether to limit the number of alternates. Both questions need further review.
        o Agreed to retain the associate member language; associate members do not have
            voting rights.
        o Discussed membership and voting status for Select Board and Planning Board
            representatives. Clarification is needed on whether the Planning Board should
            determine its representative’s membership status.
        o Clarified that a paid Town employee serving as Recording Clerk cannot also be a
            Commission member; an unpaid member may serve in that role.
        o Discussed Robert’s Rules of Order and whether to include a summary. Also
            discussed clarifying motions before votes and using the same roll-call procedure
            for in-person and virtual meetings, with any member able to request a roll-call
            vote.
        o Discussed placing email and computer guidelines, including Right to Know Law
            precautions, in the Member Handbook rather than the bylaws.
•   Linda will provide a revised draft for the Commission to review. The highlighted items
    will be revisited, with the goal of bringing proposed changes forward for a vote in
    December.

                Conservation Easements/Land Protection/ CLRC
•   CLRC meets tomorrow 11/10

                                   Easement Monitoring
•   Holden 1: Follow up on previously reported bulldozing on the property. The landowner
    has since walked the area for the first time and is looking into having the boundary
    surveyed, with help from a nephew who is a surveyor. The landowner asked about
    contacting the abutter and whether the disturbed area should be rehabilitated.
    Commissioners agreed that the boundary will first be confirmed and clearly marked.
•   Gilman Forest: Charlie is working through property monitoring.
•   Commissioners reviewed Ned’s draft easement map and discussed how it provides a
    helpful overview of conservation lands and distinguishes Commission-managed
    properties from other Tamworth conservation lands, but the boundaries are approximate
    and some labels are difficult to read. The map does not show public access, parking, or
    trails. Commissioners discussed making property labels easier to read and potentially
       providing a separate map or information link on our website for each property with
       location and access details, which would require a lot of work. Further discussion is
       planned for December.

                                   Managed Lands & Trails
   •   Nothing to report from Dylan.
   •   Lorna Gordon request – Waterfowl Haven access – Ned passed this matter to the Select
       Board; nothing further for now.

                                 Public Education & Outreach
   •   Lucy reported that the Fire Tower ‘no graffiti’ sign must’ve blown away, and some
       graffiti had appeared inside the tower. A replacement sign will be printed, with discussion
       about more securely attaching it.
   •   Commissioners discussed feedback from visitors regarding the removal of the panoramic
       pictures and whether they should be replaced. Due to tree growth obstructing some
       views, a mountain illustration was discussed as a possible alternative with PeakFinder as
       a resource. A QR code was also discussed, although one visitor noted they intentionally
       left their phone behind.
                                     Wetlands & Shorelands
   •   One Permit by Notification was received for Fish and Game forestry/logging activity by
       Hackett Hill.
                                          Other Business
   •   Jeremy and Ned visited a potential bridge location near the Recreation Field for a
       connection to the Earl Remick Trail. The location is within wetlands and would require
       permitting. Commissioners discussed the permitting process and construction challenges.

Vote to adjourn: Motion by Kit, second by Jeremy, all approved at 8:32pm.
