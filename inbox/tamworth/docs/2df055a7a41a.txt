G.      Library Director’s Report:
     1.     Programs: See September newsletter for this month’s programs. Amy will hold
            Bookworms, Bibliomaniacs, and the Wild Scientist kids’ book clubs in the next few
            weeks. Upcoming adult programs include Paul King on Abenaki Archaeoastronomical
            site in Ossipee, a New Hampshire Humanities program on AI and Ethics, pizza and
            writing limericks with author Jean Diehl, a Winnie-the-Pooh 100th birthday reading
            for all ages. One Book One Valley will take place on Sunday, November 1 this year in
            a new location, the Pub on Page Hill. The book this year is Spectacular Things.
     2.     Outreach: Two Little Free Libraries have been opened in Tamworth thanks to the
            sponsorship of the Friends of Cook Memorial Library in partnership with the Union
            Hall Association and the Sokokis Pines Homeowners Association.
     3.     Staff: Amy will attend the New England Library Association conference in Vermont
            next month. She will be cofacilitating a discussion on Storytime Innovation. Polly will
            attend the IMLS Library Convening 250 in St. Louis next month. All expenses are
            covered by IMLS. Mary will attend a five-part series of online masterclasses, Voices
            Shaping the Profession, on the future of libraries offered by Library Journal and
            University of Texas at Austin. There are a lot of AI classes going on right now.

            ** Kristel noted that she is impressed with our library keeping on top of all the AI
            courses. She loves our little library. **
