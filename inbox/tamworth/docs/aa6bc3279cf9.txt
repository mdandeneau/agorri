TAMWORTH FIRE/RESCUE DEPARTMENT
                               Board of Firewards Meeting

Firewards Present: J. Bowles, M. Baumann, J. Hartley, S. Riley, A. Brown (virtual)

Public Present: None

Meeting Opened at 19:37 by Chairman Bowles

Minutes: The Board reviewed the August 6, 2026 minutes. J. Hartley made a motion to
accept with a spelling correction, M. Baumann seconded, all were in favor.

Old Business:
D. Costello’s father-in-law, Michael Ready, has looked at the situation with the side
electrical outlets at Chocorua station and will do the work as a donation if the Department
pays for the materials. The Board agreed to have Michael go ahead with the work.

Chief Colcord is still planning to get insulation and materials to fix the ceiling above the
rescue office.

Since he was accepted as a member last month, the Board reminded Andrew Brown that
he needs to go to the Town Office to do paperwork.

Chief Colcord has received Eric Gagne’s paperwork, is waiting for results of Tarndra
Gagne’s background check, and has not heard back from Gavin Glencross.

D. Costello still needs to hitch up the other siren speaker on E1.

Reviewed and made a couple minor edits to the draft wording for an SOG for Education
and Training Tuition Assistance that S. Riley presented last month. The Board will wait
for Chief Colcord to put it into the standard SOG format before approving.

Chief Colcord has not completed the additional background check of Verne (TJ) Carey.

The Hallowell dry hydrant installation on Chocorua Lake Rd. is scheduled for tomorrow
9/4/26.

No word on progress of getting equipment back from several former members.

New Business:
M. Baumann & D. Costello will handle EMS coverage for the bicycle race on 8/13/26.

A grant application has been submitted to the Tamworth Foundation for money to replace
several truck radios. The Department’s share of this project will be $8,762.00.

Discussed the 2027 budget. Chief Colcord was not at the meeting but had left a copy of
his suggestions. Essentially leave all line items the same as 2026 except: Raise fulltime
and part time salaries 3% for COLA, raise heating fuel $2,500 due to higher fuel prices,
put $5,000 in the dry hydrant line item (only $1.00 was budgeted for 2026) in order to
replenish the stock of materials, and reduce the office supplies line item by $250. The
Board agreed with Chief Colcord’s suggestions.

The Board discussed trying to make some progress on completing Forestry 2. The work
to date has been done by in-house labor but there are some remaining things that we may
need to have done commercially. Things that were mentioned, in order of priority were:
1. Install work lighting on all sides of the truck perhaps similar to what Sandwich has on
their large forestry truck. 2. Fix the issue between the roof mounted warning lights and
the headlights. 3. Upgrade the brake lights, taillights, and headlights, and 4. If there’s
enough money left at the end of 2026, paint the truck.

Chief Colcord’s major surgery is coming up. He will likely be out completely for several
days and then on light duty for approximately five weeks. There was some discussion of
daytime coverage during this period.

There was discussion about truck checks/equipment maintenance. It seems like relying
on the Chief and those that might cover for him on days off and who sometimes assist
him during the day to do truck/equipment checks is not getting everything done. J.
Bowles brought up the idea of getting monthly checks done by assigning individuals on a
quarterly or semi-annual basis, to check the trucks in an assigned station. This idea
seemed to have merit to most people and there was discussion on how to track progress
so that all members would know what was done. S. Riley showed the Board how
Effingham has been using the State’s NERIS software to document truck/equipment
checks. Since we already use this software for tracking incidents, training, and other
activities, members would be able to access the information on their smartphones or
computers. S. Riley will work with D. Costello to make the truck check sheets in NERIS
and then we’ll talk about assigning people to do the checks. D.K. Bowles mentioned that
it may be advantageous to have a clipboard at each station to document when SCBA
batteries were last rotated so it will be obvious.

Adjournment: Motion for adjournment made by M. Baumann at 21:07, seconded by J.
Hartley, all were in favor.


Respectfully submitted,
Jim Bowles, Chairman – Town of Tamworth Board of Firewards
