TAMWORTH FIRE/RESCUE DEPARTMENT
                              Board of Firewards Meeting
                                   October 1, 2026

Firewards Present: J. Bowles, M. Baumann, J. Hartley, S. Riley

Members Present: Capt. D.K. Bowles, FF J. Larocque, Capt. D. Costello

Public Present: None

Meeting Opened at 19:39 by Chairman Bowles

Minutes: The Board reviewed the September 3, 2026 minutes. J. Hartley made a motion
to accept, M. Baumann seconded, all were in favor.

Old Business:
D. Costello advised that the side outlets at Chocorua station should be fixed in the next
month.

Chief Colcord advised that Rick Morreau will fix the ceiling above the rescue office.

Chief Colcord advised that he does not have all the paperwork back yet for new
applicants Tarndra and Eric Gagne and Gavin Glencross. He will be contacting Chief
Huddleston regarding a new background check system.

D. Costello still needs to hitch up the other siren speaker on E1.

The Education and Training Tuition Assistance SOG needs to be put into the standard
format and presented to the Board for approval.

Chief Colcord will complete the background check of Verne (TJ) Carey once he reviews
the new system with Chief Huddleston..

The Hallowell dry hydrant on Chocorua Lake Rd. was installed on 9/4/26. Waiting for
the pond to re-fill to backflush and test.

No word on progress of getting equipment back from several former members.

New Business:
The defective pressure switch on the boiler at Central Station has been fixed. Chief
Colcord will order the new boiler.

Air Ducts Plus will be replacing the leaking zone valve above the meeting room at
Central Station.

Rick Morreau will replace the vinyl siding trim on the rear gable end of the Chocorua
Station.

D.K. Bowles mentioned that Chocorua Lake water level has dropped about a foot and
will likely be varying throughout the fall as repairs are made to the dam and a rock ramp
fishway is constructed next to it. J. Bowles will contact the Chocorua Lake Conservancy
to see if there are any significant drawdowns anticipated.

The grant application that was submitted to the Tamworth Foundation for $10,000.00
towards replacing several truck radios has been approved. The Department’s share of
this project is approximately $8,762.00. It was discussed that the Department’s match
can come out of the equipment line item if necessary.

Chief Colcord will buy a generic cord reel to replace the worn out one from Engine 4.

The AFG grant awards have all been awarded and we did not get the grant for the
portable radios again. It was discussed that we may be able to buy a few new portable
radios with this year’s equipment budget.

It has been confirmed that the 3% COLA for 2026 that was supposed to be applied to the
part-time salaries has not happened. Chief Colcord will ask Dave Cribbie to help him
figure out why the spreadsheet did not update. Also, Chief Colcord will figure out the
required restitution amounts for each member.

Discussed adding more money to the part time firefighters budget line item for more per
diem shift coverage. Since not many members work around town, and those that do
cannot always leave work for calls, there have been times when no one was available and
mutual aid needed to be called. Based on 2026 expenditures to date and the desired
amount of additional coverage, it was decided to add a total additional amount of $25,000
to the 2026 amount. This will bring the 2027 budgeted amount to $120,000 plus 3% for
2026 COLA= $123,600.

Chief Colcord will follow up on completing the work to Forestry 2 that was discussed
last month. Things that were mentioned, in order of priority were: 1.) Install work
lighting on all sides of the truck, perhaps similar to what Sandwich has on their large
forestry truck. 2.) Fix the issue between the roof mounted warning lights and the
headlights. 3.) Upgrade the brake lights, taillights, and headlights, and 4.) If there’s
enough money left at the end of 2026, paint the truck

Reminder - Chief Colcord’s major surgery is coming up. He will likely be out
completely for several days and then on light duty for approximately five weeks. There
was some discussion of daytime coverage during this period.

S. Riley circulated an equipment check sheet that he created for Engine 4, for comments.
