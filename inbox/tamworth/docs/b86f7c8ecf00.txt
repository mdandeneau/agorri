1.     TEC Members and Roles

       T.J. was unanimously appointed Vice Chair of TEC, replacing Ted Morgan.

       The Committee confirmed the current TEC membership:
           Gabrielle Watson – Chair, through 2029
           T.J. McAndrew – Vice Chair, through 2028
           Ted Morgan – Member, through 2027
           John Kumm – Secretary, through 2028
           Peg Loughran – Member, through 2027

       Given the individual starting dates of the members and a nominal 3-year term, there is
       appropriate overlap of terms. The membership was asked to consider other possible
       member candidates.

2.     Select Board Meeting at 6 pm on Wednesday Sept 9th

       Gabrielle will introduce the project and recommend that the SB approve the expenditure,
       and T.J. will present the overall project plan including the summary budget and schedule.
       T.J. will request that the vendor provide monthly payments to the Town for ease of
       accounting. Any surplus funds (currently estimated at $4k+) will go into the General Fund
       account, but could be designated by the SB for possible future Town energy
       improvements, recommended by TEC and approved by the SB.
