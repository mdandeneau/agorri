                  CIP COMMITTEE MEETING/PUBLIC HEARING
                       WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 16, 2026
                          6:00 PM - TOWN OFFICE


   1. Greeting – The meeting was called to order at 6:01pm by Kathi Padgett. Present are:
      Kathi Padgett, Kyle Ball, Nick Grant, Hillary Behr.
      Ann Pollard and Emily Nolan are absent.

       Mary Cronin-Librarian, Jim and Maureen Diamond

Kathi gave an overview of the CIP process.

2. Review/Approval of Minutes of the 8/25/26 Committee Meeting – Nick made a motion to
approve, Kyle seconded. Remove Eleanor’s name from the list, she is not a member of the
committee. APPROVED unanimously

3. Suspension of CIP Committee Meeting/Opening of Public Hearing – The committee meeting
was suspended and the public hearing was opened at 6:06 pm.

4. Review/discussion of Final Draft Proposal and Budget Spreadsheet – Kathi reviewed the
highlights of the plan for 2027.

5. Closing of Public Hearing/Resumption of CIP Committee – The public hearing was closed
and the committee meeting resumed at 6:21 pm.
Kyle made a motion to approve the CIP to send to the Planning Board, Kyle seconded. Hillary

This report will not change on that possibility. APPROVED unanimously.

September 23 – Nick will do the presentation to the Planning Board. Kathi will take it to the
Selectboard on October 13.

enjoyed working with this committee. She will not be continuing her work with this committee
after this year. Nick spoke about how we have missed having John Wheeler and Jack Waldron,
and will miss having Kathi as the Chair next year.

Public Comment – Jim Diamond has been reading about the CIP and was curious how the
meetings ran, what the projections look like, as obviously it is a significant part of our town
budget.

7. Adjournment – Nick made a motion to adjourn at 6:29 pm, Kyle seconded. The meeting was
adjourned.
