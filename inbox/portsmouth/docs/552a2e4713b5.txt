1. Executive Summary & Intent
This proposal details the planned exterior restoration, repair, and site safety improvements for
the residential property located at 357 Maplewood Ave, Portsmouth, NH. The objective of this
project is to preserve and enhance the historic architectural character of the structure through
high-quality, in-kind material replacements, comprehensive exterior refinishing, and necessary
site safety enhancements. All work is designed to maintain historical fidelity while ensuring
long-term structural integrity and compliance with local Historic District Commission guidelines.

3. Detailed Scope of Proposed Work

Siding & Exterior Trim
Exterior repairs to the building include replacing rotted or missing siding with in-kind wood lap
siding. Rotted exterior trim including window/door trim, soffit, rakes, and eaves will be replaced
with matching in-kind wood trim boards. All siding and trim will be fully painted following repair
work. 3’’ reveals the siding will be uniform throughout the siding.


Doors
Replace front door slab with a new wood finish door. Door sizing will remain unchanged; door
slab and jambs to be replaced only, while surrounding trim and transom window above will
remain intact. Additionally, replacing the Studio unit entry door on the right side which is
currently a compromised wood/plexi glass door and the secondary entry door into the main unit
at the rear of the building (noting existing side and rear doors are steel/fiberglass).


Windows
Existing windows are vinyl replacement units and will not be changed; new window screens will
be installed.


Decking & Stairs
Repair rear wood deck, side stairs, and front stairs using in-kind pressure-treated deck boards
and railings where needed. Front Stairs are currently unsafe to use and require railings on each
side to meet current building code.


Masonry & Chimneys
Repoint and repair foundation brick walls, retaining walls, and chimneys. Chimneys will strictly
be repointed and repaired, not removed. Paint foundation wall and front retaining walls with
masonry white paint


Fencing & Site Safety
Remove fence panels on the front of the property and adjacent to the driveway. Replace current
fence on the right side near the retaining wall. We are open to suggestions from the HDC
regarding material type and style to keep the historic integrity while bringing safety to the
property
