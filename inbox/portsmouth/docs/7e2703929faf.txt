      A.      Public Hearing on the Discontinuance of a Portion of Cornwall Street

              CM Memo Cornwall St.pdf

              Cornwall St Legal Notice.pdf

              Cornwall Street Map.pdf
              PRESENTATION
                      Assistant Planning Director Peter Stith presented the reasons for
                      discontinuance of a portion of Cornwall Street.
                  •     CITY COUNCIL QUESTIONS
                      Councilor Moreau asked if the project, that will eventually get built there,
                      could use Cornwall Street to access their property.
                      Mayor McEachern asked for an explanation on what the discontinuance of the
                      street means for the City. Deputy City Attorney Trevor McCourt explained that
                      in 1815, it was a full street with houses until the late 1920s. The way the law is
                      set up, a municipality cannot lose its rights to a street without an affirmative
                      act. It is in the public interest to discontinue the street to allow private
                      development subject to retaining the legal right to maintain a sewer line.
                      Councilor Hopkins asked for clarification of which portion of Cornwall Street
                      is being discontinued.
                      Councilor Tabor asked if this was a voluntary lot merger.
                      Mr. McCourt explained further the history of this parcel.
                  •     PUBLIC HEARING SPEAKERS
                   David Rheaume is in favor of adopting the motion as presented.
                   Stephanie Johnson, Attorney with Hoefle, Phoenix, Gormley and Roberts,
                   spoke on behalf of petitioner, Regan Electric, to discontinue the street.
                   John Chagnon, Engineer, Haley Ward and Associates, is assisting Regan
                   Electric in preparing plans for the discontinuance which would be recorded,
                   and the condition that a sewer easement be granted.
                   Mayor McEachern closed the public hearing.
              Councilor Cook moved to discontinue a portion of Cornwall Street as presented,
              subject to the recording of a utility easement over the existing sewer line in a form
              acceptable to the City Attorney, and the recordation of a voluntary lot merger to
              align the boundaries with the City Tax Map. Seconded by Councilor Tabor.
                                                                                 Motion passed 7-0.

      G.      *Prescott Park Update
              There are three components to the update and the three specific projects that have the
              potential to impact the park. The first is the request of the arts festival to install a
              permanent stage. The second is phase one of the Prescott Park Master Plan, which
              contemplates relocating and elevating three structures within the park for future
              resiliency efforts. The third project is the Mechanic Street Pump Station Project which has
              the potential to impact a portion of the park property on the other side heading toward
              the bridge. City staff has sought the Charitable Trust Unit staff of the NH Attorney
              General's office to give their guidance in reviewing these projects.
              In response to a question asked by Councilor Bagley, the Prescott Park Trust is a single
              trust and, as of October 31, 2026, the balance of the trust was $8.344 million. The City
              does not contribute any funds from the General Fund into the trust. Rather the trust
              contributes annually to the City to help support operations in an amount calculated and
              approved by the Trustees of the Trust Funds during budget time.
              Councilor Tabor thanked City Manager for the follow through on this item. He noted that
              near where the pump station is considered to be going, there is a small section of land
              that was carved out when the road and bridge to Pierce Island was done. Is it park land? Is
              it usable for recreation?
              Councilor Cook asked for a timeframe on hearing back from the Charitable Trust Unit. City
              Deputy City Attorney Trevor McCourt explained there is no timeline. The Mechanic Street
              pump station is a more complex issue.
      H.      *Mechanic Street Pump Station - Second Public Meeting
              The meeting on November 9, 2026, reviewed the proposed preferred pump station
              replacement option, detailed alternative options to move the pump station to Pierce
              Island, and addressed questions raised at the prior public meeting in September.
