LEGAL NOTICE
                                   BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT
                                PORTSMOUTH, NEW HAMPSHIRE

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Board of Adjustment will hold Public Hearings on the
following petitions on Tuesday October 20, 2026 and Tuesday October 27, 2026 starting at 7:00 p.m.
in the Eileen Dondero Foley Council Chambers, City Hall, Municipal Complex, 1 Junkins Avenue,
Portsmouth, New Hampshire.

THE FOLLOWING ITEMS WILL BE HEARD ON TUESDAY, OCTOBER 20, 2026
The request of Ann Marie Wiley Revocable Trust (Owner), for property located at 64 Woodworth
Avenue whereas relief is needed to construct a side addition and front porch to the existing structure
which requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.521 to allow a) 8.5 foot left yard where 10
feet are required, b) 2.5 foot front yard where 30 feet are required, and c) 26% building coverage where
20% is the maximum. Said property is located on Assessor Map 243 Lot 42 and lies within the Single
Residence B (SRB) District.
The request of Ben and Andrea St. Jean, Braden and Robyn Ferrari, Bob and Laura Gunning, Mike
and Zoe Daboul, Tim and Kim Sullivan, and Jamie and Amy Baker (Appellants), for appeal of the
administrative decision of a zoning determination pertaining to the side and rear lot lines of the property
for a proposed subdivision located at 58 Humphrey’s Court. The Board will be considering the remand
from the court on the issue of the property’s rear lot line. Said property is located on Assessor Map 101
Lot 47 and lies within the General Residence B (GRB) and Historic Districts.
The request of Islington Properties LLC (Owner), for property located at 369 Islington Street, Unit
A whereas relief is needed to convert a commercial unit to a residential unit which requires the
following: 1) Variance from Section 10.5A41.10A to allow 918 square feet of lot area per dwelling unit
where 3,000 square feet are required. Said property is located on Assessor Map 144 Lot 22-1 and lies
within Character District 4-L2 (CD4-L2) and the Historic District.
The request of 100 C Albany ST LLC (Owner), for property located at 100 Albany Street, Unit C
whereas relief is needed to establish a dog grooming operation which requires the following: 1) Special
Exception from Section 10.521 Use #7.30 to allow a pet grooming service in a zone where it is allowed
by special exception. Said property is located on Assessor Map 146 Lot 24-3 and lies within Character
District 4-L2 (CD4-L2).
The request of Clever Beaumont Revocable Trust (Owner), for property located at 30 Salem Street
whereas relief is needed to remove the existing 5-foot fence and replace with a new 5-foot fence which
requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.515.13 to allow a 5-foot fence in the front yard area.
Said property is located on Assessor Map 144 Lot 35 and lies within the General Residence C (GRC)
District.
The request of Path Lab Inc C/O Labcorp (Owner), and Lilia Madden, PLLC (Applicant), for
property located at 230 Lafayette Road, Unit 11D whereas relief is needed to allow a medical office
which requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.440 Use #6.20 to allow a medical office
where the use is not allowed. Said property is located on Assessor Map 151 Lot 6-D11 and lies within
the General Residence A (GRA) District.
The request of Colbea Enterprises LLC (Owner), for property located at 1980 Woodbury Avenue
whereas relief is needed for signage which requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.1251.10
to allow for aggregate sign area greater than the maximum of 1.5’ per linear foot of the building frontage
establishment; and 2) Variance from Section 10.1252.40 to allow illumination of lightbars as canopy
sign background distinguished only by color stripes. Said property is located on Assessor Map 239 Lot
11 and lies within the Gateway Corridor (G1) District.

THE FOLLOWING ITEMS WILL BE HEARD ON TUESDAY, OCTOBER 27, 2026
The request of Emilyn Anne and Eben Kennedy (Owners), for property located at 1490 Islington
Street whereas relief is needed for construction of an addition and renovation of a single-family
residence with an accessory dwelling unit to a two-family residence, which requires the following: 1)
Variance from Section 10.440 to allow a two-family dwelling in the SRB District. Said property is
located on Assessor Map 233 Lot 108 and lies within the Single Residence B (SRB) District.
The request of Nathan Alger (Owner), for property located at 104 Thornton Street whereas relief is
needed to demolish an existing carport and deck and construct an addition; and to permit after-the-fact
installation of a fence, which requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.321 to allow a
nonconforming building or structure to be extended, reconstructed or enlarged without conforming to
the requirements of the Ordinance; 2) Variance from Section 10.521 to a) allow a 4' rear yard setback
where 20' is required, b) allow a 6’ secondary front yard setback where 15’ is required, c) to allow
44.5% building coverage where 25% is allowed; and 3) Variance from Section 10.515.13 to allow a
fence exceeding 4' in height with a 0' front yard setback where 15' is required. Said property is located
on Assessor Map 160 Lot 11 and lies within the General Residence A (GRA) District.
The request of Schofield-Kanner Revocable Trust (Owners), and Cain Investments, LLC
(Applicant), for property located at 0 Woodworth Avenue whereas relief is needed to construct a new
single-family residence on a vacant lot, which requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.521
to allow a) lot area of 3,440 SF where 15,000 SF is required, b) lot area per dwelling unit of 3,440 SF
where 15,000 SF is required, c) 40 feet of contiguous street frontage where 100 feet is required, d) lot
depth of 86 feet where 100 feet is required, e) a front yard setback of 17 feet where 30 feet is required, f)
a rear yard setback of 10 feet where 30 feet is required, and g) a building coverage of 35% where 20% is
allowed. Said property is located on Assessor Map 232 Lot 73 and lies within the Single Residence B
(SRB) District.
The request of Peters Family Trust (Owner), for property located at 54 Lincoln Avenue whereas relief
is needed to construct a second-floor accessory dwelling unit over an existing detached garage, which
requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.521 to allow a) a rear yard setback of 10 feet where
20 feet is required; and b) building coverage of 29% where 25% is allowed. Said property is located on
Assessor Map 111 Lot 21 and lies within the General Residence A (GRA) District.
The request of Michael and Stephanie Febonio (Owners), for property located at 9 Sheafe Street
whereas relief is needed to construct an 8-foot fence which requires the following: 1) Variance from
Section 10.515.13 to allow an 8-foot fence in the secondary front yard. Said property is located on
Assessor Map 107 Lot 13 and lies within Character District 4 (CD4) and the Historic District.
