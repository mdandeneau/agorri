     1. Acceptance of Minutes for September 9, 2026 meeting
     2. Tree Removal Requests -- Reminder: interacting with the listed address on a
        computer or smart device will open Google Maps to the location of the tree(s)
        marked for removal.
        • Ledgewood Dr: all trees in the right of way will be removed to develop the lot.
              Species will include pine, maple, cherry, beech, birch, and oak. See plans in
              packet for additional context.
         •    86 Thornton St: 1 spruce, good condition, removal requested by resident. The
              resident is concerned about windthrow and the tree is close to the house. There is
              a slope with surface-level roots that may impact the trees stability.
         •    213 South St: 2 hemlock, poor condition, removal requested by the resident,
              removal recommended by the City. Both trees are exhibiting low vigor with
              thinning canopies and large dead sections.
         •    99 Boyd Rd (on paper road near Garden St): 1 Norway maple, poor condition, on
              paper road, removal requested by resident, removal recommended by the City.
              There is a large torsion wound wrapping around the trunk, and large dead
              branches through the center of the crown.
         •    400 Colonial Dr: 1 little leaf linden, fair condition, removal requested by the
              resident, removal recommended by the City. This large linden has a cavity at the
              base, but there is a great amount of reaction wood forming to seal the defect,
              though the heartwood is significantly rotten. A column of decay appears to
              descend from the union of the two major leaders.
         •    15 Woodworth Ave: 1 silver maple, fair condition, removal requested by the
              resident. A recent storm failure has prompted this request. This tree has been
              pruned by Eversource, and it has a codominant stem with half of the canopy
              weight toward the house.
         •    122 Thaxter Rd: 1 red oak, removal requested by resident. A new driveway
              proposed on Sewall Rd will require the removal of this tree.
         •    17 Thaxter Rd: 2 silver maple, fair condition, removal requested and
              recommended by the City. Eastern jack-o-lantern mushroom is growing in a low,
              decaying union as well as around the base of the tree. At the base of the other
              tree there are several instances of hen of the woods growing in the root zone,

      ME. The tree will be moved on November 17th.
  •   Urban Forestry Ordinance: inclusion and definition of “exigent circumstances”:

      Section 8.309:
      G.
      The Tree Warden shall order the issuance of permits for tree removal only
      after a public hearing is held by the Trees & Public Greenery Committee and
      after the Committee finds that the desired action is necessary and the
      proposed methods and workmanship are satisfactory. Except for exigent
      circumstances, the decisions of the Tree Warden shall be subject to prior
      review and approval by the Trees & Public Greenery Committee, which will
      consider the following: (1) the reason for the request; (2) the health of the tree;
      and (3) the importance of the tree to the streetscape and/or neighborhood.
