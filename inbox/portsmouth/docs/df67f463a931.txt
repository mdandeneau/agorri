I. OLD BUSINESS
    A. Request for Rehearing – 28 Blossom Street


II. NEW BUSINESS

    A. The request of Emilyn Anne and Eben Kennedy (Owners), for property located at 1490
       Islington Street whereas relief is needed for construction of an addition and renovation of
       a single-family residence with an accessory dwelling unit to a two-family residence, which
       requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.440 to allow a two-family dwelling in
       the SRB District. Said property is located on Assessor Map 233 Lot 108 and lies within the
       Single Residence B (SRB) District. (LU-26-126)
    B. The request of Nathan Alger (Owner), for property located at 104 Thornton Street
       whereas relief is needed to demolish an existing carport and deck and construct an addition;
       and to permit after-the-fact installation of a fence, which requires the following: 1)
       Variance from Section 10.321 to allow a nonconforming building or structure to be
       extended, reconstructed or enlarged without conforming to the requirements of the
       Ordinance; 2) Variance from Section 10.521 to a) allow a 4' rear yard setback where 20' is
       required, b) allow a 6’ secondary front yard setback where 15’ is required, c) to allow
       44.5% building coverage where 25% is allowed; and 3) Variance from Section 10.515.13 to
       allow a fence exceeding 4' in height with a 0' front yard setback where 15' is required. Said
       property is located on Assessor Map 160 Lot 11 and lies within the General Residence A
       (GRA) District. (LU-26-120)

    C. The request of Schofield-Kanner Revocable Trust (Owners), and Cain Investments,
       LLC (Applicant), for property located at 0 Woodworth Avenue whereas relief is needed
       to construct a new single-family residence on a vacant lot, which requires the following: 1)
       Variance from Section 10.521 to allow a) lot area of 3,440 SF where 15,000 SF is required,
       b) lot area per dwelling unit of 3,440 SF where 15,000 SF is required, c) 40 feet of
       contiguous street frontage where 100 feet is required, d) lot depth of 86 feet where 100 feet
       is required, e) a front yard setback of 17 feet where 30 feet is required, f) a rear yard
       setback of 10 feet where 30 feet is required, and g) a building coverage of 35% where 20%
       is allowed. Said property is located on Assessor Map 232 Lot 73 and lies within the Single
       Residence B (SRB) District. (LU-26-125)
    D. The request of Peters Family Trust (Owner), for property located at 54 Lincoln Avenue
       whereas relief is needed to construct a second-floor accessory dwelling unit over an
       existing detached garage, which requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.521 to
       allow a) a rear yard setback of 10 feet where 20 feet is required; and b) building coverage
       of 29% where 25% is allowed. Said property is located on Assessor Map 111 Lot 21 and
       lies within the General Residence A (GRA) District. (LU-26-124)
    E. The request of Michael and Stephanie Febonio (Owners), for property located at 9
       Sheafe Street whereas relief is needed to construct an 8-foot fence which requires the
       following: 1) Variance from Section 10.515.13 to allow an 8-foot fence in the secondary
       front yard. Said property is located on Assessor Map 107 Lot 13 and lies within Character
       District 4 (CD4) and the Historic District. (LU-26-123)
    F. The request of 325 Corporate Drive II LLC C/O The Kane Company (Owners), for
       property located at 325 Corporate Drive whereas relief is needed to install two wall signs
       and monument signage, which requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 306.01(d)
       of the Pease Development Ordinance to allow signs to exceed the maximum aggregate area
       of two (2) square feet of sign area for each linear foot of street frontage up to a maximum
       of 200 square feet. Said property is located on Assessor Map 318 Lot 2 and lies within the
       Airport Business Commercial (ABC) District. (LU-26-122)
    G. The request of Rocket Too Trust (Owner), for property located at 10 Merrimac Street
       whereas relief is needed to reconstruct a porte-cochere which requires the following: 1)
       Variance from Section 10.521 to allow a) a rear yard setback of 10 feet where 20 feet is
       required; and b) building coverage of 26% where 25% is allowed. Said property is located
       on Assessor Map 129 Lot 17 and lies within the General Residence A (GRA) District. (LU-
       26-128)
