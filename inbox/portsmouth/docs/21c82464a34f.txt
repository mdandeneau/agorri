C.   Councilor Cook
            Amendment to the Downtown Workforce Parking Program & Request for Report
             Back
                o Expansion of eligibility effective October 1, 2026, for the Downtown
                  Workforce Parking Program (DWPP) to all downtown workers of businesses
                  who previously participated in DWPP who work in hourly clerical, customer
                  service, administrative, and support staff positions until March 1, 2027, and
                  to request a report back from City staff in October on options for businesses
                  that are downtown that might need an off-peak solution for parking during
                  the workweek.
     Councilor Cook moved to amend the RAPP Policy eligibility effective October 1, 2026,
     for the Downtown Workforce Parking Program (DWPP) to all downtown workers of
     businesses who previously participated in DWPP who work in clerical, customer service,
     administrative, and support staff positions until March 1, 2027). Seconded by Councilor
     Moreau.
     Councilor Bagley asked if this is something we can accomplish easily.
     There was discussion about hourly vs. salaried workers.
     Councilor Hopkins would like to know who really qualifies for this program.
     Councilor Bagley spoke about the reason to strike the word hourly.
     Mayor McEachern would like to know the businesses that are taking advantage of this
     program.
     Councilor Hopkins would like to recuse herself from this motion because of a family
     member who works downtown.
                                    Motion passed 7-0. Councilor Hopkins abstained.
     Councilor Cook moved to request a report back from City staff in October on options for
     businesses that are downtown that might need an off-peak solution for parking during
     the workweek. Seconded by Councilor Moreau.
     Councilor Bagley supports this motion.
