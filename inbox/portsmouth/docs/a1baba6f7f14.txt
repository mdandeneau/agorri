PUBLIC NOTICE

             CITY OF PORTSMOUTH, NEW HAMPSHIRE
              PORTSMOUTH BOARD OF REGISTRARS


The Portsmouth Board of Registrars will hold a Public Session on Thursday,
October 22, 2026, from 5:30pm to 7:00pm, at City Hall, in Room 211. This is
the last day to register to vote in person prior to the General Election being
held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Residents of Portsmouth may still
complete same day voter registration at their polling location.


                                                          SALLY A. KELLAR
                                                        PORTSMOUTN, N.H.
