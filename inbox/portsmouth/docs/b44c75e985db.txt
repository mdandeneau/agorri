III.   PUBLIC HEARINGS – NEW BUSINESS
        The Board’s action in these matters has been deemed to be quasi-judicial in nature.
             If any person believes any member of the Board has a conflict of interest,
                that issue should be raised at this point or it will be deemed waived.

       A. The request of Ryan Trust (Owner), for property located at 221 Woodbury Avenue
          requesting Preliminary and Final Subdivision Approval to subdivide one lot into two lots
          with associated site improvements. Said property is located on Assessor Map 175 Lot
          10 and lies within the General Residence A (GRA) District. (LU-26-57)

       B. The request of Ralph 1912 LLC (Owner), for property located at 175 Fleet Street
          requesting Amended Site Plan Review Approval for modifications to the approved site
          plan and amendments to Conditions of Approval associated with the Planning Board's
          January 22, 2026, Letter of Decision. Said property is located on Assessor Map 117 Lot
          8 and lies within the Character District 5(CD5), Historic and Downtown Overlay
          Districts. (LU-25-152)

       C. The request of One Market Square LLC (Owner), for property located at 1-15
          Congress Street/Newbury Way requesting an Earth Products Removal and Placement
          permit in accordance with Section 10.1020 of the Zoning Ordinance for the removal of
          approximately 1,275 cubic yards of material. Said property is located on Assessor Map
          117 Lot 14 and lies within the Character District 4 (CD4), Character District 5 (CD5),
          Historic and Downtown Overlay Districts. (LU-26-145)

       D. The request of Road to the 140 West LLC (Owner), for property located at 140 West
          Road requesting Site Plan Approval for site improvements including parking, outdoor
          dining, lighting, drainage and landscaping. Said property is located on Assessor Map 252
          Lot 2-1301 and lies within the Industrial (I) District. (LU-26-107)
