LEGAL NOTICE
                                  BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT
                                PORTSMOUTH, NEW HAMPSHIRE

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Board of Adjustment will hold Public Hearings on the
following petitions on Tuesday November 17, 2026 and Tuesday November 24, 2026 starting at 7:00
p.m. in the Eileen Dondero Foley Council Chambers, City Hall, Municipal Complex, 1 Junkins Avenue,
Portsmouth, New Hampshire.

THE FOLLOWING ITEMS WILL BE HEARD ON TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 17, 2026

The request of Christopher H Daniell (Owner), for property located at 50 Whidden Street whereas
relief is needed to construct a rear addition and cantilevered deck which requires the following: 1)
Variance from Section 10.521 to allow a 9.5 foot rear yard where 25 feet are required. Said property is
located on Assessor Map 102 Lot 66 and lies within the General Residence B (GRB) and Historic
Districts.

The request of Colbea Enterprises LLC (Owner), for property located at 1980 Woodbury Avenue
whereas relief is needed for signage which requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.1251.10
to allow for aggregate sign area greater than the maximum of 1.5’ per linear foot of the building frontage
establishment; 2) Variance from Section 10.1252.40 to allow illumination of lightbars as canopy sign
background distinguished only by color stripes; and 3) Variance from Section 10.1251.20 to allow for
canopy sign area of 460.74 square feet and 192.90 square feet where 20 square ft is the maximum. Said
property is located on Assessor Map 239 Lot 11 and lies within the Gateway Corridor (G1) District.

The request of Kathleen B. and Kent A. Logan, Susan E. Lassen, Scott A. Lupkas, and Guy D. and
Elizabeth R. Spiers (Appellants), to rehear the case for appeal of decision of administrative officials to
classify the art studio attached to the side of the third dwelling unit as an R2 non-transient sleeping unit
for property located at 28 Blossom Street. On September 15, 2026 the Board voted to deny the appeal.
Said property is located on Assessor Map 111 Lot 40 and lies within the General Residence B (GRB) and
Historic Districts.

The request of PWED2 LLC (Owner), for property located at 921 Islington Street whereas relief is
needed to create an additional parking space in front of the building which requires the following: 1)
Variance from Section 10.113.20 to allow an additional parking space between the principal building and
the street. Said property is located on Assessor Map 172 Lot 10 and lies within the Character District 4-
W (CD4-W).

The request of Carilyn J. Kussmaul Revocable Trust of 2021 (Owner), for property located at 27
Thaxter Street whereas relief is needed to demolish and reconstruct the front porch which requires the
following: 1) Variance from Section 10.521 to allow a 14.5 foot front yard area where 30 feet are
required. Said property is located on Assessor Map 166 Lot 39 and lies within the Single Residence B
(SRB) District.
The request of Andrew Super (Owner), for property located at 310 Dennett Street whereas relief is
needed to reconstruct a deck which requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.521 to allow a) a
5.5-foot right side yard where 10 feet is required and b) building coverage of 39% where 25% is
required. Said property is located on Assessor Map 143 Lot 27 and lies within the General residence A
(GRA) District.

The request of Arundo Group Grantor Trust (Owner), for property located at 125 Echo Avenue
whereas relief is needed to remove and replace the front porch which requires the following: 1) Variance
from Section 10.521 to allow a 20.5 foot front yard where 30 feet are required. Said property is located
on Assessor Map 237 Lot 61 and lies within the Single Residence B (SRB) District.

THE FOLLOWING ITEMS WILL BE HEARD ON TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 24, 2026

The request of Portsmouth Lumber and Hardware LLC (Owner), for property located at 103 Bartlett
Road whereas relief is needed for outdoor storage as a principal use and to install a fence in the principal
front yard, which requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.440 to permit the outdoor storage
of lumber and building supply products where such storage is not allowed as a principal use; and 2)
Variance from Section 10.515.13 to permit a fence over four feet in height in the principal front yard.
Said property is located on Assessor Map 164 Lot 2 and lies within the Character District 4-W (CD4-W)
District.

The request of The Edwin B. Christly Revocable Trust (Owner), and Brian Zanesky (Applicant), for
property located at 197 Richards Avenue whereas relief is needed to renovate, enlarge, and convert the
existing principal structure from a three-family to a single-family and to construct a second single-family
structure. The request requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.521 to allow a) 5,162 sf lot
area per dwelling unit where 7,500 sf is required, b) a left side yard of 3.5 feet where 10 feet is required;
and 2) Variance from Section 10.513 to allow a second free-standing dwelling on the lot where no more
than one is allowed. Said property is located on Assessor Map 129 Lot 42 and lies within the General
Residence A (GRA) District.

The request of John Michael Jr. and Natalie Murphy (Owners), for property located at 175 Middle
Road whereas relief is needed to demolish and construct a landing to and infill and reconfigure a portion
of the rear deck which requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.521 to allow for 37% building
coverage where 20% maximum is allowed. Said property is located on Assessor Map 168 Lot 16 and lies
within the Single Residence B (SRB) District.

The request of Ryan Adam and Katherine Kaplan (Owners), for property located at 19 Coakley Road
whereas relief is needed to construct walls and conditioned area for the conversion of the carport into
living space which requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.521 for a rear yard of 12.5 feet
where 30 feet is required. Said property is located on Assessor Map 234 Lot 31 and lies within the Single
Residence B (SRB) District.
The request of 350 Hanover Street Portsmouth LLC (Owner), for property located at 350 Hanover
Street whereas relief is needed to demolish the rear addition, construct a garage addition and renovate
the existing structure, which requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.5A41.10A for a
building coverage of 65% where 60% is allowed; and 2) Variance from Section 10.321 to allow a
nonconforming building to be extended, reconstructed or enlarged without conforming to the
requirements of the Ordinance. Said property is located on Assessor Map 126 Lot 29 and lies within the
Character District 4-L1 (CD4-L1) District.

The request of Shiembob E & B Family Living Trust (Owner), for property located at 635 Lincoln
Avenue whereas relief is needed to demolish the existing garage and subdivide the property into two lots
which requires the following: 1) Variance from Section 10.521 to allow a) 7,046 square feet of lot area
for Lot B where 7,500 are allowed, b) 63 feet of continuous frontage for Lot A and 39 feet of continuous
frontage for Lot B where 100 feet are required, and c) a 5 foot left side yard on Lot A for the existing
house where 10 feet are required. Said property is located on Assessor Map 148 Lot 38 and lies within
the General Residence A (GRA) and Historic Districts.
