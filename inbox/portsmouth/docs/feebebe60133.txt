LEGAL NOTICE
                                        PLANNING BOARD
                                   PORTSMOUTH, NEW HAMPSHIRE

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Planning Board will hold a public hearing on the following
applications on Thursday, November 19, 2026 starting at 6:00 p.m. in the Eileen Dondero Foley Council
Chambers, City Hall, Municipal Complex, 1 Junkins Avenue, Portsmouth, New Hampshire.

The request of Ryan Trust (Owner), for property located at 221 Woodbury Avenue requesting Preliminary
and Final Subdivision Approval to subdivide one lot into two lots with associated site improvements. Said
property is located on Assessor Map 175 Lot 10 and lies within the General Residence A (GRA) District.

The request of Ralph 1912 LLC (Owner), for property located at 175 Fleet Street requesting Amended Site
Plan Review Approval for modifications to the approved site plan and amendments to Conditions of Approval
associated with the Planning Board's January 22, 2026, Letter of Decision. Said property is located on Assessor
Map 117 Lot 8 and lies within the Character District 5 (CD5), Historic and Downtown Overlay Districts.

The request of One Market Square LLC (Owner), for property located at 1-15 Congress Street/Newbury
Way requesting an Earth Products Removal and Placement permit in accordance with Section 10.1020 of the
Zoning Ordinance for the removal of approximately 1,275 cubic yards of material. Said property is located on
Assessor Map 117 Lot 14 and lies within the Character District 4 (CD4), Character District 5 (CD5), Historic
and Downtown Overlay Districts.

The request of Road to the 140 West LLC (Owner), for property located at 140 West Road requesting Site
Plan Approval for site improvements including parking, outdoor dining, lighting, drainage and landscaping.
Said property is located on Assessor Map 252 Lot 2-1301 and lies within the Industrial (I) District.

                                      Planning and Sustainability Director
                                                  Peter Britz

Members of the public also have the option to join the meeting over Zoom, a unique meeting ID and password

Those interested in submitting written comments should email Planning@portsmouthnh.gov . Comments
received by close of business the day before the meeting will be incorporated into the record of the meeting.
Any comments received after this deadline must be submitted in person by the individual at the meeting.
