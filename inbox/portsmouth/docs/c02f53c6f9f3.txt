CITY OF PORTSMOUTH, NEW HAMPSHIRE

  SEACOAST SHUTTLE LOOP & TRANSIT WORKING GROUP - Meeting 2
            Monday, November 2, 2026, at 3:30-5:00 p.m.
                 Conference Room A, City Hall*
               1 Junkins Avenue, Portsmouth, NH


                                   AGENDA

    1. Welcome and recap of Meeting 1 (5 min)
    2. Convenience barriers (15 min)
       Setting routes, schedules and frequency aside, what stops people from riding?
       We’ll think through the whole trip:
       • Finding out a service exists
       • Planning the trip
       • Paying
       • Waiting
       • Riding
       • The last stretch to the destination
    3. Portsmouth Downtown Evening Circulator (20 min) Carl Weber
    4. CMAQ grant opportunity (15 min)
       The federal Congestion Mitigation and Air Quality program funds 80% of
       eligible projects, including operating costs, with a 20% local match. COAST
       would apply, and the City would provide the match.
       Discussion: What should a CMAQ application fund? For example, more
       frequent service on an existing route, or a smaller shuttle loop.
       Action item: Consensus on projects and ranking for recommendation to the
       City Council.
    5. Next Steps (5 min) and Meetings: Monday, November 15, 2026, 3:30-5:00 pm
    6. Via: On-demand Microtransit (30 min)
    7. Adjourn
