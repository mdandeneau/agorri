                 Municipal Building Blue Ribbon Committee Recommendation
             Councilor Bagley feels this is a solid municipal building plan and he supports it. It is a

             was too large, and the cost was too great. Overtime she learned the committee worked

             He believes this project can be done as efficiently as possible without having to look at a

B.   Public Hearing and Second Reading amendments to Chapter 10, Zoning Ordinance, which
     will remove most parking regulations from the Zoning Ordinance to the Site Plan Review
     Regulation (except one and two-family dwellings)
             PRESENTATION
              Assistant Planning Director, Peter Stith, explained the proposed zoning
              amendments regarding parking regulations, a history of parking, changes to site
              plan review regulations, parking flexibility, changes not being made, and
              implementation.
             CITY COUNCIL QUESTIONS
              Councilor Cook asked about the site planning regulations. How does this
              procedure work? Peter Stith explained that the Planning Board has simplified the
              process in line with site plan regulations. Councilor Moreau echoed his answer.
              Councilor Tabor commented on the site review parking standards in the packet. It
              is much simpler.
              Mayor McEachern asked questions about parking and conditional use permits. Will
              parking be a part of the site plan and come before the Planning Board with every
                                                                                               Page 4 of 10
                     application? It changes from a conditional use permit to a site plan review through
                     the Planning Board.
                     Councilor Bagley feels we should be eliminating parking requirements. He feels we
                     are taking a complicated thing and making it more complicated.
                     Councilor Moreau explained we are making things less complicated by putting it
                     into site plan review.
                     Councilor Tabor thinks looking at parking requirements over time and putting it as
                     a part of the site plan to be presented in front of the Planning Board is a step in the
                     right direction.
                     Councilor Cooks feels this is a step in the right direction in order to approve
                     housing.
                     Mayor McEachern added how this relates to the City's zoning ordinances. He is in
                     support of these amendments.
                     Councilor Moreau requested redline site plan regulations in the ordinance in
                     advance of third and final reading.
                   PUBLIC HEARING SPEAKERS
                   Tyler Garza, on Zoom, is in support of these changes and the efforts to bring this

C.   Councilor Bagley
           ADU Property Tax Exemption
     Councilor Bagley moved to request that Portsmouth's Delegation to the General Court
     file legislation for the 2027 session authorizing cities and towns to adopt a local option
     property tax exemption on the added assessed value of newly created accessory
     dwelling units, for a period of up to 20 years, as outlined below. Seconded by Councilor
     Moreau.
     Councilor Bagley explained why he likes this proposal.
     Mayor McEachern discussed how this request relates to the Housing Action Plan. It also
     has the potential to create more senior housing.
     Councilor Cook hopes this is something that can pass at the State level. She feels this
     should apply to RSA 79-E.
     Assistant Mayor Kelley would like to have an understanding from the City Assessors as it
     relates to tax exemption. There are a limited number of lots that can be converted to
     accessory dwelling units. She would like the maximum sized ADUs allowed on lots. She
     would like to have a report back from the Assessing Department.
     Councilor Bagley would like to know how many accessory dwelling units have been built
     since 2017.
     Councilor Moreau mentioned the last Housing Committee meeting reviewed the first draft
     of the proposed changes to RSA 79-E. Feedback was given about changes they would like
     to see and have should have more information towards the end of this year or the
     beginning of next year.
     Councilor Cook mentioned the deadline for filing.
                                                          Motion passed unanimously.
     Councilor Bagley moved to request that the City Attorney report to the Council on
     whether tax relief under RSA 79-E:4-c, as amended in 2026, could apply to newly
     created accessory dwelling units. Seconded by Councilor Moreau.
                                                          Motion passed unanimously.
