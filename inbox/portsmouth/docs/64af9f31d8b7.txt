THE PROPERTY AT 413 ISLINGTON STREET IS AN EXISTING TWO-FAMILY GREEK REVIVAL
RESIDENCE, ORIGINALLY CONSTRUCTED IN APPROXIMATELY 1835 ACCORDING TO CITY       A3               HISTORIC TIMELINE
RECORDS. BASED ON A REVIEW OF HISTORIC SANBORN MAPS, THE ORIGINAL STRUCTURE     A4               EXISTING CIVIL PLAN
CONSISTS OF THE EXISTING 2-1/2-STORY BUILDING ORIENTED TOWARD ISLINGTON         A5               EXISTING FLOOR PLANS
STREET, WITH A SERIES OF SINGLE- AND TWO-STORY ADDITIONS CONSTRUCTED AT THE     A6               EXISTING FLOOR PLANS
REAR OVER TIME. A SEPARATE DETACHED STRUCTURE LOCATED ALONG THE REAR            A7               EXISTING FLOOR PLANS
PROPERTY LINE HAS BEEN PRESENT SINCE AT LEAST 1887 AND HAS ALSO UNDERGONE
                                                                                A8               EXISTING ELEVATIONS
CHANGES TO ITS FOOTPRINT AND NUMBER OF STORIES OVER TIME.
                                                                                A9               FLOOR PLAN/SITE
                                                                                A10              FLOOR PLANS
GENERAL PROJECT DESCRIPTION:                                                    A11              FLOOR PLANS
                                                                                A12              ELEVATIONS
THE PROPOSED PROJECT INCLUDES A COMPREHENSIVE RENOVATION OF THE EXISTING        A13              ELEVATIONS
RESIDENCE WHILE MAINTAINING ITS HISTORIC CHARACTER AND EXISTING TWO-FAMILY      A14              PERSPECTIVE VIEWS
RESIDENTIAL USE. THE WORK WILL INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING:                           A15              MATERIALS
•   FULL RENOVATION OF THE ORIGINAL 2-1/2-STORY 1835 STRUCTURE.                 A16              MATERIALS
•   REMOVAL OF THE EXISTING SINGLE- AND TWO-STORY REAR ADDITIONS AND            A17              MATERIALS
    CONSTRUCTION OF A NEW, EXPANDED 2-1/2-STORY ADDITION AT THE REAR OF THE
    ORIGINAL STRUCTURE.
•   CONSTRUCTION OF TWO SINGLE-CAR ADDITIONS, INCLUDING ONE ATTACHED GARAGE                                                 SOUTH WEST VIEW FROM ISLINGTON STREET
    AND ONE DETACHED GARAGE.
•   RESTORATION AND REPLACEMENT IN KIND OF EXISTING HISTORIC EXTERIOR TRIM,
    MOLDINGS, AND ARCHITECTURAL DETAILS WHERE REQUIRED.
•   REMOVAL OF EXISTING VINYL SIDING AND REPLACEMENT WITH WOOD CLAPBOARD
    SIDING.
•   REMOVAL OF THE EXISTING REAR CHIMNEY WHILE RECONSTRUCTING THE PROMINENT
    FRONT CHIMNEY.
•   ADDITION OF DORMERS TO THE ROOF TO PROVIDE ADDITIONAL USABLE AREA WITHIN
    THE UPPER FLOOR.

THE PROPOSED WORK IS INTENDED TO RETAIN AND RESTORE THE ARCHITECTURAL
CHARACTER OF THE HISTORIC ISLINGTON STREET-FACING STRUCTURE WHILE ALLOWING
THE PROPERTY TO ACCOMMODATE UPDATED RESIDENTIAL NEEDS. NEW ADDITIONS AND
ALTERATIONS WILL BE DESIGNED TO COMPLEMENT THE SCALE, PROPORTIONS, MATERIALS,
AND ARCHITECTURAL CHARACTER OF THE EXISTING HISTORIC RESIDENCE AND
SURROUNDING NEIGHBORHOOD.
