LEGAL NOTICE
                           HISTORIC DISTRICT COMMISSION
                           PORTSMOUTH, NEW HAMPSHIRE

       NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Historic District Commission will hold Public
Hearings on Applications #1 through #4 on Wednesday, December 02, 2026. The meeting will
begin at 6:30 p.m. and will be held in the Eileen Dondero Foley Council Chambers, Municipal
Complex, 1 Junkins Avenue, Portsmouth, New Hampshire.

     There will be a second Historic District Commission meeting held on Wednesday,
                                    December 09, 2026.

PUBLIC HEARINGS

1.     Petition of Knight Trust, owner, for property located at 213 South Street, wherein
permission is requested to allow exterior renovations to an existing structure (replacement of 13
windows) as per plans on file in the Planning Department. Said property is shown on Assessor
Map 111 as Lot 38 and lies within the General Residence B (GRB) and Historic Districts.

2.      Petition of Family Access Trust For Katsapetses N., owner, for property located at 117
Bow Street, Unit #203, wherein permission is requested to allow exterior renovations to an
existing structure (replacement of 13 windows and 4 patio doors) as per plans on file in the
Planning Department. Said property is shown on Assessor Map 106 as Lot 57B-3 and lies within
the Character District 4 (CD4), Downtown Overlay, and Historic Districts.

3.      Petition of Kaila Krauser and James Taylor, owners, for property located at 85 Gates
Street, Unit B, wherein permission is requested to allow exterior renovations to an existing
structure (replacement of 10 windows) as per plans on file in the Planning Department. Said
property is shown on Assessor Map 103 as Lot 94-B and lies within the General Residence B
(GRB) and Historic Districts.

4.      Petition of New Horizon Real Estate LLC, owner, for property located at 12 Fleet Street,
wherein permission is requested to allow exterior renovations to an existing structure
(replacement of 12 windows) as per plans on file in the Planning Department. Said property is
shown on Assessor Map 116 as Lot 28 and lies within the Character District 4 (CD4) and
Historic Districts.

or mail to the Planning Department, City Hall, 1 Junkins Ave, Portsmouth, NH 03801.
