CITY OF PORTSMOUTH, NEW HAMPSHIRE
           SEACOAST SHUTTLE LOOP & TRANSIT WORKING GROUP
                                   Meeting 3
            Monday, November 16 | 3:30–5:00 pm | Conference Room A

WHERE WE ARE
  • Meeting 1: Who we’re serving. Downtown and hospitality workers who need
    evening and weekend service; residents without reliable car access, especially
    for medical and essential trips; and the many people who don’t know transit is
    an option.
  • Meeting 2: What keeps people off existing transit. Awareness, frequency and
    hours, travel time, cash-only fares, wayfinding, and shelters and lighting.

Today we hear two presentations, shape a community survey, and plan a public
listening session.

QUESTIONS TO KEEP IN MIND FOR EACH PRESENTATION
  • Who does it serve, and at what hours?
  • Which of the stories and barriers we’ve heard does it address?
  • The New Hampshire filter: can we pay for it, can we legally do it, and does it fit
    Portsmouth’s scale?
  • How would we know it worked?
