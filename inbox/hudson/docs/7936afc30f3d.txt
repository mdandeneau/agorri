Hudson, NH Board of Selectmen 09-29-26 Minutes


                                 HUDSON, NEW HAMPSHIRE BOARD OF SELECTMEN
                                 Minutes of the September 29, 2026 Board of Selectmen Meeting
                                 4:30 PM
                                 Board of Selectmen Meeting Room, Town Hall


1.   CALL TO ORDER – by Chairman Dumont the meeting of September 29, 2026 at 4:32 p.m. in the Selectmen
     Meeting Room at Town Hall.
2.   PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE – Elvis Dhima.
3.   ATTENDANCE
     Board of Selectmen: Xen Vurgaropulos, Kimberly Allan, Bob Guessferd and Heidi Jakoby. Dillon Dumont is
     excused.
     Staff/Others: Town Administrator: Roy Sorenson and Development Services Director: Elvis Dhima.
4.   PUBLIC INPUT
     Selectman Guessferd: First, as we normally do, does anyone in the audience wish to address the Board on any
     issue for which the Board has control of at this time? Please state your name and address for the record. Seeing
     none, we'll move on to the next section of the meeting.
5.   OLD BUSINESS
            A. Merrimack River Crossing Grant Acceptance – DSD/Decision
     Selectman Guessferd: So, we're going to be going into old business. This has to do with Merrimack River Crossing
     Grant Acceptance. I will recognize Town Administrator Roy Sorensen, who will provide some up-front details.

     Roy Sorenson: All right. Thank you, Vice Chair, or Chair, Guessferd, for this evening. Pursuant to 31:95-b, we're
     here tonight to have a public hearing on this item. This is the item that Mr. Dhima brought up at the last meeting.
     Legal advertising was September 26th in the Telegraph, so that was posted. At this point, I will actually turn it
     over to Mr. Dhima, and he can give you the details of this, the amount, who it's from, and what we're doing with
     it.

     Elvis Dhima: Thank you, Mr. Sorensen. So, as you all know, we're trying to build the crossing to make sure that
     we have enough water for domestic fire suppression, as we do with the PFAS. We're working with St. Gobane.
     We're also working with the state as well, and Pennechuck, to basically turn this into a regional project. We did
     exactly that, and that's what this grant does. This grant takes this transmission line from a 16-inch transmission
     line to a 24-inch, increases capacity significantly, and also provides the ability for the town, Pennechuck, and
     the state to not only provide the water that we need here in Hudson, but also take some of this water and give
     it to the neighbors as well. And that's what this 334 will do. So, we are required to have a public hearing, and
     then the Board of Selectmen accept them. So, in the last meeting, as you know, you authorized me to grant, to
     apply for it officially. We did. We got it. Tonight, you're accepting it. With that said, I'll take any questions you
     might have.

     Selectman Guessferd: Okay, first, before we do anything else with this, we are going to open a public hearing.
     So, I will open the public hearing at 4:35 p.m. Is there anyone present who would like to speak on this matter?
     Seeing none, I will close the public hearing at 4:35 p.m. Okay, Board members, any questions?

     Selectman Jakoby: I just wanted, for the public, for you to state where this grant is coming from again, just so
     that it's all on the record for today.

     Elvis Dhima: It's the New Hampshire Department of Environmental Services, and it's the Water Bureau. It's my
     understanding. This is related to $9.7 million that the state put aside for PFAS-related projects and created
     transmission lines from Nashua to Litchfield-Hudson, and that's where this money is coming from, from a pot of
     money that originally started as $9.7 million, and now I think it's about $9.2 million.

     Selectman Jakoby: Just so the public knows, I think it's important to know why, the cause, and the effect, so
     we're coming to that point where we're seeing it happen. So, thank you.

     Selectman Guessferd: Okay, thank you. All right, any other input from the Board?

     Selectman Jakoby made a motion, seconded by selectman Allan, to accept the $339,000 grant from the New
     Hampshire Department of Environmental Services for the Regional Water Main Project, with grant funding
     providing 100% reimbursement and no required local match, and to designate the Development Services Director
     as the town's point of contact for the grant application and associated coordination. Further, to allow the town
     administrator full signatory authority as applicable for effectuating the grant. Motion carried, 4-0.

     Selectman Guessferd: I just want to say that, you know, this is probably the best kind of grant we can get,
     obviously, 100% reimbursement, no required local match, and so I'm fully in favor of this myself. Absolutely.
     Anything else? Okay, well, that concludes our business. Selectman Vurgaropulos, do you have anything you
     want to do here?

6.   ADJOURNMENT
     Selectman Vurgaropulos made a motion, seconded by Selectman Jakoby, to adjourn at 4:38 p.m. Motion carried,
     4-0.
