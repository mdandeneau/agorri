 Presentation provided by: Dr. Moulis, Superintendent Hudson Schools and
Jenny Graves, Business Administrator, regarding updates on school enrollment
number and employee vacancies. The School Board will review feasibility data
regarding the structure of the employee healthcare plan. Discussions will focus on
optimizing cost management through either restructuring the risk pool
administration or transitioning to a standalone self-insured model. A draft School
District Budget for FY 28 will be available to the Budget Committee on 8 Oct 26
with change orders prepared for budget hearings on 28 Oct 26.
