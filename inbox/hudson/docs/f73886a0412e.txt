RE:          Merrimack River Crossing Grant Acceptance

Recommended Motion: To accept the $339,000 grant for the regional water main project, with
grant funding providing 100% reimbursement and no required local match, and to designate the
Development Services Director as the Town’s point of contact for the grant application and
associated coordination.

Background: The Town has been advancing the design of a water infrastructure project
originally intended to address Hudson’s local water system needs. Through coordination with the
State and regional stakeholders, it was determined that the project could be expanded to
accommodate broader regional water supply needs.

The expanded scope requires additional design work associated with the larger regional water
main, including revised routing, connections, permitting, and related engineering efforts. Weston
& Sampson has prepared a revised scope and fee of approximately $279,000 for this work.

The Town received a 100% reimbursement grant totaling $339,000, with no local match
required. The grant funding consists of:

         Approximately $279,000 for the Weston & Sampson regional design contract;
         Up to $30,000 for permit/application fees, additional permitting efforts, and unforeseen
          engineering or regulatory costs; and
         Up to $30,000 for engineering support during the bidding phase.

The two $30,000 allowances would be maintained separately from the Weston & Sampson
contract and utilized by the Town only as needed. The Development Services Director will serve
as the Town’s point of contact for the grant and associated project coordination.

RSA/Rule: Env-Dw 1300, Env-Dw 1400, RSA 485-F:3, II and RSA 485-H:9
All funding recipients (Grantee) must demonstrate authority for the Grantee to accept grant funding, enter into a grant
agreement with the New Hampshire Department of Environmental Services (NHDES), and further demonstrate that the
Grantee’s designated authorized representative (AR) has the authority from the Grantee to execute the grant
documents.
Demonstrating authority may be done by completing a certificate of vote (COV). A COV demonstrates that a vote
occurred at a meeting of the Grantee’s governing body in which:
       The Grantee decided to accept and expend the grant funds and enter into a grant agreement with NHDES.
       The Grantee delegated authority to a particular individual (the AR) to sign the grant documents on behalf of the
        governing body. Meeting minutes must be submitted to NHDES that demonstrate a proper vote occurred.
The COV shall also certify that the authority remains effective and has not been revoked, superseded or amended. The
Grantee vote may take place any time prior to the grant agreement being signed. The grant agreement will be provided
to the Grantee by NHDES.

Instructions
Do not complete the COV until you have received a grant agreement (G-1 with exhibits) from NHDES.
The COV must be completed as follows:
    1) A representative of the Grantee that witnessed or has personal knowledge of the vote and can certify the details
       of the vote for purposes of the COV (for a municipality this is typically a town clerk, board member or an
       administrative assistant; for a corporate entity this is typically an officer) signs this form – the AR does not sign
       this form.
    2) The COV must be signed on the same date as, or within 30 days of, when the grant agreement is signed.
Projects that are receiving grant funds from more than one NHDES program for the same project can complete one COV.
Before completing the COV please make sure all steps above were followed and the person signing/completing the
COV is not the AR. The original COV must be mailed to the appropriate program contact at NHDES. A template COV is
available on the following page.
