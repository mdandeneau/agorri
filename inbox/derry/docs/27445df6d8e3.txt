Consent Agenda
Motion to approve minutes made by Chris and seconded by Dan. Motion passed 4-0 with Colton
abstaining.

Introduced Colton Ratey as our new Alternate Member.

Community Power: Freedom Energy chosenas the vendor going forward for managingtheroll-out of a
Community Powerprogram. Quotes from suppliers are expected early next year, with a potential rollout
in April 2027, if pricing is competitive.

Nolegislative update.

Landfill Solar Project: Production is meeting expectations & Eversource is processing net metering
credits. No billing available yet to validate the rest of the model.

EVCS: 1) Shared a memo to Mike Fowler and Tom Carrier with the Net Zero recommendation to change
the breakers to 20 ampsfor the two charging stations in the Municipal parking lot, saving $1470/year for
the town. 2) New NH DESprogram for L2 chargers available for up to $10k per charging port, with
matching funds for public charging locations in town. Requires 20% match from participants.
Applications due 11/15. Will start soliciting candidates from the schools, Pinkerton, businesses and the
Town. Webinar on 10/14 for more information & resources, hosted by Clean Energy NH.

New Business: Recommendationto solicit interest from schools & Pinkerton with regard to educational
opportunities for the landfill solar project.
