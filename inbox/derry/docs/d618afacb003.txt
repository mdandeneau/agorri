Title: Agenda: Supervisors of the Checklist Session Notice
Fetched: 2026-11-01

Supervisors of the Checklist Session
                      Tuesday October 27, from 4:00 – 6:00PM
                         Derry Municipal Center, Room 104


               Derry Supervisors of the Checklist will meet in session to:

                               Register New Voters
               Accept Name and Address Changes Of Current Voters
              Consider Updates To Voter Checklist (removals, transfers)

                 Derry residents have 3 options to register to vote:
             o apply with the Town Clerk on weekdays during open hours
              o register with the Supervisors of the Checklist at a session
           o Register at the polling place on Election Day, November 3, 2026


New Hampshire has 4 qualifications to register to vote: age 18 or older by election day,
  domicile in the town or ward, US citizenship, and an accepted photo ID to establish
     identity. Applicants must present a document to establish each qualification. If
documents to prove citizenship are not available, applicants may complete a Qualified
                                        Voter Affidavit.
    An applicant's current address on a government-issued photo ID or government
     document satisfies the domicile qualification. Applicants who do not possess a
qualifying document can complete an Attestation of Not Possessing Qualified Domicile
                               Proof Documents and provide:
                                   o a rental/lease agreement
                         o a utility or other bill with current domicile,
       o a Confirmation of Domicile form signed by the person owning or renting the
                                              domicile, or
                        o a Homeless Confirmation of Domicile form.
   3 documents establish citizenship: a US birth certificate, passport or naturalization
   certificate. Real ID driver's licenses, military IDs, and Social Security cards do not
establish citizenship. Applicants previously registered in New Hampshire may not need
  to establish citizenship or age if the Supervisors of the Checklist or Town Clerk can
                       verify registration in the state voter database.
                                          Resources
     Comprehensive information on registering and voting is available online on the
       Secretary of State website: https://www.sos.nh.gov/elections/register-vote.
 The voter checklist is posted in the lobby of the Derry Municipal Center and the Derry
                                         Public Library.
