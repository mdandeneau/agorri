General —

Asfall starts to descend upon us, there are some upcoming events slated for the community.

    1) Downtown Trick or Treat — Saturday, October 24, 2026 starting with a costume paradeat
       Hood Park from 11am to noon, followed by downtowntrick or treat noon to 3pm. We
       appreciate the participating businesses for this event. Further details are available on the
       Parks & Recreation Facebook page and the Town’s website.

    2) Saturday October 31, 2026 is Halloween. There has been a suggestedtrick ortreat time
       of 6pm to 730pm whichhas been followed for many years. There is a misconception that
       the TownofDerry officially regulates when the event is scheduled by official ordinance
       or declaration. We do not. In a world wherethere are expectations of a conciergestyle
       experience, the Town doesnotneedto get into legislating individual circumstances such
       as weatherbeing too cold, too warm,too dark, conflict with youth sport event, etc. and
       reacting to predicted weather concerns. Several years ago (2019), there was a movement
       to postponethe traditional October 31 date due to a predicted storm. Other communities
       pushedtheir event out, but Derry held to October31, and the night of Halloween was
       over 70 degrees. Derry Police will be in neighborhoods with a visible presence.

Financial —

On October 7, the Town received notification of FY 28 insurance rates. The Town’s overall cost
will increase approximately 8.2% overcurrent rates. By comparison FY 26 was up 15% and FY
27 was up 26% overthe previous year. Town Council should expect about $550k to $600k of
increased insurance costs to be wrapped into the preliminary FY 28 budget. There wasa silver
lining that all other coverages were flat or nominally increased over FY 27. It is important to
note that NHRScontributions will be dropping next year to offset these increases.

SUBJECT:            Adoption of Chapter 40 of the General Code - Electioneering at Polling Places


INTRODUCTION
 During the 2026 State Primary and in the daysafter, Election officials have received multiple
 complaints about electioneering that occurred in the parking area of the polling location.


BACKGROUND
 Many voters complained about the pamphlets being left in the booth, many complained that
 they were feeling accosted, some said theyfelt threated and intimidated by the person.
 There were complaints made to the Attorney General’s office, both by the Moderator and by
 voters. We have requested a report of the complaints they received. However, one complaint
 caused the Attorney General's office to call the Moderator and request to speak to the officer
 in the building at that time, Captain Michaud.

DISCUSSION
 The Town Moderator has the power by RSA 659:43 to control Electioneering at the polling
 location, however that power does not extend to the parking lot itself. Upon review of
 various statutes, RSA 31:41-c gives the Town Council the power to set bylaws regulating the
 “distribution of campaign materials or electioneering or any activity which affects the safety,
 welfare and rights of voters at any election held for any such purpose in such town”.

 RSA 31:39 gives the Town Council the power to appoint who shall enforce these and can
 issue a penalty not exceeding $1000.00.

 With a large parking lot and the volume wehavefor large elections (18,148 voters in
 November 2024), the parking lot with the road closed has a lot of moving parts and having
 people handing things out and stopping the flow of traffic will be problematic to the police
 department.

FISCAL IMPACT
None


RECOMMENDED ACTION BY COUNCIL
Please review the attached ordinance under a new Chapter 40 in the general code. Two public
hearings are required, the vote would be scheduled for October 20, 2026.

 SUBJECT:          2027 Milfoil Treatment at Beaver Lake


  INTRODUCTION:
  To seek authorization to continue working with the Beaver Lake ImprovementAssociation
  (BLIA) to provide milfoil treatment at Beaver Lake.

 TOWN ADMINISTRATOR COMMENTS
  Town Council is encouraged to continue to support volunteer and Town staff efforts to combat
  milfoil in Beaver Lake.

 BACKGROUND
 Milfoil is an invasive species which has infiltrated Beaver Lake. Since FY20 the Town Council voted to
 appropriate funds to assist with the control and future eradication of the plants. Before that date,
 BLIA exclusively assumed this responsibility, with grant assistance from the State Department of
 Environmental Services. The Council, recognizing both the financial burden upon a private
 organization preserving a public body of water, and the potential adverse impact upon property values
 should milfoil not be controlled, earmarked approximately $6,000 in FY 26 towardthis effort.


 DISCUSSION

The Beaver Lake Improvement Association (BLIA) had set aside up to $3,000 toward the Milfoil
eradication each year from 2022 through 2026. The BLIA estimated a local match of up to $3,000
again for 2027. For 2026,the total cost of the herbicide treatment was quoted not to exceed
$29,990. NH DES awarded the Townan Exotic Aquatic Plant Control grant in the amount of
$14,995 whichis a 50% local match grant. Less BLIA’s $3,000 commitment, Derry’s estimated
commitment in FY27 would be not to exceed $11,995. For 2027, the Town is assuming a similar
estimate as in 2026.

The BLIA volunteer divers and spotters continue to spend significant time in and on the water
each year removing milfoil. The volunteers typically contribute over 100 hours and as much as
200 hoursper year, removing invasive milfoil plants. In 2020 BLIA invested in sea scooters to
travel underwater at much faster speeds. With the use of this type of equipment BLIA covered a
tremendous amount ofacreagein a fraction of the time increasing the number of gallons of
milfoil removed per diver hour.

In 2025 and 2026 no milfoil treatment was needed based on DESand BLIA inspections. This is a
positive indication that the BLIA and Town efforts are addressing the presence of milfoil. The
Townwill, however, continue to participate in this program and apply for available grants to
conduct any treatment necessary depending on the prevailing lake conditions. Grant funds may
also be used to address other invasive species as approved by NH DES.

FISCAL IMPACT:

The Town cost share would not exceed $12,000 as its share of the 50% match for the herbicide
treatment based on last year’s contractor proposal, which is allotted entirely to the Herbicide
Treatment and is included in the FY27 budget. The final grant award will be determined by
NHDESduring winter 2026.

RECOMMENDED ACTION BY COUNCIL It is recommended that the Council authorize the Town
Administrator to apply for, accept and expend up to $15,000 in NH DES Exotic Aquatic Plant
Control Grant Program funds for the treatment of milfoil in Beaver Lake and any other invasive ©
species as may be approved by NH DES.

Further, it is recommended that the Council accept and expend up to $3,000 in funds from the
Beaver Lake Improvement Association to be applied toward the required 50% matching funds
under the grant.


Action #1 — Support Resolution #2026-056 whichwill allow the Townto receive up to $15,000in
NHDES Grant Funds;

Action #2 — Support Resolution #2026-057 whichwill allow the Town to receive up to $3,000 in
funds from Beaver Lake Improvement Association.

                                     Project


WHEREAS,Section 5.1 of the Town Charter establishes the Town Council as the governing
and legislative body of the Town, and

WHEREAS, the Town has partnered with NHDOT on the Exit 4A project, which includes
extensive road improvements in Derry; and

WHEREAS, NHDOTseeksto changethe designto facilitate a tunnel for connectivity of the Rail
Trail; and

WHEREAS, NHDOThas determined the various design elements which will meet regulatory
needs of the New Hampshire Department of Historical and Cultural Resources; and

WHEREAS,this revised Memorandum of Agreement replaces the previous document; and

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED IN COUNCIL that the Town Administrator is hereby
authorized to sign a Memorandumof Agreement with NHDOTwhich will govern the execution of
this work.

This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its passage.
