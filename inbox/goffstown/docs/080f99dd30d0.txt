4.       6:05 P.M. Public Hearing – Traffic Regulations

         The Goffstown Select Board will conduct a Public Hearing on Monday, October 19, 2026
         at 6:05 pm, at Goffstown Town Hall, 16 Main Street, Goffstown, NH to receive public
         input on two (2) proposed traffic regulations:

     l   Consideration of restricting parking on East Union Street. No Parking on sidewalk side
         (odd/north side) to run length of East Union Street and No Parking between intersection
         of Prospect Street to utility pole east of intersection (approximately 100ft).
     l   Consideration of a 4-way stop at the intersection of Wallace Road and Shirley Hill Road
         (Bedford side).
