Goffstown Cemetery Trustee

                                Minutes to Meeting of September 22, 2026

__________________________________________________________________________________________

In attendance were Deb Grant, Jean Walker, Valerie Neal, Adam Jacobs, and Jason Georgantas.

Location: 9:00 AM, on-site walkthrough of Hillside, Shirley Hill, and Westlawn Cemeteries.

There were no public guests.


                                               OLD BUSINESS

As previously discussed, on June 30, 2026, the Cemetery Trustees, along with Adam Jacobs and Jason
Georgantas, completed a walkthrough of all three municipal cemeteries to evaluate and document the
needs at each location. The walkthrough provided an opportunity to review the condition of the
cemeteries, identify areas requiring attention, and develop a punch list with an anticipated timeline for
completion.

The walkthrough began at Hillside Cemetery, then continued to Shirley Hill Cemetery and Westlawn
Cemetery. The following items were noted during the walkthrough. The items are listed in no particular
order and are not intended to be a complete list, as additional needs may be identified as the work is
reviewed and completed.

Hillside:

   ▪   Headstone resetting, repairs & cleaning
   ▪   Grub & weed control to be added into the yearly budget
   ▪   Entry brick area needs to be maintained
   ▪   Fencing repairs
   ▪   Septic vent-slide-on granite covering
   ▪   Bittersweet & Knotweed removal
   ▪   Hearse House needs to be sanded & repainted and cut branches and growth around it
   ▪   Many of the stone walls need to be reset
   ▪   Tree limb trimming above headstones in many areas
   ▪   3 Large pines near NE corner need to be removed
   ▪   Maple & fern thicket needs to be cleaned up
   ▪   Remove dead perennials
   ▪   Overgrowth alongside stone walls
   ▪   Planted garden areas need to be maintained throughout the year
   ▪   Loam & seeding in certain areas
   ▪   Curbing around family plots need to be reset

Shirley Hill:

Westlawn:

                                                 NEW BUSINESS

The Uncanoonuc Mountaineers of Goffstown Snowmobile Club requested permission to use Parson’s Field
on October 26th for their annual snowmobile & minibike show and swap meet. The event runs from 8:00
a.m. to 3:00 p.m. on October 26th. Deb made a motion to approve the use of Parson’s Field. Jean seconded
the motion. The motion passed unanimously, 3-0.


Adjournment:

We concluded all the cemetery walkthroughs at 12:30 p.m.


Next Scheduled Meeting:

The next Cemetery Trustees meeting will be scheduled at a later date.


Respectfully Submitted,

Valerie Neal, Secretary
