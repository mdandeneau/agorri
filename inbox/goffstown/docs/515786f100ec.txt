6.   6:15 P.M. Public Hearings (RSA 41:14-B) Adoption Of Ordinances

     The Goffstown Select Board is conducting this Public Hearing to accept public input on the
     adoption of three (3) proposed ordinances.

         l   Camping on Public Property Ordinance of the Town of Goffstown, NH
                 ¡ Proposed Ordinance regulating and restricting any person from using or
                    causing to be used any of the streets, sidewalks, square, parks, rail trail,
                    public trails, or any other town-owned public place as a camping place
                    absent prior written permission from the Goffstown Select Board or its
                    designee.
         l   Fires and Cooking on Town Property Ordinance
                 ¡ Proposed Ordinance regulating and restricting any person from starting,
                    igniting, kindling, cooking with any fire, cooking fire, or deep-pit barbecue on
                    any property owned or operated by the town absent prior written permission
                    from the Goffstown Select Board or its designee.
         l   Campers and Recreational Vehicles on Public Property Ordinance
                 ¡ Proposed Ordinance regulating and restricting any person from occupying,
                    storing, or parking and remaining in any camper or recreational vehicle on
                    any town-owned public property, public street, or public right of way, except
                    on public streets where and when parking is otherwise permitted.


         l   This is the second of two (2) required public hearings.
         l   The Select Board is scheduled to vote on these proposed ordinances on 10/19/26.
