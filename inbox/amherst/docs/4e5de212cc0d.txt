43          5.1.  Buck Meadow Field Improvement Project Presentation — Sam Foise, Meridian
44                Land Services and Arene Berry, Recreation Director
45
46   Chairman Pray recused herself from this item.
47
48   Sam Foisie, Meridian Land Services, presented the revised plans for the future development of
49   the Buck Meadow Recreation and Conservation Area. The proposed project includes the
50   construction of two full-size athletic fields, four pickleball courts, a basketball court, associated
51   parking and access/egress improvements, and ADA-compliant pathways around the perimeter of
52   the proposed improvements. The revised plans incorporate the major site features and design
53   elements necessary to advance the project for formal review and approval by the New Hampshire
54   Department of Environmental Services (NHDES) and New Hampshire Department of
55   Transportation (NHDOT). The plan shows a total of 210 parking spaces, with 68 paved and 64
56   unpaved, and 78 overflow grass parking spaces. The fields will not be lit. Additional design
57   details related to landscaping, screening, and sound mitigation are still being developed and will
58   be incorporated into the plans at a later stage. These elements will take into consideration the
59   questions, concerns, and input received from abutters as the project continues through the design
60   and permitting process. The purpose of the presentation is to provide the Board and public with
61   an opportunity to review and provide input on the current design and for the Board to consider
62   approval of the plans so the Town may proceed with formal submissions to NH Department of
63   Environmental Services (NHDES) and NH Department of Transportation (NHDOT). Following
64   receipt of the necessary state approvals, the Town will be positioned to submit its formal
65   application for Land and Water Conservation Fund (LWCF) grant funding to support the project.
66
67   In response to a question from Selectman Dokmo, Mr. Foisie stated that he believes NH DOT
68   will want to access from the site onto Route 101A to remain as a right turn only. Arene Berry,
69   Recreation Director, stated that if the access way becomes backed up during tournaments, there
70   could be a police detail.
71
72   Selectman Coughlin asked about the emergency access area. Mr. Foisie explained that there will
73   be a gate internal to the site at the emergency access way. There is already a gate in place at
74   Stearns Road. There will also be gates to block the overflow parking area when it is not in use, in
75   hopes of preserving the grass.
76
77   Town Administrator Daley asked about the stormwater area south of the parking lot next to the
78   condos and if there will be visible standing water. Mr. Foisie stated that these are all designed as
79   infiltration basins. This plan complies with the Town ordinances to the maximum extent
80   possible. The detention area is approximately 120’ away from the existing septic system for the
81   condos.
82
83   Town Administrator Daley asked about placing fencing around the fields themselves. Mr. Foisie
84   stated that some netting is proposed behind the soccer goals. Fencing would be recommended
85   and could be installed by the Town at a later date.

293          8.2.    Recreation Department - Vehicle Replacement Plan Proposal
294
295   Recreation Director Berry explained that the Parks and Recreation Department relies on a fleet of
296   vehicles and equipment to support field maintenance, snow operations, transportation, and other
297   essential services. Several existing assets are already beyond their planned replacement cycles,
298   making the next three years a necessary catch-up period. The proposed replacement program
299   would establish a predictable funding structure and reduce the risk of emergency repairs and
300   unplanned purchases. She explained that for FY28, the request is to have the Equipment
301   Purchase line in the -01 funded account at $35,000. Also, to establish a capital reserve as part of
302   a warrant article to be funded at $90,000 in Year 1. In FY28 she is proposing to purchase an
303   F250, a car for the Rec Office, and a mower, totaling approximately $118,000.
304

309

312   Forcier Contracting. Luongo's proposal also includes a drainage system to carry water away from
313   equipment beneath the deck and establishes a defined unit cost and allowance for replacement of
314   deteriorated joists discovered during construction. While the three proposals are not identical in
315   their proposed materials and drainage systems, DPW believes each generally addresses the
316   primary objective of replacing the deteriorated Transfer Station deck and managing water
      BOARD OF SELECTMEN MEETING MINUTES                                                                 7
