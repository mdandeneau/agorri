Title: Minutes: Capital Improvements Plan Meeting Agenda
Fetched: 2026-11-01

TOWN OF AMHERST
     CIP Committee

     September 9, 2026                                                                     DRAFT

 1   In Attendance: Andrew Pataky, Ways and Means Committee Representative; Stephanie Grund,
 2   Souhegan School Board Representative; Gordon Leedy, Planning Board Representative; Jason
 3   White, Amherst School Board Representative; and John D’Angelo, Alternate Board of
 4   Selectmen Representative.

 5   Staff Present: Kristy Jobin, Community Development Director; Anthony Ciampoli, Police Chief;
 6   Donald Waldron, Fire Chief; Eric Slosek, Director of Public Works; Arene Berry, Director of
 7   Parks and Recreation; and Lincoln Daley, Town Administrator.

 8   The meeting was called to order at approximately 4:03 p.m. +/-.

 9   The Capital Improvement Planning Committee convened to hear scheduled presentations from
10   Town department heads regarding their Capital Improvement Program submissions.

11   Chief Ciampoli presented the Police Department’s CIP submission for the replacement of the
12   communications tower and related equipment.

13   Director Slosek presented six Department of Public Works CIP submissions, including capital
14   reserve funding for bridge replacement and Phase I of the proposed new DPW facility.

15   Director Berry presented three Parks and Recreation CIP submissions, including the proposed
16   Community Center, Amherst Middle School tennis court replacement, and Buck Meadow
17   development.

18   Chief Waldron presented sixteen Fire Department CIP submissions, including capital reserve
19   funding requests and the replacement of various small and large equipment.

20   Director Jobin presented three CIP submissions, including two for the Community Development
21   Department and one on behalf of Town Administration. The requests included impact fee
22   funding, scanning and digitization for Community Development records, and scanning and
23   digitization for other Town departments as needed.

24   Mr. Leedy made a motion to approve the September 2, 2026 meeting minutes. Mr. D’Angelo
25   seconded the motion. The motion passed, 4-0-1.

26   The Committee’s next meetings were scheduled as follows:
27   September 16, 2026, at 4:00 p.m. – SAU/School presentations
28   September 23, 2026, at 4:00 p.m. – Finalize CIP Draft
29   September 30, 2026, at 4:00 p.m. – public hearing for plan adoption.
30
31   At 5:37 p.m. +/- the meeting ended.
32
33   Respectfully submitted,
34   Kristy Jobin
