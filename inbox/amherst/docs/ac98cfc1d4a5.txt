44          5.1. Public Works Department – Promotion of Wayne Titus to Heavy Equipment
45                 Operator

47   DPW Director Slosek explained that Wayne Titus was hired by the Department of Public Works

57          5.2.    Public Works Department - Surplus Vehicle Designation
58
59   DPW Director Slosek stated that the Department of Public Works currently has one former
60   Police Department vehicle remaining in its inventory that is no longer needed for municipal
61   operations. Town Administration recommends that the Board of Selectmen designate the 2014
62   Ford Taurus, VIN 1FAHP2MK4EG175071, as surplus property and authorize its disposal. Upon
63   approval by the Board, the Department of Public Works will offer the vehicle for sale through a
64   public auction on Municibid, consistent with the Town’s established procedures for the disposal
65   of surplus property.
66
67   A MOTION was made by Selectman Grella and SECONDED by Selectmen D’Angelo to
68   designate the 2014 Ford Taurus, with VIN # 1FAHP2MK4EG175071, as surplus equipment, and
69   further authorize the Director of Public Works to sell said vehicle at public auction with
70   proceeds to be deposited into the general fund.
71   Vote: 4-0-0; motion carried unanimously.

158   5.3.   Public Works Department - Plowing Contract Approval
159
160   DPW Director Slosek stated that the Public Works Department currently has an employee out on
161   an extended absence who is not expected to return during the upcoming winter season. To
162   maintain adequate plow-route coverage and ensure continuity of winter operations, DPW
163   solicited a proposal from Knott’s Land Care to provide plowing and treatment services for Route
164   5. Knott’s Land Care has proposed a seasonal cost of $48,000 for the 2026–2027 winter season.
165   The proposal includes plowing and treatment of Route 5, with road salt supplied by the Public
166   Works Department. Although the proposal provides pricing for three winter seasons, staff is
167   seeking authorization only for the 2026–2027 season.
168
169   A MOTION was made by Selectman Grella and SECONDED by Selectmen D’Angelo to approve
170   the one-year agreement with Knott's Land Care, LLC in the amount of $48,000 for snow and ice
171   management services on DPW Route 5 for the 2026-2027 winter season, and to authorize the
172   Director of Public Works to execute the agreement on behalf of the town.
173   Vote: 4-0-0; motion carried unanimously.
174
175   5.4.   Public Works Department - Walkway Shoveling Contract
176
177   DPW Director Slosek stated that the Public Works Department is seeking approval to enter into a
178   new three-year agreement with the existing winter maintenance contractor, Knott’s Land Care,
179   LLC, for seasonal snow and ice management of building walkways. The proposed agreement

180   provides walkway clearing and treatment at both Fire Department locations, the Police Station,
181   Library, and Town Hall. The contract runs from November 15th through April 15th of each
182   winter season and includes seasonal walkway clearing, treatment, installation/removal of snow
183   stakes, and repair of plow- related damage. The Department will continue to supply the deicing
184   materials used for walkway treatment.

187   the three-year Building Walkway Snow and Ice Management Agreement with Knott's Land Care,
188   LLC in the amount of $14,000 for the year 2026-2027 with season, $14,420 for the 2027-2028
189   winter season, and $14,855 for the 2028-2029 winter season, and to authorize the Director of
190   Public Works to execute the agreement on behalf of the Town.
