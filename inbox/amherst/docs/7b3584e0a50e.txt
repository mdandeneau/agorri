Title: Minutes: Capital Improvements Plan Meeting Agenda
Fetched: 2026-11-01

TOWN OF AMHERST
     CIP Committee

     September 23, 2026                                                                          DRAFT

 1   In Attendance: Andrew Pataky, Ways and Means Committee Representative; Gordon Leedy,
 2   Planning Board Representative; Jason White, Amherst School Board Representative; and John
 3   D’Angelo, Alternate Board of Selectmen Representative.

 4   Staff Present: Kristy Jobin, Community Development Director

 5   The meeting was called to order at approximately 4:00 p.m. +/-.

 6   The Capital Improvement Planning Committee convened to review and prioritize Capital
 7   Improvement Program submissions from both Town departments and SAU 39.

 8   Mr. Pataky stated that Ms. Grund was unable to attend the meeting and read an email from her
 9   regarding her CIP priorities. Ms. Grund indicated that projects addressing safety concerns should
10   receive the highest priority. She also expressed support for long-term capital planning that
11   includes adequately funding capital reserve funds and drawing from those funds when planned
12   purchases occur. Ms. Grund stated that she trusts the Police Chief, Fire Chief, and DPW Director
13   to identify and prioritize the needs of their respective departments and supports projects that
14   improve public safety.

15   Selectman D’Angelo stated that he does not favor the current 1, 2, and 3 scoring system for CIP
16   requests. He indicated a preference for assigning projects a priority of either 1 or 2 and adjusting
17   the timing of projects, where appropriate, to minimize the tax impact.

18   Mr. Leedy stated that Town and School submissions funded through Capital Reserve Funds
19   should be considered a priority, as those projects have been planned and funded over a period of
20   years. He requested that the CIP spreadsheet be reorganized to clearly distinguish projects
21   funded through Capital Reserve Funds from those that would have a direct tax impact.

22   Mr. Pataky stated that he considers the Police Department communication center and Fire
23   Department radio replacements to be priorities, noting that reliable communications are essential.
24   He also expressed support for the Department of Public Works’ phased improvements needed to
25   provide adequate staff and operational space, the Parks and Recreation tennis court project, and
26   the Community Development scanning and digitization project. Regarding the School District
27   submissions, Mr. Pataky expressed support for maintenance and code compliance projects,
28   improvements to the football field lighting, and projects associated with Wilkins School.

29   The Committee’s next meetings were scheduled as follows:
30   September 30, 2026, at 4:00 p.m. – public hearing for plan adoption.
31
32   At 4:31 p.m. +/- the meeting ended.
33
34   Respectfully submitted,
35   Kristy Jobin
