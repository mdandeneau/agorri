181          7.1. Public Works Department - NEW Driver/Laborer Classification
182
183   DPW Director Slosek explained that the Department of Public Works is requesting Board
184   approval to establish a new Grade 5 “CDL Driver/Laborer” classification within the Highway
185   Division, effective November 1, 2026. The existing Grade 4 “Driver/Laborer” classification
186   would remain in place and would be utilized for positions that do not require a Commercial
187   Driver’s License (CDL). The new Grade 5 classification would be reserved for employees who
188   possess the required CDL and whose assigned duties include the operation of commercial motor
189   vehicles. The proposed classification change is intended to better recognize the additional
190   qualifications, responsibilities, and operational value associated with maintaining a CDL while
191   providing the Department with greater flexibility in recruitment and employee development. As
192   part of this proposal, DPW is requesting approval of a new Grade 5 CDL Driver/Laborer position
193   and associated job description. The job duties and responsibilities would remain substantially the
194   same as those contained in the existing Grade 4 Driver/Laborer job description. The distinction
195   between the two classifications would be the CDL qualification. Taken together, the three
196   personnel changes would result in an estimated net base-wage savings of $4,875.20 during
197   FY27. Accordingly, creation of the new classification can be accomplished while improving the
198   Department's CDL recruitment and retention structure without increasing the overall FY27
199   Highway Division wage budget based on these three positions. He reviewed the potential savings
200   that could be involved in this change. There is currently one employee that would move from
201   Grade 4 to Grade 5, if this moves forward.
202
203   A MOTION was made by Selectman D’Angelo and SECONDED by Selectmen Grella to approve
204   the creation of a new Grade 5 CDL Driver/Laborer classification and associated job description
205   within the Department of Public Works, effective November 1, 2026; to approve the amendment
206   of the existing Grade 4 Driver/Laborer job description to remove the CDL requirement; and to
207   authorize the proposed personnel placements within the new Grade 5 classification as presented.
208   Roll Call Vote: 4-0-0; motion carried unanimously.

210          7.2.    Public Works Department - North 03 (CMAQ) Update/Discussion
211
212   DPW Director Slosek explained that the CMAQ Project 44351 was originally awarded for
213   construction of a sidepath along the north side of Amherst Street between NH Route 101 and
214   Courthouse Road. At the time the grant application was prepared, the Town's separate Amherst
215   Street sidepath project was still under construction and was intended to extend from the Milford
216   Town line to Courthouse Road. That locally funded project subsequently experienced
217   construction cost overruns and was terminated at Boston Post Road, rather than continuing to
218   Courthouse Road as originally planned. As a result, proceeding with Project 44351 under its
219   existing limits would leave an approximately 700-foot gap between the existing sidepath and the
220   new CMAQ-funded improvement. Staff believes leaving this gap would be inconsistent with the
221   overall intent of the project and with the intent of the voter-approved funding, which was to
222   advance a connected pedestrian facility. Extending Project 44351 to Boston Post Road would
223   complete the missing connection and establish a continuous sidepath along Amherst Street from
224   NH Route 101 to the Milford Town line Town staff, NHDOT, and VHB met on October 1 to
225   discuss the project limits and proposed expansion. NHDOT indicated that additional CMAQ

      BOARD OF SELECTMEN MEETING MINUTES 2026.10.19                                                   5
226   funding is expected to be available for an eligible scope expansion. As CMAQ participation is
227   based on an 80% federal / 20% local cost share, staff believes expanding the project now
228   represents a favorable opportunity to complete the connection as originally intended. The Town
229   has appropriated $75,650 annually toward its local match, with FY26 and FY27 appropriations
230   providing a current local balance of $151,300. This funding, together with the corresponding
231   CMAQ participation, is expected to be sufficient to advance engineering in the near term.

280          7.4.    Finance Department - Fire Truck Bond Refinancing
281
282   Finance Director Fitzgerald stated that The Town currently maintains a $1.9M bond with a 10-
283   year repayment term and a fixed interest rate of 2.75%. As a condition of the financing, the
284   Town is required to maintain a $1M deposit with the lending institution, which earns interest at a
285   rate of 1.00%. Although the borrowing rate is favorable, the required deposit restricts the Town's
286   ability to maximize investment earnings on a significant portion of its available funds. The Town
287   has identified an opportunity to refinance the outstanding debt with the NHMBB as part of their
288   January issuance at an anticipated rate in the range of 3.75%- 4.00%, with no compensating
289   deposit requirement. The proposed refinancing would involve approximately $7,000 in closing
290   costs. Eliminating the deposit requirement would allow the Town to invest the $1M without
291   restriction. As investment earnings are subject to market conditions, the financial benefit of
292   refinancing depends on the Town's ability to earn a sufficient return on the released deposit.
293
294   A MOTION was made by Selectman Grella and SECONDED by Selectman D’Angelo to
295   authorize the application for refinancing of the Town’s existing $1.9 million bond through the
296   New Hampshire Municipal Bond Bank (NHMBB), utilizing a 10-year bond, for the purpose of
297   eliminating the existing $1 million compensating deposit requirement and improving the Town’s
298   overall financial position, subject to final financing terms and review by bond counsel.
299   Roll Call Vote: 4-0-0; motion carried unanimously.
