37
38   Mr. Ludt explained that the Amherst Conservation Commission recently heard an update about
39   this project. A large spring storm wiped out this bridge and there is an effort to rebuild it in its
40   existing position. However, approximately 100 yards away, there are historic abutments which
41   he suggested they try to utilize. The owners plan to move forward with their suggested location
42   as presented.
