TOWN OF AMHERST, NH
                                   Board of Selectmen
                              NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING

                       The Barbara Landry Meeting Room- 2 Main Street
                            Monday November 11, 2026, at 6:30 PM
Pursuant to the provisions of RSA 33:3-d, the Board of Selectmen for the Town of Amherst will
hold a public hearing on November 9, 2026, at 6:30 p.m. at the Barbara Landry Meeting Room,
Amherst Town Hall (2 Main Street, Amherst, NH 03031) regarding the possible issuance of
refunding bonds. The proceeds of these refunding bonds would be used to refinance the Town’s
general obligation note dated as of April 28, 2026 which matures on April 28, 2036, in the
aggregate principal amount of $1,900,000 in order to achieve interest cost savings and to pay the
costs of issuing the refunding bonds at their regular meeting.


                                All citizens are invited to attend.
