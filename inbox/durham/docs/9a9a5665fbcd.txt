*NO ZOOM ACCESS
In the past, the Town offered access to Conservation Commission meetings through Zoom.
For various reasons we have discontinued service for Zoom for most board meetings, including
the Conservation Commission, for the immediate future.

PUBLIC INPUT
Members of the public may provide input on any matter before the commission in one of two
ways:
1) Email. Send an email to Madeleine Harris, Director of Planning & Community
   Development, mharris@ci.durham.nh.us by 12:00 noon on the day of the meeting (You can
   also mail, drop off, or fax a letter); or

2) Speak at the meeting. The public is welcome to speak about any specific agenda item when
   that item comes up on the agenda and about any other appropriate subject during Public
   Comments time.
