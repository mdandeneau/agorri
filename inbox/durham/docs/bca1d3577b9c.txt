PLEASE NOTE
The public is welcome to attend this meeting and may speak at the discretion of the
subcommittee members.

This meeting will not be recorded on DCAT and participation on Zoom will not be available.

The purpose of the subcommittee is to develop recommended changes to the current
amendment for the proposed Wetland and Shoreland Overlay District for presentation to the
Conservation Commission. You can see documents related to the proposed ordinance here:
https://www.ci.durham.nh.us/boc_planning/wetland-and-shoreland-overlay-district

Basic minutes of this meeting will be taken in accordance with state law giving particulars of
the meeting and any motions taken but will not include detailed notes about the discussion.
Detailed notes will be included in all subsequent discussions by the Conservation Commission.

Please give the Town 48-hours notice if special communication aids are needed. Contact the
Planning Department at (603) 868-8064 with any questions.
