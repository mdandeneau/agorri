CONSERVATION COMMISSION
                                        October 26, 2026 – 7:00 pm
                                    Council Chambers – Durham Town Hall
                                       8 Newmarket Road, Durham, NH
                                                AGENDA

      I.   Call to Order
     II.   Land Acknowledgment Statement
    III.   Roll Call and Seating of Alternates
    IV.    Approval of Agenda
     V.    Public Comments
    VI.    Land Stewardship Update. Monthly update. Veronique, Ludington, Land
           Stewardship Coordinator. 7:10 to 7:20 pm (These times are approximate targets
           only)
            VII. 121 Technology Drive – Light Industry Buildings. Conditional use for activity within
           a wetland and the 75-foot wetland buffer in the Wetland Conservation Overlay District
           (WCOD), including a portion of a building, driveways, parking aisles, and other improvements.
           R.J. Kelly c/o Shawn Smith, owner. Bridget Souza and Matt Peterson, for Keach Nordstrom,
           engineer. Map 204, Lots 1 and 2. Office Research Light Industry District (ORLI). 7:20 to
           8:10 pm

            VIII. 28 Watson Road Wetland Buffer. Request from Town Planner for guidance in
           evaluating proposal from property owner to remove dead, diseased, unsafe, and invasive trees
           and vegetation, to maintain a slope as a native meadow, and to create an access path within the
           100-foot wetland buffer in the Wetland Conservation Overlay District. Kevin Wiley, property
           owner. Evan Crafts, Seacoast Land & Brush Services, contractor. Map 208, Lot 32. 8:10 to
           8:40 pm

           IX. WCOD and SPOD changes. Discussion about limited changes to the wetland and
           shoreland overlay districts currently proposed by the Town Planner as part of a broader zoning
           amendment. These changes are separate from the larger work involved in creating a Wetland
           and Shoreland Overlay District. 8:40 to 8:55 pm

           X. Calendar for 2027. Proposed Conservation Commission calendar for 2027. 8:55 to 9:00
           pm

           XI. Other Business 9:00 to 9:10 pm
           XII. Review of Minutes: July 27, August 24, and September 29, 2026 9:10 to 9:20
           pm
           XIII. Roundtable. Updates from Conservation Commission members. 9:20 to 9:30
           pm
XIV. Adjournment (by 9:30 unless extended by the commission)
*Note that the times listed after each item above are approximate targets only. (over)

       PUBLIC INPUT
       Members of the public may provide input on any matter before the commission
       in one of two ways:

    1. Email. Send an email to Michael Behrendt, Town Planner, at [email protected] by
       12:00 noon on the day of the meeting (You can also mail, drop off, or fax a letter);
       or

       2) Speak at the meeting. The public is welcome to speak about any specific
       agenda item when that item comes up on the agenda and about any other
       appropriate subject during Public Comments time.
