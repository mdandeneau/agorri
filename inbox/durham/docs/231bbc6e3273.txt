IWMAC Meeting Minutes – FINAL
                       Durham Town Hall, Upstairs Conference Room
                                 September 16, 2026


In attendance: Susan Richman, Nell Neal, Janet Perkins, Peter Yost, Naomi Kornhauser, and
Paul Rasmussen.

Meeting called to order at 8:02.

Agenda approved; Minutes approved with 2 corrections.

Updates: DPW and Town Council representatives absent.

Planning Board Update by Paul. PB is working on 121 Technology Drive and Smith Park Lane
subdivision. Nothing currently pertinent to IWMAC.

IWMAC Charge. Vote delayed until next Town Council meeting due to lack of quorum.

Dunkin'/Hannaford/Single use plastics.
  • Susan still has had no response from Dunkin’.

      legislative session. Susan will keep us informed.

     • Durham Day table
     • Investigating composting options

Illustrated "This Not That" Poster requested by resident Josh Meyerowitz, to clarify what can
and cannot be recycled. Could this fit into a single poster/graphic? How would it be distributed?
Place it in DYK? No conclusion reached.

How to educate and motivate residents about plastic reduction? We agreed that this should
be a priority and discussed this question for close to one hour. Points made and questions asked
included:
   •   Are reuse programs like Ridwell useful or “guilt erasers”?
   •   Susan said that only 5% to 10% of plastics are actually recycled.
   •   Janet suggested that a project reusing plastics could raise awareness about what is not
       being recycled.
   •   We could set up booth near Hannaford to get input from public and provide information.
   •   Can public be motivated to avoid plastic bags if they are not banned?
   •   If a bill to ban plastic bags statewide is filed, we should campaign for public support.

Composting
  • Paul reported that Londonderry is permitting a biogas facility, using methane from food
     waste. This kind of business provides infrastructure for statewide composting.
  • Janet will contact Casella about scheduling field trip.

Capstone Project. Naomi read report from Julie:

I met for the first time with the university students last Tuesday, September 8. We will have five
students working with us this semester. They will research, report and educate fellow students
and community members on plastics being used at local coffee shops, and what we can do to
help students and community members make better choices. The students plan to attend our
October 7 meeting to introduce themselves and talk about the project in-depth. In the meanwhile,
if you have questions or ideas for the project, please reach out to me anytime. We plan to meet at
5 PM on Thursdays moving forward. You’re always welcome to join us if you wish.

Naomi will ask Julie where they are meeting on Thursdays and will suggest they consider tabling
at public space near The Works and Aroma Joe's.

Sustainability Calendar. Naomi hasn't yet heard from Steve Lutterman about training to create
