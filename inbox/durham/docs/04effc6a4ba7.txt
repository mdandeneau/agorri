*LAND, WATER AND LIFE ACKNOWLEDGMENT STATEMENT
The Town of Durham, New Hampshire wishes to acknowledge the spiritual and physical
connection maintained by the Pennacook, Abenaki and Wabanaki peoples to N’dakinna
(homeland), aki (land), nibi (water), lolakwikak (flora) and awaasak (fauna). New
Hampshire has been home to these Indigenous people for thousands of years. The
residents of Durham acknowledge the ongoing hardships endured by our Indigenous
neighbors after the loss of their unceded homelands which we now steward, and we
will work to foster relationships to support the well-being of Indigenous people who
carry forward the traditions of their ancestors.
