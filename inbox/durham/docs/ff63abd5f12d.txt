*NO ZOOM ACCESS
In the past, the Town offered access to Historic District Commission meetings through
Zoom. For various reasons we have discontinued service for Zoom for most board
meetings, including the Historic District Commission, for the immediate future. A
recording of the meeting will be available on DCAT within a few days after the meeting.

*OTHER INFORMATION
Public Comments. The public is welcome to speak about any specific agenda item when that item
comes up on the agenda and about any other appropriate subject during Public Comments time.
HDC/HC. HDC stands for Historic District Commission. HC stands for Heritage Commission. The
commission is one body with two roles. The HDC reviews applications in the Historic District. The
Heritage Commission’s broader role is to promote historic preservation throughout Durham.
Additional information. Files on the items above may be reviewed in the Planning Office from 8:00
a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday through Friday and on the website at
https://www.durhamnh.gov/1617/Historic-District-Commission-and-Heritag.
Contacting us. Contact the Planning Department with any questions or comments about the items

*LAND, WATER AND LIFE ACKNOWLEDGMENT STATEMENT
The Town of Durham, New Hampshire wishes to acknowledge the spiritual and physical
connection maintained by the Pennacook, Abenaki and Wabanaki peoples to N’dakinna
(homeland), aki (land), nibi (water), lolakwikak (flora) and awaasak (fauna). New Hampshire has
been home to these Indigenous people for thousands of years. The residents of Durham
acknowledge the ongoing hardships endured by our Indigenous neighbors after the loss of their
unceded homelands which we now steward, and we will work to foster relationships to support
the well-being of Indigenous people who carry forward the traditions of their ancestors.
