Chair Brickner-Wood noted that no members of the public were present, and public comments
were closed.

Town Planner Michael Behrendt presented a mock-up of a new sign for the Village Cemetery
designed by Charlotte Hitchcock, incorporating language from Janet Mackey and Carolyn
Singer. Mr. Behrendt explained the sign will be one-sided with a black powder-coated aluminum
square post and a 24 by 30-inch bronze plaque. The cost from Franklin Bronze is $2,515 plus
shipping, with a lead time of four to six weeks. Thomas Janosz asked about trees recently taken
down at the cemetery, and Chair Brickner-Wood noted the tree removal was on the town's work
plan. Mr. Janosz also suggested ensuring official town documents refer to the site consistently as
the "Village Cemetery" rather than the "Schoolhouse Cemetery" to avoid confusion.

Carolyn Singer: Motion to approve the sign for the village cemetery as presented for not to
exceed cost of $3,000.; Second: Elizabeth LaRocca; Vote: 4-0-0 — PASSED
Mr. Behrendt provided an update on standardized historic signs for placement on private
buildings. He reported that he asked Sundance Signs for more models and a template to resolve
questions about capitalization and spacing. He stated he had not yet received a response and
would follow up.

Chair Brickner-Wood reported on the historic marker for the Grange building, currently occupied
by the Freedom Cafe. He stated he had spoken with Brian Bessette, director of the Freedom
Cafe, who agreed the nonprofit could contribute up to $500 toward the sign. With the property
owner already committing $1,500, Chair Brickner-Wood stated he hoped to keep the town's
contribution to $500. The next step is to finalize the wording for the sign in collaboration with
the Historic Association.

Chair Brickner-Wood explained a new minute-taking process outlined by town staff. The town
will use an AI program to produce draft minutes, which will then be sent to a designated
Commission member for review and editing within five business days. Chair Brickner-Wood
expressed a preference for shorter, corporate-style minutes that focus on actions and salient
points rather than a full transcript. Ms. LaRocca volunteered to be the designated reviewer for
the Commission.

The Commission discussed the downtown adjacent zoning update proposal to rename the
Churchill District. Ms. Singer raised concerns, arguing that removing the name intentionally
disconnects the historical quality of the area. Chair Brickner-Wood agreed, noting that
geographic and historical names humanize zoning districts and help residents identify
neighborhoods better than alphanumeric codes. The Commission reached a consensus to send a
comment to the Planning Board advocating for the retention of the Churchill District name.
Mr. Behrendt stated he had nothing to add for the staff report.

Under reports from members serving on other boards, Chair Brickner-Wood noted that Carden
Welsh sent a report from the Town Council indicating that a proposal to allow parking lots by
right is currently deferred. Ms. Singer reported on the Land Stewardship Committee, noting that
several dead trees were cut down at Wagon Hill Farm. She also discussed the Town Council's

decision to implement paid parking for non-residents at Wagon Hill. Ms. Singer supported the

Under other business, Mr. Behrendt announced that the Town Council confirmed Maddie Harris
as the new planner. She will overlap with Mr. Behrendt for about two months before his
retirement. She will attend the November HDC meeting.

Chair Brickner-Wood mentioned he wants to write a piece about the historic preservation fund to
raise awareness and spur donations. He also would like to write something for the Friday
Updates to make people aware of the vacancies on the HDC.

Elizabeth LaRocca: Motion to adjourn.; Second: Carolyn Singer; Vote: 4-0-0 — PASSED
