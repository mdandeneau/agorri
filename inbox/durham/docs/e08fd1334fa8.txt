IWMAC Meeting Minutes – DRAFT
                        Durham Town Hall, Town Council Chambers
                                   October 21, 2026


In attendance: Julie Kelley, Nell Neal, Susan Richman, Mike Pazdon, Paul Rasmussen, Allison
Small, Peter Yost, and Naomi Kornhauser.

Meeting called to order at 8:07.

Agenda and minutes approved.

No DPW Update – Sam absent

Town Council Update. Curtis absent, but Paul reported that TC approved two zoning changes
re: downtown building heights and requiring owner occupancy for residences with ADUs.

Planning Board Update by Paul. Working on other zoning ordinance changes. Approved
upgrades and picnic bench on Sweet Trail. Suggested inviting new planner Maddie Harris to
future meeting and to provide her with questions in advance.

Plastic Detox. Debrief on October 29 screening at DPL. Positive reception and good turnout
(25-30), but audience was almost entirely older residents. Julie will ask Erin if it could be shown
to UNH students as part of a class.

DYKs. Resident asked if past DYKs are available in searchable database. Mike started working
on this but more work required. Naomi volunteered to help later this year.

Website Updates. Susan will forward to Mike suggestions from John Baker.

Sustainability Calendar. Naomi will contact Steve Lutterman later this year for training.

Big Belly Satellite Composting Unit - report on meeting with company reps, attended by Susan,
Naomi, and Julie.
   • Cost of one unit would be approximately $7000 plus collection fee to Mr. Fox.
   • Would require grant – Mike will look into grant possibillities.
   • Possible locations: Mill Plaza, Waysmeet, a town-owned parking lot?
   • Susan will ask Big Belly if they would consider a rental unit for pilot, and she will
       request contacts at smaller towns using their units.
   • Susan will ask Mr. Fox if they would collect from a Big Belly satellite unit.

Black Earth Composting Facility.
   • We agreed that it is unlikely to find Durham-owned land for site.
   • Lee and Portsmouth might have suitable land.
   • Paul suggested that private landowners in Durham might be interested, but that zoning
      ordinance would have to be revised to add composting to table of uses.
Hannaford Letter. Susan gave letter to Hannaford manager, but response is delayed because of
Hannaford company hack.

Compostable Foodware. Is it preferable to plastics? Should we encourage or discourage use?
BYO is clearly better than compostable cups. To be discussed further at Priorities Meeting.

Composting – How to encourage state-mandated action? Peter will try to find out how Vermont
composting law was successful.

Educational Programs. Resident suggested screening Tapped, a film about plastics in water.
To be discussed at Priorities meeting.

Meeting adjourned at 9:50, purposing to next meet 11/4/2026.
