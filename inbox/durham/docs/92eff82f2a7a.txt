IWMAC Meeting Minutes – DRAFT
                        Durham Town Hall, Town Council Chambers
                                  November 4, 2026


In attendance: Julie Kelley, Nell Neal, Susan Richman, Mike Pazdon, Janet Perkins, Paul
Rasmussen, Allison Small, Peter Yost, Sam Hewitt, and Naomi Kornhauser. Also attending,
UNH Capstone student Ben (last name?)

Meeting called to order at 8:05.

Agenda and minutes approved.

DPW Update by Sam.
  • Leaf and brush collection will begin next week.
  • Haven’t been able to find a source to recycle old trash containers. Due to bad plastic
     recycling market, Toter is not honoring its commitment to recycle. Peter suggested
     continuing to offer containers for reuse.
  • Will need at least a year for enough data to evaluate how the new system has affected
     amounts of trash and recycling. Will supply available data at end of December.
  • Tour of Casella scheduled for December 9 at 10:30 am. Will confirm availability of P&R
     van.
  • Sam has given notice to leave job once a replacement is found. We will miss him!!

Town Council Update. Curtis absent.

Planning Board Update by Paul. Nothing relevant to report.

Hannaford Letter and Plastics Legislation Update by Susan.
  • Rachel Kuhn at Hannaford says not to expect a corporate response.
  • New plastic bag bill will be presented this year, with Democratic majorities in NH House
     and Senate.

New Planner Maddy Harris will be coming to November 18 meeting.

Thank you Nell! Todd Selig presented Nell Neal with plaque honoring her for her many years
of work as IWMAC member and chair.

UNH Capstone Project Update by Ben
  • They created list of coffee shops that will allow BYO cups – all but Dunkin’.
  • Will advertise list on Instagram sites of UNH Sustainability Institute and Paul College.
     Suggestion made to put on town website as well.
  • Will create interactive website showing coffeeshops.
  • Will order stickers for shops to hand out to provide publicity for their business and
     policy. Stickers will be customized for each shop.
   •   Peter made motion to provide a maximum of $1000 from IWMAC budget to pay for
       stickers. Motion passed unanimously.

IWMAC Priorities for year ahead. Susan provided worksheets with categories based on input
from committee members: Recycling, Compost, Plastic reduction, Repair, PFAS, and
Sustainable Practices.
   • We agreed that there is much overlap between categories, and that Sustainable Practices
       might better be called Reduce and Reuse.
   • Julie suggested we look at overlap of “Circle of Concern” with “Circle of Influence.”
   • Through a ranked-choice activity, we picked top three priorities as: 1.Compost; 2.Plastic
       Reduction; and 3.Reduce/Reuse.
   • PFAS subcommittee will continue its work.
   • At next meeting we will work on creating list of tasks for each priority.

We adjourned at 9:50, planning to next meet on November 18, 2026
