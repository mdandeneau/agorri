IWMAC Meeting Minutes – FINAL
                               Council Chambers, Town Hall
                                    September 2, 2026


In attendance: Susan Richman, Julie Kelley, Mike Pazdon, Sam Hewitt, Janet Perkins, Paul
Rasmussen, Peter Yost, Allison Small, and Naomi Kornhauser.

Meeting called to order at 8:05.

Minutes approved with two revisions.

Agenda approved.

DPW Update by Sam.
  • DPW has identified a company interested in taking high density plastic carts for
     recycling. Old carts will be trucked to them in October; we can give Oct. 1 as deadline
     for accepting them from public.
  • Returning students still adjusting to new system.
  • Waste Management found problem with too much yard waste in trash. Bagged invasives
     can be put in trash, but not other yard waste.
  • Town received first rebate from state of solid waste disposal costs.

No Town Council Update – Curtis absent.

Planning Board Update by Paul. Working on plans for 121 Technology Drive.

IWMAC Charge passed unanimously, with Allison voting in Nell's absence. To be voted on by
Town Council at September 14 meeting.

Sustainability Newsletter. Julie reported that we now have 205 subscribers. Two articles have
been submitted thus far for November edition.

Capstone Project. Julie met with Erin Hale. Students are considering planning Repair Fair for
March. Fall project will focus on town coffeehouses and their handling of waste, plastic use, etc.

IWMAC Budget. Susan learned that we cannot encumber last year's budget.

Durham Day. Susan and Naomi gave out lots of free Hannaford bags and reminder hangers.
Three new signups for newsletter and three expressing interest in composting.

Trash 2 Treasure. Janet and Susan had good conversations with students and gave away lots of
bags.

        UNH newsletter, and with help of Sienna Schaier, Erin Hale, Emily Abruski, and UNH

Sustainability Calendar. Paul suggested creating calendar as clearinghouse for all
sustainability-related events, to be available on IWMAC web page and through links on town
site. He will discuss with Steve adding this to town calendar. Naomi volunteered to manage
calendar if she receives training.

IWMAC Priorities will include composting, PFAS, plastics, education/outreach, and UNH
Collaboration. We will discuss specifics soon. To be presented to Town Council as part of
yearly review.

Composting is a Big Priority! We discussed various questions and possible paths forward.
  • How likely is Casella to handle organics?
  • Would Mr. Fox handle a satellite facility? Julie will contact them.
  • What PFAS issues would be related to working with Casella Organics?
  • How to raise public awareness? One avenue is to have educational table at Swap Shop.
  • Does the state law about having to divert waste to compost have any bearing on us, and is
     it even enforceable?

Ridwell. Peter brought up this company, which accepts hard-to-recycle materials for a fee.
Janet has recently subscribed. Worth looking into further?

Meeting adjourned at 9:55, with plan to meet next on September 16.
