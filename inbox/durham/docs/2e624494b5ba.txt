  I.   Calling meeting to order, acknowledge absent members, identify minutes-taker,
       introductions of members and guests

       The meeting was called to order at approximately 7:02pm. Dr. Elizabeth Burakowski was in
       attendance. Jim Lawson would be taking minutes.

       Dr. Burakowski said that Matt Davis would not be in attendance, and that she anticipated
       being assigned to the committee as UNH’s representative.

 II.   Approval of Minutes

       Motion by Jim Lawson to approve the September meeting minutes. Second by Steve
       Holgren. Passed unanimously 5-0.

III.   Activities toward 2026 Goals

       a.    Durham Newsletter

       Chris Smith was asked to make an Energy Committee contribution to the Durham
       Sustainability Newsletter. He submitted information about Durham’s EV Charging Stations.
       There was further discussion about the Sustainability Newsletter and the material to be
       provided by the Energy Committee in the future.

       b.    Data Centers
      Andrew Coppens believes it is unlikely Dr. Stephen Bird could attend an Energy Committee
      meeting to discuss Data Centers at this time due to his busy schedule and responsibilities at
      the Carsey School of Public Policy.

      Jim Lawson said that the data center topic came up at the Town Council when discussing the
      proposed Planned Unit Development (PUD) zoning changes. UNH’s representatives had
      stated that Hyperscale Data Centers were not envisioned in their plans for the West Edge.
      Jim Lawson said it was unlikely that data centers would be addressed at this time in the
      PUD zoning.

      There was discussion about how a large or hyperscale data center would be defined. Jim
      Lawson suggested defining it by its power requirements – possibly 20MW or more. He said
      he would provide a draft definition to the members to discuss. Steve Holgren said that the
      white paper raised concerns about water consumption, and that the amount of consumption
      depended on the data center design. He also suggested reaching out to Eversource to see
      what level of power is of concern to them that can’t be absorbed.

      Harrison Potter said that acoustic limits should also be a consideration, and highlighted
      zoning examples that had auditory limits. There was continued discussion on the topic and
      the need for the Energy Committee to become more knowledgeable about Data Centers and
      a resource to town government by early 2027.


      c.       Durham Energy Web-Page

      Steve Holmgren reviewed the document he and Malcolm Brown had provided to the
      committee.

           •   He said Malcolm’s review of other websites found that they were generally
               comparable with what has been done in Durham.
           •   He noted that there are some dead links and outdated material on our website.
           •   He recommended that a committee member or small group take responsibility for the
               website.
           •   The consensus of the committee was that it was not necessary to have a vote to
               approve what material is provided on the site.
           •   He said we should provide links to other websites with an introduction. He said we
               shouldn’t try to recreate the material.
           •   He said we need to be smart about what we put on the website and avoid having too
               much material that we can’t maintain.

      There was continued discussion about the content that could be included on the website.

IV.   Motion to Adjourn

      Motion made by Chris Smith at 8:14 PM. Second by Jim Lawson. Passed unanimously 5-0.
