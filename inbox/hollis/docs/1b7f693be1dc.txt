20
21   REPORTS
22   Schools - T. Gehan asked R. Zack to update the committee on School Board business. R. Zack stated
23   since the last Budget Committee meeting the Hollis School Board has had two meetings. He began with
24   an update from the Hollis Renovation & Expansion Committee (HREC), formally the Enrollment
25   Committee. Enrollment for 1st and 3rd grades at HPS are under the projected NESDEC numbers.
26   Kindergarten is a little higher at 89, NESDEC is at 90. However, class sizes are all within the desired
27   range. Current enrollment for HPS is 370. R. Zack stated NESDEC is projecting kindergarten to
28   significantly high for next year’s incoming class. HUES enrollment has had little change and falls in line
29   with the NESDEC reporting.
30
31   R. Zack noted that HREC has a community survey in progress. This survey has been available to town
32   residents since August and has an end date of September 21st. Over 300 responses have been currently
33   received. The survey can be located on the HREC page of the SAU41 website, social media, the library
34   and Town Hall. The goal of the survey is to prioritize the needs in order to narrow the scope of the
35   existing renovation/ addition plans to HPS to decrease the cost.
36
37   It was noted that HPS has gone from two pickup lanes back to one lane with an emergency lane.
38   The paving on Drury Lane is still happening although timing is to be determined.
39   The new data dashboard on SAU41 website is now live. Residents can find state testing, enrollment,
40   athletic and graduation data on this new platform.
41   SAU41 has approved a Retained Fund Balance of $324,456 for FY27. They are expecting approximately
42   $300,000 to be returned to the tax payers.
43   T. Gehan asked if the school board would be coming to the March meeting with a revised plan for HPS.
44   R. Zack said that this is still to be determined.

64   NEW BUSINESS
65   R. Zack spoke to discussions that were had regarding HB1300 at the recent School Board meeting. He
66   said that the schools are waiting on guidance from the DRA especially because of the pending litigation
67   regarding this bill due to confusing and misleading language. He stated that this bill will cause further
68   confusion when it is on the ballot as it is written as one question but actually covers 2 separate limits, the
69   6% limit to the SAU operating budget and the inflation adjusted tax cap.
70
71   D. Mann stated that the DRA will be the authority in figuring out how to implement the wording of HB
72   1300. The calculation will be using data from March of 2026. The definition provided states the
73   percentage of property tax growth is added to the inflation percentage. The net new taxable property
74   growth is comprised of change in prior year new construction, physical expansion or improvement of
75   structures, subdivisions, or redevelopment of land, and conversions from exempt to taxable status. Any
76   physical change that increases taxable market value. It is not to address market appreciation, reevaluations
77   and changes in assessment methodology. C. Hyde asked to clarify if the percentage of property growth is
78   additive to the CPI. D. Mann confirmed that this is how the bill currently reads. She stated that Input from
79   DRA is forthcoming and will prove to be helpful when factoring this calculation.
