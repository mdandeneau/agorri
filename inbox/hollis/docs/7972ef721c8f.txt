POTENTIAL NEW PROJECTS:
Cassandra mentioned that several people asked her what the next project is going to be for the Heritage
Commission. After a discussion, the members agreed that the current projects of the White Barn interior,
Cooper Shop second floor and repairs to the Icehouse take precedence. This topic will be revisited after
completion of those projects.

WHITE BARN CLEAN OUT:

Jan had distributed photos of the White Barn interior to members. After a discussion, it was decided that
members should meet at the barn on Monday, October 12 at 9 AM with supplies to clean out the front
right side section of the barn. Members will also assess the scope of the clean out project to determine
which items in the barn could be salvaged and which need to be discarded.

GAMBREL BARN: Topic has been moved to the October meeting agenda.

ICEHOUSE:

Erol stated that he has been able to complete repairs to two sides of the icehouse. His goal is to raise the
boards off the ground so that they do not deteriorate as quickly.

COOPER SHOP: Finish flooring in the loft

Erol and Tim stated that there should be enough wood stored in the White Barn to complete the second
floor of the cooper shop. Jan mentioned that she had spoken to David Tremblay, the carpenter who
completed a significant amount of work on the gambrel barn and cooper shop, if he would be interested in
completing the second floor. He is interested. Jan will ask him for a quote to submit to commission
members.

CALENDAR UPDATE:

Distribution of calendars for sale: Cassandra will supply Lull Farm, Karla will supply Brookdale and
Jan will supply Hollis Art Space with calendars for sale.

Distribution of calendars to sponsors and photographers: Karla will distribute many of the calendars
to sponsors. Others will pitch in as needed. Jan will contact photographers to determine how they would
like to receive their calendars.

Calendar sales at Old Home Days: Cassandra stated that 3 or 4 were purchased using PayPal. About 25
others were purchased.

OLD HOME DAYS AND 2027 PLANS:

Cassandra stated that there were many visitors to the Cooper Shop with Ron Raisilis, master cooper,
demonstrating his craft. She also suggested that a representative from the Heritage Commission should
attend most if not all of the Old Home Days planning meetings prior to the 2027 event. Jan mentioned
that we might have had additional calendar sales if the canopy had been set up on the walk way, instead
of back several feet. Cassandra will contact this year’s chair of the Old Home Days Committee to request
notification of next year’s planning meeting dates.


HISTORIC DISTRIC COMMISSION REPORT:

Erol stated that an application was submitted for demolition of a two family home on Proctor Hill Road
and replacement with a different style of two family home. The pictures and plans submitted did not meet
the current HDC guidelines. After a discussion, the members did not vote on the application because the
design submitted would not be the final plan. If the applicant wants to proceed, he will have to submit a
more detailed plan that is within the HDC guidelines.
ADJOURNMENT: Motion to adjourn at 7:50 pm was made by Tim and seconded by Sharon. Motion
passed unanimously.

Next Meeting to be held at 6:30pm, Tuesday, October 27, 2026, at the Town Hall.

Jan Larmouth, Recording Secretary.
